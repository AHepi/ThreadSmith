## What each patch gave up (kept from log 21 on)
- Patch 1 (rig 1): exceptions to a "usually" line are ignored unless that line explains something observed. Gives up: noticing a bare unexplained exception.
- Patch 3 (rig 1): BECAUSE is tested only on what MAKES. Gave up: "because" as a reason to expect. Given back by patch 6 as SINCE.
- Patch 4 (rig 1): departures from usual-case lines are questions, not faults. Gives up: nothing found so far.
- Patch 6 (rig 1): SINCE accepts any route. Gives up: it cannot tell a good reason to expect from a backwards one.
- Patch 7 (rig 1): the final conclusion is whichever is argued for last. Gives up: a paragraph that states its conclusion first and its reasons after.
- Patch 8 (rig 1): opposed "usually" lines are kept side by side. Gives up: nothing is concluded about that fact at all, even where a reader would see an obvious winner.
- Patch 9 (rig 1): exempt switches a USUALLY line off for a thing. Gives up: nothing is then said about what did happen to it.
- Patch 10 (rig 1): tests added lines by taking out everything the writer wrote. Gives up: it cannot see an added line that does the writer's work only together with one of the writer's lines.
- Patch 11 (rig 1): MAKE NOT SO removes the lines that simply state the fact and adds its denial. Gives up: a fact that other lines produce stays produced, and the what-if world contradicts itself.
- Patch 12 (rig 1): named cases one level deep. Gives up: a case inside a case.
- Patch 13 (rig 1): sets aside results tied to what was changed. Gives up: a result with two stated causes is set aside when only one is withdrawn.
- Patch 14 (rig 1): a denied BECAUSE. Gives up: nothing is said about a denied SINCE.
- Patch 15 (rig 1): a told world is looked at alone. Gives up: a story that leans on everyday background finds none of it inside.
- Near-case check (audit workflow): bounded to cases that already exist. Gives up: a row that no outside case has strained keeps its far-apart pair unchallenged.
- Patch 16 (rig 1): "cannot tell" when the ledger says nothing about a plan's route. Gives up: a plan that truly cannot work, in a ledger that says nothing, now passes as "cannot tell".
- The bridge between the rigs: movements only. Gives up: a BECAUSE whose effect is a change of state stays untested by the laws.
- Rig 2 patch 3: "cannot tell" for a thing with no stated response. Gives up: a real change from nothing involving such a thing now passes quietly as "cannot tell".
- Rig 2 patches 1 and 2: "cannot tell" and "not checked" replace false alarms. Gives up: a real change from nothing involving wind or an unknown verb will now pass quietly as "cannot tell".

## Traps
- Reading a clean checker report as "the reasoning is right". It only means the lines fit together.
- Taking the rig bundle as matching an older results file. It holds the checkers as they are now.
- Running a driver on another machine without s(CASP) at the path it expects: the raw log stays empty and the exit code is 1 (Lesson 53).
- Writing a forcing case's prediction from a description of the case instead of its text (Lesson 51).
