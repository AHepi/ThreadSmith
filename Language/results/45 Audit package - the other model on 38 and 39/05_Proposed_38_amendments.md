# Proposed amendments to the supplied file 38

## Status and scope

Read these clauses together with the frozen `sources/38_original.md`. They are candidates arising from the recorded audit, not a previously accepted version and not a tested implementation. Each named replacement below controls only its stated passage. The fourteen subject-matter kinds, the five line fields, the separation of translator/checker/read-back, and the exclusions otherwise remain. R12's intransitive extension is not adopted here.

R06 remains a proposal requiring a declared choice of the force-dynamic reading. R11 states a preservation obligation without claiming a completed machine alias syntax. Do not advertise this package as a complete implementation contract or as a replacement for the underlying theory 02.

## Interpretation and preservation: R01 and R03

A translation preserves the admitted commitments of the source in their scope. The bin retains the excluded expression with enough surrounding structure to recover what was asserted, denied, quoted, asked, desired or supposed. It preserves consequential line breaks and punctuation rather than silently replacing them with different constructions.

An interpretation not settled by the source is marked filled in. Where material alternatives remain, the record identifies them and the reason for any selection. Alternatives that constrain each other stay together. A finding on one chosen reading remains conditional on that reading. Agreement among an incomplete set of readings does not by itself establish a source-settled conclusion. An unresolved local reading does not erase another independently explicit commitment.

Readings needed to reconstruct the source's commitments must be available from the ledger and bin, even when a separate chosen-readings section presents them more conveniently. Preserving the original text is not, by itself, performing the translation of its admitted content.

## TOLD and excluded attributions: R02

Replace the blanket mental-content transfer instruction with the following meaning.

TOLD records content that the source presents as asserted within an identified report, story, note, letter, speech or dream. The mere presence of a proposition inside a denial, desire, doubt, command, question, or conditional does not assert that proposition in the actual ledger or in a personal TOLD world.

Preserve the enclosing operator and its scope. If the required attribution or nesting is outside the language, retain the whole relevant construction in the bin rather than detach an embedded proposition as a held line. An affirmed reported conditional can be retained as a conditional without asserting its antecedent or its consequent separately.

This clause does not create a logic of belief, desire, knowledge or nested supposition. Told worlds still have the isolation rule of the original definition. The declared one-level restriction on named cases remains.

## Exceptions: R04

Replace the restriction to USUALLY-only attachment in translator rule 12 and in the companion prompt.

An Exception points to a named USUALLY or ALWAYS line and preserves the source's distinction between non-application and a countercase. A claimed exception does not silently change that line's strength or scope. Against an ALWAYS line, a claimed exemption is reported as the stated exemption or scope dispute; a countercase supplies the condition and contrary outcome that conflict with the unqualified rule. An expressly out-of-scope instance is not a countercase merely because it is called an exception.

## Results and causal relations: R05a

Replace the mandatory “as a result of” wording in the Result shape.

A Result records MOVES, STAYS, TOUCHES or BECOMES with the described participants and any stated stage. It need not identify a producer. Association with the stage of a happening does not itself assert production. When the source attributes production, record the causal relation separately as BECAUSE, so that the result and the attribution can be retained or denied independently.

No missing cause is filled in solely to complete the Result shape. BECOMES remains not yet checked where the original definition says so. This amendment does not invent an unknown verb's shape or make an outcome its own warrant.

## Mentioned endpoints: R05b

A numbered line may wrap an otherwise admitted content as `MENTION [content]`. This wrapper identifies the content without asserting that it holds. The line's standing and source mark describe how that mention is introduced, not the truth of the enclosed proposition. It is not a fifteenth subject-matter kind or a new truth status.

A goal or queried outcome can point to this numbered mention. A checker must not use a mentioned target as an additional premise. An actual asserted occurrence with that content is a different role and remains separately identifiable. Do not convert assertions into mentions simply to avoid a contradiction.

## Only-ways: R05c

The negative-all-listed-ways rendering of an Only-ways necessity condition is an ALWAYS SHOWS relation within the source's stated scope. It does not establish any particular alternative from the target alone, and an alternative alone does not establish the target. It does not imply exclusivity. A causal absence assertion requires a separate source assertion or marked addition; it is not obtained simply by labelling the necessity rule MAKES.

## Causal words: R06, scope choice stated

The prior-tendency rule for MAKES is a test of the explicitly stipulated force-dynamic construction, not a definition of every ordinary English attribution of production. A source can attribute production with a prior tendency present; preserve that offered relation as such without inferring an unstated opposing tendency. If the chosen causal-word construction is not warranted, its assessment remains conditional or cannot tell rather than becoming a contradiction in the writer's ordinary production claim.

LETS and HELPS do not alone assert achievement of their target. STOPS does not assert every contrary result. Where the source separately states achievement, prevention, continuation or a tendency, retain that content separately. This clause does not settle all senses of those verbs or add a general multi-force model.

## Contradiction, route and final target: R07

Replace the unqualified local reading of “NOT [E BECAUSE C] is fine unless ...”.

An explicitly asserted relation and its negation, about the same referents in the same context and respect, are conflicting commitments independently of whether a productive route has been established. A causal denial may also conflict with a supplied producing route. Absence of such a route is not by itself a contradiction and does not erase a directly opposed assertion. A finding depending on an added line is identified as conditional on that addition.

Assess the offered reason separately from support available to its conclusion by another route. Independent support for E does not validate every offered C-to-E relation; a failed or circular route does not erase an independently supported route.

The final conclusion belongs to an identified argument and is the source-designated or explicitly selected target, not automatically the last sentence. A consequential target choice not settled by the source is marked. Separate arguments can have separate targets.

## SO THAT: R08

A finding that an action changes a stated dependency is a relevance finding. A supported route to the goal additionally needs an applicable source-supplied connection in the appropriate direction. Making a necessary condition true does not by itself establish the goal. Where only relevance or necessity is supplied, say cannot tell whether the plan can work. Where the source supplies a sufficient route, the positive finding remains conditional on those premises and is not proof of actual achievement or real-world truth.

## Additions and report scope: R09

The added-lines-alone diagnostic reports what it detects when it fires. Its silence does not establish that all additions satisfy translator rule 9. In particular, an unsupported added bridge can depend on a said premise and therefore escape that diagnostic. Every consequential addition remains identifiable, and findings dependent on it remain conditional.

A clean limited report means that no clash was found by the applicable stated checks on the represented content. It does not certify translation fidelity, every unanalysed description, every bin entry or implementation conformance. Name the assessment question and the capability limits rather than presenting a source-attribution answer as a production result.

## Actual records and changed cases: R10

An observation or report of an actual outcome remains evidence about the actual case. Considering a changed case does not automatically make that outcome a held premise of the changed case. Evidential independence from another witness is distinct from a warrant that the outcome persists under the stated change.

Preserve the unchanged background that the case actually supplies. Do not use the disputed target, or a restatement of it, as its own carryover warrant. Where the described account does not determine what carries over, the changed outcome remains unsettled. The original distinctions among WITHDRAW, MAKE NOT SO and slot changes, the stated decline rule, the one-level case boundary and the excluded multiple-cause problem remain.

## Repetition and identity: R11

A source occurrence is not automatically a new happening or an independent observation. Preserve explicitly stated repetition of one event and explicitly stated distinct events. Preserve repeated reports and their given source lineage. Where a representation or comparison relies on a consequential identity decision, make that decision visible and mark any addition.

This clause does not supply an automated coreference procedure or a completed event-alias syntax. If the available representation cannot carry a required identity relation into a particular check, retain it for the reader and do not claim that check has assessed it.
