# L66 audit protocol

Scope: Do the supplied printed reports alone communicate findings, source sentences, unstated consequences and remaining uncertainties? Do not judge the truth of the passages or claim to have executed the checkers. Source passages, full ledgers, rules and executable checkers are absent by design.

P01–P04 were read before fixing the following expectations. P05–P18 will test them without changing the criteria. These are not independent validation cases for L66: the bundle's development and selection history is unknown.

1. A finding can be paraphrased without inventing a missing predicate definition or source assertion. Evidence against a readability defect: a complete, concrete finding with its scope in the report itself.
2. A reported ledger line is traceable to a sentence through an explicit map or an unambiguous report statement. Evidence against a mapping defect: actual source-sentence identification, not guesswork from order or topic.
3. A clean verdict distinguishes checks that ran from checks that had no eligible input. Evidence against a vacuity concern: named exercised checks and their targets, even where there are no failures.
4. Each unstated consequence identifies its content, premise lines and source sentences, and distinguishes assumptions/interpretive additions from the writer's explicit claims. Evidence against a dependency defect: enough printed support to recover those distinctions.
5. Deletion results are read as reported changes in the stated output set, not automatically as proof of premise irrelevance or a causal intervention. A group-deletion or alternate-support witness is needed for a stronger dependency conclusion.
6. Missing support is not treated as refutation; an untested portion is not treated as a pass; an encoded causal convention is not treated as an independently established fact about causation.

Planned conceptual mutations (not checker executions): remove headings while retaining concrete content; rename internal line identifiers consistently; remove concrete bridges while retaining verdicts; compare rule-only input with grounded input; remove alternative premises individually and together; change report wording while leaving its represented case unchanged. Record what should change and what should stay fixed.

Evidence labels: Observed = text inspected directly; Reported = a checker/program's assertion not independently executed; Inferred = a conclusion of this audit from observed text; Not tested = missing source, implementation, history, or independent reader evidence.
