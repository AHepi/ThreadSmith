## Passage 11

The dockyard clerk read through the harbourmaster's log for the week. In it, the harbourmaster had written that the trawler crossed the bar early because the tide had turned. The log went on to list who had crewed the boat and how much the catch had weighed. The clerk also noted, from her own count, that six boats had tied up before noon. The rest of the entry concerned repairs due on the north pier.

## Passage 12

The building surveyor filed a short report on the flood in the cellar. She wrote that the cellar had flooded because the valve may have been left open. The report noted that the water had reached the second step by morning. It also recorded that the caretaker had been away for the weekend.

## Passage 13

The morning was cold and the platform was nearly empty. The train left the station late because the signal failed at the junction. Two porters carried the mailbags to the far end of the platform. By the time it pulled out, the platform lamps had already been switched off.

## Passage 14

The incoming nurse read the handover notes left by the night sister. The notes said the sister planned to move the patient nearer the desk so that his breathing would be easier to monitor. The notes also recorded that his temperature had been normal at midnight. The last line told the day staff to chart his medication at six.

## Passage 16

The farm foreman kept notes on his evening rounds. He wrote that the shepherd had concluded the flock must be in the top field, since the gate stood open. The foreman added, from what he saw himself, that no grass remained in the lower field. The note ended with the time the rounds had finished.
