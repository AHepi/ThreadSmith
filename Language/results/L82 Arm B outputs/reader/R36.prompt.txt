# Reader brief (Arm B): read one report

You are a reader. Below is the printed report of a checker on a short passage of prose. You have nothing else: not the passage, not the ledger the checker read, not the rules. Say only what the report lets you say.

Answer in exactly these four numbered parts, plain English, short sentences:
1. **What the checker found**: one sentence per finding, in your own words. If the report says nothing was found, or lists checks as asked with nothing found, say so.
2. **Which sentence of the passage each finding points at**: give the sentence number the report prints for it, or write "the report does not say".
3. **Inside a report or story**: if the report shows findings inside a named world (a story, an account, a note, a log), say which world and what was found inside it, and whether anything was found in the passage's own claims outside that world.
4. **What you could not make out**: one line, and what one added sentence would settle it.

Rules: guess at nothing about the passage; do not judge whether the writer was right; do not invent a sentence number the report does not print. Begin your answer with the line READER: and end with the line READING COMPLETE.
