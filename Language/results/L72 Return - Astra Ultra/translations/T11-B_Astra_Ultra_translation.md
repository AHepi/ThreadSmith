## 4. Readings I chose

Sentence 1: ‘reason for concluding’ is inferential, so I use SINCE rather than BECAUSE. The inspector’s conclusion is attributed, so its content and inferential relation belong to inspector_world. ‘The burning lamp’ also presents the lamp’s burning as GIVEN in the narrator’s actual sentence. The reason is explicitly named inside the attribution, so it has a separate told line; no actual fact is inherited automatically into that world.

Sentence 1: the reason is the lamp in its stated burning condition, represented as burning(lamp), not an unstated law about all lamps or evidence of visitors in general. I retain ‘burning’ rather than replace it with a different physical verb. It is a state Fact, not a DOER–VERB–THING happening.

Sentence 2: I take ‘It’ to refer to the burning lamp, not to the act of concluding. ‘Not what made the visitor return’ denies that cause; it does not deny the return. File 38 explicitly leaves an effect standing when its cause alone is denied. The actual return is therefore GIVEN from sentence 2, separately from the inspector’s TOLD conclusion in sentence 1.

Sentences 1–2: ‘a visitor’ and ‘the visitor’ receive one name. Return has no object and is kept as an eventive Fact; no return location, doer–object frame or physical shape is invented. ‘Had returned’ precedes the reported conclusion; no measured duration or second return is added.

Sentences 1–2: the reported inferential relation itself is retained as said-content; I add no SHOWS rule that would supply its support, and no MAKES rule that would settle the causal denial. Things are assigned only the stated kinds and no press response.
