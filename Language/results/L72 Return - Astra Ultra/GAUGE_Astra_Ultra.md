The verb column is Astra Ultra’s inventory from file 38’s fixed shape book, not a physical-law finding printed by rig 1. It counts distinct lexical verbs per text retained as Happenings or eventive Facts. Return in T11-B appears in two standings and is counted once. Descriptive states such as burning, open and stayed dry, copulas, causal relation words represented as BECAUSE/SINCE, and attribution words retained only in the bin are not happening verbs in this inventory.
