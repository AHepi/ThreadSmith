3. **Sentence 1 — "caused".** Readings open: (a) one thing produced another → BECAUSE; (b) a reason to expect or believe → SINCE; (c) a standing general MAKES rule ("opening a window makes a letter stay dry"). Taken: (a) only. Option (c) is refused: it would write as a premise the very claim the sentence is making (translator's rule 9).

Taken: "not stated", said-mark, because the text says nothing and any guessed class would be a filled-in line.
6. **Spelling.** No slips; all words read literally. No line written from a corrected reading.
