# Audit of this audit

## What is evidence here

The authorities are the supplied 38 and 39, preserved unchanged. An interpretation of an older rulebook is not evidence that these files contain the same defect. The inherited corpus is a collection of tests, not an authoritative answer key. Every reported conflict is therefore tied to the wording of 38/39, and every inherited expectation is distinguished from the current assessment.

This is a manual document and semantic audit. No checker executable, parser, runtime trace or independent model output was supplied. The six worked translations were written by this assistant. The 324 inherited inputs each have an indexed receipt; related variants share a case-family rationale rather than constituting 324 independent experiments. The 60 new inputs form 30 further contrast families. No statistical accuracy, semantic pass rate, exhaustive search or implementation conformance follows from their number.

## Corrections that made the findings narrower

The explicit kingfisher colour/burning contrast is a control, not a surviving counterexample to 38/39. Both literal source assertions and a conservative partial treatment of an image are already available. The isolated Hopkins fragment has too little context to warrant an exclusive plain paraphrase. Binning its figurative content is an honest scope boundary, provided an empty check is not presented as full analysis of the poem.

An ALWAYS countercase can still be recorded as a universal claim, its condition and its contrary outcome. This defeats an overbroad accusation that the language cannot detect any universal counterexample. F01 is instead the inconsistent permission to record the exception relation itself, especially an exemption asserted without an outcome. A repair must not invent a negative outcome to avoid the conflict.

Negated mental attributions can safely remain whole in the bin. F02 therefore criticises the conflicting blanket extraction instruction in 39, not an inevitable error of every translator. The revision preserves operators without creating a theory of beliefs, desires or mental correctness.

An uncaused outcome can be recorded as an opaque Fact. F04 is not total unrepresentability; it concerns preserving the outcome-specific representation and capability without inventing a producer. Similarly, patient-free ringing is recordable as a Fact. R12 is explicitly an optional structural extension rather than an in-scope contradiction of a transitive grammar.

The general contradiction checker can detect a positive causal claim and its explicit negation even without a supported mechanism. F15 concerns precedence of that safeguard over the local “fine unless” sentence, not proof that a real checker necessarily misses the contradiction.

The original Rule 9 already constrains answer-smuggling additions. The delete-all-said diagnostic is not expressly claimed to be a complete validation of that rule. F08 identifies what this diagnostic cannot establish; it does not attribute to the source a licence to invent bridges.

The what-if text already removes explicitly tied results. The camera challenge concerns the applicability of actual observations to the altered case, particularly where the dependency is not settled. It is not evidence that every observation is retained by the original rules, and it does not repair their acknowledged multi-cause limitation.

## Corrections to the inherited controls

T22's explicit cancellation makes its intended nonachievement reading available, but this does not establish that every bare English “helped move” expression lacks an achievement implication. N17 deliberately concerns assistance to an attempted opening, so the distinction is not hidden in an awkward cancellation.

T27 is useful against turning “one of” into “exactly one.” A separately asserted numerical exclusivity, however, does not acquire a complete numerical/disjunctive calculus by being placed in the bin. T31's default-priority material likewise cannot be used to demand a priority mechanism that the language does not supply.

T42's word replacements are licensed only where the fixture establishes the intended identity or equivalent descriptions. Sharing a dictionary neighbourhood is not a general guarantee of semantic equivalence. A change from one object kind to another can matter to response classes and must not be called harmless merely because it looks like a noun substitution.

Several U-series counterparts add clarifying information. They test whether a reading changes when context changes; they are not automatically meaning-preserving rewrites. The files retain the two texts and their local question rather than presenting every counterpart as a paraphrase.

L06, L13 and L14 are short fragments whose missing contexts constrain interpretation. Familiarity with a famous author or work does not put the rest of that work into the submitted input. These fragments do not supply an unquestionable gold answer merely because the wider work is known.

An independently reported actual outcome does not become counterfactually independent. This distinction bears on C15, T46 and the new photograph controls. Conversely, deleting every independently supported background fact would also be an error. The retained repair separates the actual observation record from a warrant for altered-case applicability.

## Repairs that the second pass rejected

Making every mental complement TOLD invents affirmations. Binning every reported content loses a feature expressly admitted by the language. R02 retains only the content actually presented as asserted within the report and preserves excluded embedding intact.

Treating agreement among the considered interpretations as source certainty fails when those interpretations are explicitly incomplete. Requiring exhaustive interpretation enumeration before preserving any local statement fails clear local controls. R03 keeps consequential uncertainty without turning an entire passage into uncertainty.

Treating any action that changes a goal dependency as a working plan ignores direction. Requiring the action to establish a necessary condition is still insufficient to establish the goal. R08 now distinguishes relevance, necessary conditions and a supplied route to the proposed outcome. It does not demand a general planner.

Deleting every causal association to permit uncaused outcomes loses explicit causal assertions. Requiring a producer on every Result invents one when the source does not. R05a separates the outcome from the causal relation and keeps both when both are supplied.

Treating every ALWAYS exception as a contradiction fails ordinary out-of-scope instances of an explicitly restricted rule. Silently weakening ALWAYS to USUALLY rewrites the source. R04 preserves both rule strength and exception kind.

Banning a conjecture because it was prompted by the outcome blocks legitimate proposed explanations. Accepting it as an independent observation manufactures support. R09 separates the proposal from its asserted warrant.

## The worked translations were checked too

An internal review found that the two kingfisher ledgers initially recorded feathers without expressly preserving whose feathers they were. Both now include a said ownership Fact tied to the source's possessive expression. Their counts were updated to five and seven said lines respectively. This is a correction to these new outputs, not a modification of the frozen source texts.

The negated-belief bin initially pointed to the source printed elsewhere in the document. That did not fully serve the ledger-plus-bin reconstruction criterion. It now repeats the two short source sentences inside the bin itself. Merely keeping the source somewhere in a package is not the same as making the promised translation self-contained.

STRUCK is not a shape-book entry. It is retained without silently equating it to HIT. The pear contact table similarly retains TOUCHED without inventing a checked pressing shape. Unknown verb shape is a stated capability limit, not a finding that the event is impossible.

The use of TOLD for all narrated fixtures is explicit. The original single standing field does not also preserve a separate local CLAIMED/GIVEN status inside that world. The worked examples therefore do not certify a complete within-story assertion/background analysis. A general layered scope/status extension remains open, not silently added to the revised prompt.

## What remains provisional

R06 requires a declared choice about the narrow force-dynamic construction versus ordinary production language. A translator's label does not itself settle that linguistic choice.

R11 preserves source-occurrence and event-identity distinctions as semantic obligations. No complete machine representation or co-reference algorithm has been validated. R12 is not adopted by the companion prompt. R05a changes the representation of outcomes and requires implementation review before use with existing code.

Nested cases and nested belief execution, quantitative force, a general theory of spatial containment, normative modality and jointly acting causes remain excluded. Honest loss reports on those questions are not counted as failures. A case that crosses those boundaries is identified as such in the coverage records.

No verdict here shows that the language operationalises an absent 02 or 36. The supplied files can be audited on their own wording, but conformance to an unseen parent is a different claim.

## Stopping statement

This package records a completed finite audit pass and an audit of its candidate repairs. It does not assert that all possible breaks were found, that every retained amendment is necessary, that the amendments are an executable complete definition, or that no further criticism is possible. The structural validation checks identifiers, text hashes, file integrity and count reconciliation only. They do not judge meanings.
