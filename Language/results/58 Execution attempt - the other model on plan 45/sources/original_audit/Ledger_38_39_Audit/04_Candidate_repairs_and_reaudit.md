# Candidate repairs, including the repairs that fail

## How to read this dossier

These are proposed changes or clarifications, not claims about unseen code. Each is tested against its triggering case, a nearby rival, an opposite/control case, and a further challenge. “Retained” means the recorded objection has a defensible answer under the stated limits. It does not mean exhaustive survival, independent replication, implementation conformance, or a computed hardness score.

R01 through R12 name repair families. Where a family has separable clauses, the subclauses are identified so that unrelated jobs are not hidden inside one supposedly indispensable part. Source anchors and finding identifiers refer to `00_Audit_report.md`. New source cases are in `02_New_adversarial_paragraphs.md`.

## R01. Local reading with recoverable residue

**Candidate.** Interpret complete source constructions in context. Preserve the source expression, its scope and consequential formatting in the bin when excluded. Retain separately stated admitted facts. A selected paraphrase of imagery is filled in unless its relevant content is explicit. The report identifies what was retained only as an unanalysed name.

**Tempting first repair, rejected.** “For poetry, record only explicit literal facts.” N01-B and N04-B are literal happenings within literary passages; a blanket rule can erase them. More subtly, literalness is itself an interpretive judgment, not something a genre filter supplies.

**Second tempting repair, rejected.** “Translate every image into its most likely plain meaning.” N02-A does not establish an optical mechanism, intact feathers or a particular catastrophe. The proposed paraphrase can invent a determinate source commitment.

**Remove or swap.** Removing source preservation permits a one-line image note to lose whether a claim was denied, quoted or hypothetical. Replacing local decisions with a whole-paragraph figurative label loses the actual chair in T01 and the bolt in U02. Replacing every verb by a physical shape turns N03's colour description into contact.

**Relevant and irrelevant changes.** Replacing the explicit colour clarification with explicit burning changes the combustion answer. Consistent renaming of the narrator does not. Moving a true line boundary inside an unfinished sentence must not add a period, as N28 shows.

**Further attack.** Copy the entire text to the bin and leave the ledger empty. That preserves archival text but does not perform the promised translation of admitted events. The retained repair requires extraction of the admitted commitments and a coverage statement; source preservation is not sufficient by itself.

**Disposition.** Retained as a translation and reporting obligation. It does not compute a unique literary interpretation or demand literal paraphrase of every image.

## R02. Preserve the embedding before extracting the content

**Candidate.** TOLD is for content the source presents as asserted within a described report/story/note/dream. Do not infer that status merely from being inside belief, desire, denial, doubt, a command or a conditional. Preserve excluded attribution together with its operator and scope in the bin. Use an admitted mention-only form only for jobs already admitted; do not enlarge psychology silently.

**Tempting first repair, rejected.** “Give each person one TOLD world and put every mental complement there.” N05 places an explicitly unheld proposition into a belief-like world. N06 puts a desired ripe condition alongside a described unripe condition, inviting a conflict the source did not assert as a factual worldview.

**Nearest rival, also rejected.** “Never retain reported content.” T06, C05 and N11 require report/world content to remain accessible and permit an actual contradiction inside one told world. Blanket binning throws away an admitted job.

**Remove or swap.** Remove the embedding guard and N05/N07 can acquire affirmative attributions. Replace TOLD by actual CLAIMED everywhere and the liar's window creates the wrong outer clash. Replace every embedded clause by SUPPOSED and it inherits actual background that the told-world rule forbids.

**Further attack.** A note asserts an if-then relation, but not its antecedent. The word asserts must be read at the level of the conditional, not as permission to assert each component. N08 remains whole as a conditional; an unavailable nested-case analysis stays in the bin.

**Opposite control.** In N07-B the affirmative report really was made. The repair must retain it. In N11-A the contradiction is explicitly in one tale and must not be hidden by inventing separate worlds.

**Disposition.** Retained within the declared scope. Nested belief/case execution and general doxastic consistency remain excluded.

## R03. Conditional selected readings and correlated alternatives

**Candidate.** A selected filled-in reading yields a result conditional on that reading. Keep material alternatives and the reason for the choice recoverable from the ledger/bin, with a pointer from the chosen-readings section. Keep jointly constrained choices together. Do not infer completeness from agreement among an incomplete set of readings. Preserve settled local content independently.

**First repair, rejected.** “Report all conclusions shared by the listed readings as source facts.” C03 defeats it: the list is explicitly incomplete and gives no independently settled key-holder.

**Second repair, rejected.** “Never conclude anything unless every possible reading has been enumerated.” C04 and T80 contain explicit local facts. This repair converts ordinary source certainty into universal fog.

**Remove or swap.** Without joint alternatives, N27 can invent both people moving red by recombining slots. Replacing selected-reading conditionality with a silent default makes T39's pronoun referent falsely settled. Merely adding many variants does not establish interpretive coverage.

**Further attack.** Allow any fanciful interpretation to prevent a conclusion. The retained proposal requires a textual or contextual reason for a material alternative; it does not license arbitrary stories. Ordinary explicit identity controls such as T70 and T71 still resolve their local questions.

**Disposition.** Retained. No complete interpretation enumerator or fixed probability threshold is supplied. The branch choice remains a marked translator judgment, not an assertion of the checker.

## R04. Permit the exceptions the language claims to report

**Candidate.** An Exception may point to a USUALLY or ALWAYS line and states non-application or a countercase. Do not alter the target rule's original strength or scope. For ALWAYS, report the claimed exemption/scope dispute or the countercase conflict that the language already promises.

**First repair, rejected.** “Convert a rule to USUALLY whenever an exception appears.” N12-A and T34 lose the author's unqualified commitment. The repair wins by changing the source.

**Nearest rival, too coarse.** “Every ALWAYS exception is a contradiction.” N13-B is outside the expressly daytime scope; ordinary non-application does not contradict it. A claimed exemption without an outcome also does not supply a negative outcome.

**Remove or swap.** Remove ALWAYS targets and N12 again cannot be recorded as requested. Replace the two exception kinds by a generic cancellation and the difference between a scope claim and an observed countercase disappears.

**Further attack.** An author calls an instance exempt from a rule that expressly includes it. Do not silently accept the exemption as an amendment. Preserve both commitments and report their tension; an invented outcome is still not licensed.

**Disposition.** Retained as a correction of the direct cross-document conflict. Ordinary fact-based detection of countercases remains available and is not falsely claimed as newly invented.

## R05. Separate outcome, mention and necessity

**R05a, outcome clause.** A Result records a qualitative outcome and any stated stage. A source-asserted causal link is separately recorded as BECAUSE; stage/temporal association alone is not that link.

**R05a, rejected rival and re-test.** Deleting all “as a result of” information loses N14-B and N15-B. Keeping it mandatory invents a producer in N14-A or reasserts the denied producer in N15-A. The separate relation survives both controls. Unknown causes do not imply that nothing caused the event.

**R05b, mention clause.** A numbered MENTION wrapper may name an admitted endpoint without asserting the enclosed content. Its standing concerns the source's introduction of that mentioned content. References retrieve content, not a held premise. This clarifies an existing semantic distinction rather than adding a fifteenth subject matter.

**R05b, rejected rival and re-test.** Giving every target SUPPOSED standing makes N22's goal or T48's ringing an assumption. Conversely, making every endpoint merely mentioned erases actual results. Only the intended nonasserting occurrences receive the wrapper.

**R05c, necessity clause.** The Only-ways negative-all-alternatives compilation is ALWAYS SHOWS within the original scope. It does not become a causal MAKES rule solely from necessity.

**R05c, rejected rival and re-test.** Choosing MAKES to represent every missing prerequisite lets an inferential necessity relation manufacture production. Banning every causal absence assertion also fails: a source may separately say an absence produced a result. Preserve that separate assertion; do not infer it from Only-ways alone.

**Group-removal check.** R05a without R05b can still let a queried outcome become a premise. R05b without R05a can still put an unasserted producer inside an actual Result. R05c does a different job and is independently removable without repairing either error; it is not claimed to be necessary to them.

**Disposition.** Retained as three separable clarifications/changes. Existing software compatibility has not been tested.

## R06. Qualify the causal-word test rather than legislate every English verb

**Candidate.** Make explicit when MAKES denotes the stipulated force-dynamic construction and when a source simply attributes production. Do not refute every ordinary production claim solely because a prior tendency is present. LETS and HELPS alone do not assert achievement; STOPS does not assert every contrary result.

**First repair, rejected.** “A prior tendency means nothing can cause continued movement.” N16 supplies the contrary source attribution without changing the question into a quantitative force problem. Even if the attribution is false of the world, its ordinary meaning has not become an assertion of opposing tendency.

**Nearest rival, rejected.** “Ignore tendencies entirely.” This removes the stated job of the narrow LETS/MAKES construction and the declared missing-tendency diagnostic. It changes the force-dynamic project rather than repairing its translation boundary.

**Further attack.** Translate “made her continue” as BECAUSE in one run and as the narrow MAKES construction in another, then publish their different verdicts as if the same interpretation were fixed. The retained candidate requires that consequential reading to be explicit. It does not claim that a label alone solves lexical interpretation.

**Opposite control.** An explicit source description of production against an opposing tendency can still receive the narrow forcing analysis. An explicit assistance-to-an-attempt clause in N17 does not establish success.

**Disposition.** Retained as a bounded proposal requiring an owner choice about the scope of the force-dynamic terms. Not declared a fully settled replacement theory of causation. The companion translator should preserve a plain production attribution rather than infer an unstated tendency.

## R07. Keep direct clashes, route support and the target separate

**R07a, direct clash.** An affirmed relation and its explicitly negated counterpart in the same context and respect clash as commitments even if neither is independently supported. Preserve provenance: a clash depending on a filled-in line is conditional on that addition.

**Rejected rival.** “Every unsupported causal denial is a contradiction.” N18-B defeats it. A denied cause with no positive counterpart and no producing route need not clash with anything. Conversely, dropping unsupported statements to clean the ledger destroys source fidelity in N18-A.

**R07b, route clause.** An independent route can support E without validating the particular C-to-E reason being assessed. State both findings where applicable. No source report or inferred consequence becomes independent merely by being copied.

**Rejected rival.** “A circular offered route invalidates every conclusion it mentions.” N19 has an independent press-based route. Reject only the unsupported route; preserve the independently supported conclusion on its premises.

**R07c, target clause.** The final conclusion is the explicitly designated target of the argument being assessed; page order is not its definition. When no target is settled, retain multiple candidates or report the ambiguity.

**Further attack.** Call every proposition a final conclusion to avoid choosing. N20 explicitly designates one. The repair must use that information rather than manufacture uncertainty. Several separate arguments can have separate targets; no new single global target is imposed.

**Disposition.** Retained as separable semantic/reporting clauses. They require no external truth oracle or new statistical ranking.

## R08. Relevance is not enough for a plan verdict

**Candidate.** SO THAT may establish that an action affects a stated goal dependency. A favourable goal verdict additionally needs a source-supplied applicable route in the appropriate direction. A necessary condition alone is not a sufficient route. Where that route is absent, report cannot tell, not success.

**First repair, rejected.** “Any action affecting a dependency makes the plan work.” N21-A affects the lid in the wrong direction.

**Second repair, rejected by a fresh challenge.** “The action makes a necessary condition true, so the goal follows.” Fresh case: “Opening the gate needs the latch released. This action releases the latch. The source says nothing about whether release makes the gate open.” Releasing the latch supplies a prerequisite, not the target. This case does not require several interacting causes; it only refuses an illicit converse.

**Opposite control.** Where the source explicitly supplies a sufficient route, as in N21-B or N22-B, do not withhold a conditional route finding merely because every real-world factor has not been modelled. It remains a finding on the stated premises, not empirical success.

**Remove or swap.** Remove direction and N21 fails. Remove necessity/sufficiency and the fresh latch case fails. Replace all goal checks with refusal and the explicit sufficient-route controls lose a promised admitted use.

**Disposition.** Retained within the stated plan-dependency scope. If the intended original check was only relevance, this changes its report wording rather than proving its original relevance question wrong.

## R09. Do not promote a diagnostic's silence into validation

**Candidate.** Keep the added-lines-alone diagnostic as a warning when it fires. Its silence does not establish that an added bridge is legitimate, source-grounded, noncircular or discriminating. Findings depending on additions remain conditional and point to them. Translator Rule 9 remains a separate obligation.

**First repair, rejected.** “Ban all filled-in lines.” C10 is a legitimate conjecture proposed as such, and unresolved reference choices may need explicit provisional readings. The task allows additions; banning them changes it.

**Second repair, rejected.** “Delete all said lines; a stopped conclusion proves the additions are harmless.” N23-A defeats this exactly: a fabricated rule parasitises a real source fact.

**Further attack.** Change the fabricated rule to its opposite when asked the opposite question. N24 exposes absence of discrimination. The retained candidate records that criticism without pretending a finite fixed diagnostic has searched every possible rival premise.

**Group check.** R09 without R07b still lets an independent endpoint route launder a bad offered reason. R07b without R09 still lets a fabricated bridge appear validated merely because it used a source premise. The two protect different joins in the assessment.

**Disposition.** Retained as a reporting and translator-review limit, not as a completed computable test for all legitimate additions.

## R10. Actual evidence stays actual unless carryover is warranted

**Candidate.** A source record about the actual case remains a record about that case after a what-if is considered. It does not automatically become a premise that the same outcome holds in the altered case. Preserve the independent background specified by the change and the language's explicit limits. When applicability in the altered case is not supplied, leave the outcome unsettled.

**First repair, rejected.** “Keep every independently observed outcome.” C15 and N25 confuse evidential independence with an invariant outcome.

**Second repair, rejected.** “Delete every actual fact.” That discards genuine unchanged starting conditions and turns WITHDRAW into physical alteration. N26 and T45 protect the distinction.

**Further attack.** An auditor declares a desired outcome invariant merely because no dependency link was written. Absence of the link is not a carryover warrant. Conversely, an expressly supplied applicable background commitment must not be dropped simply to make the case look unresolved.

**Boundary.** This proposal does not fix the acknowledged multi-cause problem, create nested case execution or replace the stated rule for declining an incompatible MAKE NOT SO request. Those remain separately declared limits.

**Disposition.** Retained as a clarification of what evidence is about. A general computable intervention model is not supplied.

## R11. Occurrence and event identity without fictitious witnesses

**Candidate.** Preserve each source occurrence and any explicitly stated same-event identity without turning repetition into another event or independent warrant. A canonical event may have multiple source attestations. An alias or reidentification used by a check must be an explicit, revisable translation commitment, not a silent equality guessed by the checker.

**First repair, rejected.** “Deduplicate every identical sentence.” T41 has repeated reports of one event, but a repeated sentence can also describe a new push at another stage. N29-B explicitly supplies that distinction.

**Second repair, rejected.** “Each line number is a new physical happening.” N29-A explicitly denies a second push.

**Further attack.** Treat the words once and twice as permission to model general arithmetic. The proposed job is preserving the explicitly stated identity/distinction; arbitrary quantified event counts remain outside scope. Copy counts are not observation counts, and no evidence weight is inferred.

**Disposition.** Retained as a representation clarification. A full alias syntax or automated coreference solver is not included in the companion prompt. Where the existing format cannot express the relation, retain it in the bin and do not claim event-deletion or evidence-independence checking occurred.

## R12. Optional intransitive event extension

**Candidate.** A Happening records an occurrence and only the participants/roles supplied by the source. A patient or agent may be absent when the source supplies none. The verb remains the source verb and its shape remains unknown where the shape book has no entry.

**First repair, rejected.** “Every bell ringing means someone rang it.” N30-A supplies no agent. A dummy patient is equally invented.

**Nearest rival.** Keep every intransitive as a Fact. This is a defensible conservative alternative and preserves simple state-like content. It does not automatically provide the Happening/Event-specific comparison and causal checks.

**Further attack.** Permit arbitrary participant deletion even where the source names a patient. N30-B must retain Ada as agent and the bell as patient. “Optional” means absent when unstated, not removable whenever convenient.

**Disposition.** Optional, not silently adopted. The original displayed Happening grammar can define a narrower scope, so this is not counted as an in-scope refutation. Its benefit is a stated expansion in directly representable occurrences. It needs a separate complete definition before claiming implementation support.

## What survived the audit of the audit

The strongest repairs do not add a universal literary understanding module. They preserve scope, attribution, dependence and source uncertainty, and they stop the report claiming more than a limited check has established.

A single sentence's change of wording is not automatically a valid invariance test. Several original U counterparts introduce facts absent from their base, and T22's completed-help phrasing is not a clean ordinary-language control. Those defects remain recorded instead of being edited out of the frozen corpus.

No candidate is declared universally hard to vary. R06 still has a semantic design choice, R11 lacks an automated identity contract, and R12 is not adopted. The remaining candidates have survived the specific attacks stated here as proposals, not as executed code.
