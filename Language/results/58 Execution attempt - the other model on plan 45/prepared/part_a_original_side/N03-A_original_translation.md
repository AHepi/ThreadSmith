TRANSLATION COMPLETE.

**Audit result.** This tests the distinction between describing a colour and asserting an event of touching. The exact phrase “They are touched red.” appears in the University of Pennsylvania transcription of Wallace Stevens's *Study of Two Pears*. That four-word external illustration motivated this separately authored probe; it is not a previously recovered corpus item or a full translation of Stevens.
