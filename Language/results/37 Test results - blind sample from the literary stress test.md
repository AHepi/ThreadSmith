## What was fixed, and the reruns
- A told world. A story, report, note or dream now stands alone and does not inherit the actual ledger. T07-B comes out clean.
- "Cannot tell" for a plan the ledger knows nothing about. T10-B now says the route from action to aim is unstated.
- All 29 other ledgers rerun: reports unchanged.
- The language file was updated as a new clean file, 38.

## What this shows and does not show
Shows: on eight outside texts the language held its main lines (mention is not fact; achieved is not caused; a reason is not a producer), and failed exactly where predicted (a told story). Does not show: anything about the other 316 inputs; anything about irony, metaphor or voice, which file 38 sends to the bin; anything about a translator other than Claude.
