# 21 Test plan - your Markus paragraph

Written before the run. Not edited afterwards.
Authority document: "Claude Fable Semantics - standalone theory" (file 10). Translated by the rulebook (file 20).

## The paragraph, as you sent it
"That darn Markus. He's always so crude and impolite. When the wedding comes, expect him to smash the cake. That's usually what he does."

## What kind of case this is
An argument, but not the kind rig 1 was built on. Tomato and henhouse argue about what PRODUCED something. This one gives REASONS TO EXPECT something: a habit ("usually what he does") and a character ("always crude"). Nobody says the habit causes the smashing. The authority document keeps these two kinds of question apart (what produces it / what can we tell from what we see).

## How I will translate it
- "That darn Markus", "so": tone. To the bin.
- "He's always so crude and impolite": CLAIMED, a standing description, strength ALWAYS.
- "When the wedding comes": GIVEN (there will be a wedding). That there will be a cake: filled in.
- "expect him to smash the cake": CLAIMED, a prediction. "Expect" as an instruction to the listener: to the bin.
- "That's usually what he does": CLAIMED, a habit, strength USUALLY. By rule 7 it SHOWS, it does not MAKE. "That" read as smashing the cake; "at weddings" is my filling in.
- The text never says "because". The two links (prediction resting on the habit; prediction resting on the character) are filled in by me and marked so.
- "Smash the cake" as a happening for rig 2: Markus, smash, cake.

## What I expect from both rigs, unchanged
- Rig 1: a FALSE ALARM. Since patch 3, a BECAUSE is tested only on what MAKES things happen. A habit only SHOWS. So rig 1 will call the prediction a JUMP, when it is a perfectly ordinary inference from a habit.
- Rig 1 on the second link (crude, so he will smash the cake): JUMP, and this time fairly. Nothing connects being crude to smashing cakes.
- Rig 2: "smash" is not in the shape book, so: not checked, and counted. And there is no way to write the result, because a cake that is smashed has changed state, not moved. The rulebook's results are only moves, stays and touches.
- The standing slot will mark three lines CLAIMED and will not be able to say which of them is the conclusion.

## What would count as a failure of my prediction
Rig 1 reports the first link as following; or rig 2 checks "smash".

## What I expect to have to build
A second kind of because, for reasons to expect, tested on what follows by any route and reported with its unstated assumptions. And a way to name the conclusion: the claimed line that others support and that supports nothing.
