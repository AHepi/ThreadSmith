# L82 Test plan - Arm B, sixteen reported arguments, two API translators, old rig and new, second version

DRAFT until the premise check returns; then frozen by this file's SHA-256, recorded in the log, and not edited. Second version: the first (kept beside this one) was written before the calibration note L81 was reviewed; the review (results/L81 Review) found that no role saw a ledger beside its prose, that only one of the four pairs was a matched pair in the skill's sense, and that the corpus could not test the owner's question about vague translations. This version adds six passages (a hedged pair, a narrator's-own passage, three near-neighbour rewordings), a prose reader who sees the passage beside the report, and the expectations B10 to B13. Written under Lessons L10, L11 and L12, decision L11 (the API agents), the regime of review A4 (L76) and the calibration L81 second version. Arm A held the translator constant and showed the second driver does what it says on the ledgers in hand. Arm B varies the translator and the text: sixteen new passages nobody in the loop wrote, each a report of someone else's argument, translated by two outside models that have never seen a ledger of these texts, run on the old driver and the new, with a reader given the reports alone.

## Who does what, and what each is blind to
- **Text writer**: a Sonnet agent, from the brief in `tests/L82 Arm B corpus brief/` and a second brief (in the log, L83); it wrote ten passages in four matched pairs and two controls, then six more (a hedged pair, a narrator's-own passage, three rewordings), with a map of which is which; both maps are sealed (`MAP.enc`, `MAP2.enc`) and the orchestrator has not read them (the writer once rewrote the first map after it was sealed; its hash matched the seal and it was deleted unread). The pairs are visible to anyone who reads the texts (each pair shares its opening sentence), so the seal checks the writer's intent at marking, not the marker's blindness; every expectation below is stated by class and by observable features of the texts, never by the map.
- **Translators**: Atria (`Atria-Dawn-Preview`, 30 requests a minute) and Mimo (`mimo-v2.6-pro`, 100), each called once per text through `tools/translate_via_api.py`, which sends 39, L80, the encoding guide with three examples, and the text; a ledger that fails validation is sent back once with the failure. Neither sees the other's work, the plan, the map, or any earlier ledger of these texts (there are none).
- **Runner**: the orchestrator, by script: both drivers on every ledger, `consequences_2.py` on every ledger, `sameness_2.py` within each pair per translator and across translators per text; every output kept.
- **Reader**: Atria, one fresh call per report through the reader brief in the brief folder, on all 64 reports (32 ledgers, old and new) in a shuffled order under neutral names; it never sees a passage or a ledger.
- **Prose reader**: Mimo, one fresh call per new-driver report through the prose reader brief, given the passage and the report together, never a ledger; it says, per finding, whether the passage's reported person claimed it as a fact, hedged it, or never made it, and whether anything argued in the passage is missing from the report.
- **Runner script**: `tools/L82_run.sh` with `tests/L82 Arm B corpus brief/PAIRS.txt`; translations in parallel lanes per provider.
- **Markers**: the orchestrator against this table; then a fresh Opus agent given this plan, the outputs and the opened map, and nothing else; both markings kept.
- **Premise checker**: a fresh Opus agent, before the freeze.

## The texts, by observable features (the map is not consulted)
The corpus is `results/L82 Corpus - sixteen reported arguments/`, B01 to B16, hashes in P1. Read from the texts alone: B01 and B08 share their opening and B01 adds a rule ("always left that gate open only once ..."); B03 and B06 share theirs and B06 adds a rule ("always crosses the bar early once ..."); B04 and B09 share theirs and B04 adds a route ("since a bed near the desk let ..."); B05 and B10 share theirs and differ in what is denied (B05 denies the same cause it claimed, the bus route; B10 denies a different one, the entrance hall); B02 and B07 contain no because, since, so that or always. Call the passage of a pair with the added rule, route or different cause its **S-side** (B01, B06, B04, B10) and the other its **F-side** (B08, B03, B09, B05); B02 and B07 are the **controls**. The six added: B12 and B15 share their opening and differ in one word, B12's cause clause carrying "may have been" and B15's "had been" (the **hedged pair**); B13 is the one passage with no report in it, whose bare because is the narrator's own (**narrator's-own**); B16, B11 and B14 are B08, B03 and B09 with one non-argument sentence reworded and every other sentence byte-identical (the **rewordings**). In every passage but B13 the argument is a reported person's, inside a note, log, minutes, report or handover: under 39's attribution rule both translators are expected to put it in a told world; B13's belongs in the actual ledger.

## What is scored and what is only recorded
By L81: differences of wording, line form, standing where the text does not settle it, and Thing lines are recorded from the cross-translator sameness runs and not scored. What is scored is the report kind inside the world, the variation within pairs, the controls, the reader's trace.

## Near-miss cases that must not fire
The four S-sides (B2), the two controls (B4), every old-driver report (B8), the three rewordings against their originals (B12), and B15 against B12 in the prose reader's answers (B10).

## Not tested
The world half of the split; rig 2; a reader other than Atria and a prose reader other than Mimo; the outside-line count at a nonzero value (no passage has a general line outside its world); which translation is right.

## Traps
- Reading the map before marking. It is opened after both markings of B1 to B9 are drafted, and used only to confirm the writer's intent matched the observable features.
- Scoring wording differences. L81 section 3.
- Reading B7's misses as the language failing. They charge 39's room for choice; B1 and B2 are where the language and the driver are charged.
