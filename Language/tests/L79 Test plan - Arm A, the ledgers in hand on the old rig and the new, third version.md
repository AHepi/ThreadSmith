- D3. Where any tying line or set-aside line is filled in or usual case: `  NOTE: leans on lines you did not write: h.` The verdict rule does not change. *Gives up:* report length.
- D4. (No ledger in hand carries a `line` key in its whatifs entries, so the what-if head clause below is not exercised this round; it stands for Arm B.) The heads `BECAUSE-claim on line h`, `SINCE-claim on line g`, `PLAN on line b`, `DENIED BECAUSE on line n` become `... on line h [mark, sentence N]: "..."`. After a JUMP sentence: `  Claim line:` then that line in the fixed form (`  - line h [mark, sentence N]: text`); `  Cause's lines:` then the lines of every justification of `holds(cause)` as check 2 collects them for its FOLLOWS test (the same set, whatever it is), or `  - (no line supports the cause)`; `  Effect's direct lines:` then the lines that alone state the effect, or `  - (none)`. After a NO CONNECTION sentence: `  Claim line:`, `  Reason's lines:` (the same set check 2b collects), `  Expected's direct lines:`. After a CANNOT TELL sentence: `  Plan line:`. Chain rows become `  line g [mark, sentence N]: text  ->  VERDICT`. A what-if head becomes `WHAT-IF [whose] (line j, sentence N): "..."` when the ledger's whatifs entry carries a `line` naming a ledger line, else unchanged. The abduced "a single line that would close the jump" text is unchanged. *Gives up:* report length; a JUMP may over-name.
- D5. On every ledger, after the actual ledger's findings and before any world section, and at the end of each world section, an `OUTCOMES:` block, one line per check in this order and with these names: `contradictions`, `because claims`, `since claims`, `the chain`, `plans`, `exceptions`, `likeness`, `two usually lines`, `exemptions`, `added lines`, `what-ifs` (actual ledger only), `denied because`; each line `  <name>: asked, nothing found` or `  <name>: asked, N found` or `  <name>: not asked, no line of that kind` or `  <name>: ran out of time`. The `NO FAULT FOUND in the lines that were checked.` line is removed. *Gives up:* the one-line header.
- D6. Supposed cases are unchanged.
- D7. The gauge line keeps its marks prefix and reads `N bin entries over M sentences (not checked)` where it read `N of M sentences went to the leftover bin (not checked)`.
