DRAFT until the premise check returns; then frozen by this file's SHA-256, recorded in the log, and not edited. Written 23 September 2026 under decision L10's rule (a reversible pick, made without the owner's word; reversal costs a lesson), after Arm B (L84, finding 2; L85 decision 3): on the two rule texts the report printed `JUMP` and `NO CONNECTION` with `It would take a line nobody wrote`, while a general line of that kind sat in the ledger with conditions no line met, and the prose reader found it on three of four reports. This plan builds the driver's third version as a new file beside the second, changing what a JUMP and a NO CONNECTION print and nothing else, and tests it on the four rule ledgers, all 32 Arm B ledgers and all 76 Arm A ledgers. ## What is built (a new file beside the old, with a give-up line)
`rigs/rig 1 - arguments/patched/run_check_3.py`, made from `run_check_2.py` (P1) with these changes and no others; its header names the plan's hash and each D with its give-up line.
- **D1. A general line, defined for the driver.** From the ledger's `.pl` text (the same text the checker is given), a clause `Head :- Body.` whose head is `produced(F)` or `holds(F)`, whose body contains a goal `line(<id>)` (the line the clause belongs to; Arm A's ledgers carry their clauses in the `.pl` only, Arm B's in the `.pl` built from the JSON's `prolog_clauses`) and at least one goal other than it, is a general clause of that line; the line is a general line. (The language's MAKES and SHOWS lines are written this way by 39, the guide and both translators: P2; ledger A's line 5 is `produced(dies(P)) :- line(5), kind(P, tomato_plant), holds(reached(the_cold, P)).`) Clauses are read up to the full stop that ends them; body goals are split at top-level commas; a clause spanning lines is joined first.
- **D2. Under a JUMP**, after the `Effect's direct lines` block and before the `A single line that would close the jump` line, a block headed `  General lines that could produce <effect>, and what each needs:` listing every general clause whose head term matches the effect (the head's inner term and the effect's atom unify, variables in the head binding by position; a mismatched functor or arity does not match), as `    - line <id> [<mark>, sentence <N>]: <text>` followed, one per body goal other than `line(<id>)`, by `        <goal with the head's bindings applied>: ` and one of `holds (line <ids>)` when `ask("<goal>")` returns an answer (the ids are that answer's lines), `no line says so` when it returns none, or `open (<the unbound variables>)` when a variable stays unbound. When no general clause matches: `  General lines that could produce <effect>: (none)`.
- **D3. Under a NO CONNECTION**, after the `Expected's direct lines` block and before the `It would take a line nobody wrote` line, two blocks. First `  Reached without the reason:` listing, per route that `ask("holds(<expected>)", removed=direct)` returns whose lines do not meet the reason's lines, `    - lines <ids>` (the route's lines, sorted), or `  Reached without the reason: (no route)`. Second, the D2 block with `reach` for `produce`: `  General lines that could reach <expected>, and what each needs:` over general clauses whose head matches the expectation, each goal marked as in D2, and after the goals of each clause one line, `        the reason's atom, <reason>, is among these conditions` or `... is not among these conditions` (textual identity after bindings). When none matches: `  General lines that could reach <expected>: (none)`.
- **D4. Nowhere else.** Neither block is printed under FOLLOWS, FOLLOWS ONLY IF THE CAUSE IS GRANTED, CIRCLE, TO BE EXPECTED, CANNOT TELL, a plan verdict, a what-if, or in the actual ledger's or a world's OUTCOMES. The blocks are printed inside told worlds and supposed cases as under the actual ledger (the check runs there too, D2 of L79).
- **Give-up lines.** D1: a general clause written with another head (a `depends` or `changes` clause, or a bare `holds` fact) is not examined; D2, D3: report length, one `ask` per body goal (time), and a goal that is not a term the checker knows returns `no line says so` rather than an error; D4: none.
- **Removing the two headed blocks and their indented lines from a third-version report gives the second version's report byte for byte** (expectations E3, E4); the instrument for that is `tools/L86_strip.py`, twelve lines: it drops every line matching `^\s*(General lines that could (produce|reach) .*|Reached without the reason:.*)$` and every line that follows one of those and is indented deeper than it until the indentation returns.

## Not tested

## Traps
- Reading the block as a finding. It names what was examined; the verdict stands as before.
- Letting the block's goal queries change the verdict: the goal asks are made after the verdict is fixed and add nothing to `report` but the block.
