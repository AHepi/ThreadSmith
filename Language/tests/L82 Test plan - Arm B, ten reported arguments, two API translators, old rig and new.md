# L82 Test plan - Arm B, ten reported arguments, two API translators, old rig and new

DRAFT until the premise check returns; then frozen by this file's SHA-256, recorded in the log, and not edited. Written under Lessons L10, L11 and L12, decision L11 (the API agents), the regime of review A4 (L76) and the calibration L81. Arm A held the translator constant and showed the second driver does what it says on the ledgers in hand. Arm B varies the translator and the text: ten new passages nobody in the loop wrote, each a report of someone else's argument, translated by two outside models that have never seen a ledger of these texts, run on the old driver and the new, with a reader given the reports alone.

## Who does what, and what each is blind to
- **Text writer**: a Sonnet agent, from the brief in `tests/L82 Arm B corpus brief/`; it wrote ten passages in four matched pairs and two controls and a map of which is which; the map is sealed (`MAP.enc`) and the orchestrator has not read it. The pairs are visible to anyone who reads the texts (each pair shares its opening sentence), so the seal checks the writer's intent at marking, not the marker's blindness; every expectation below is stated by class and by observable features of the texts, never by the map.
- **Translators**: Atria (`Atria-Dawn-Preview`, 30 requests a minute) and Mimo (`mimo-v2.6-pro`, 100), each called once per text through `tools/translate_via_api.py`, which sends 39, L80, the encoding guide with three examples, and the text; a ledger that fails validation is sent back once with the failure. Neither sees the other's work, the plan, the map, or any earlier ledger of these texts (there are none).
- **Runner**: the orchestrator, by script: both drivers on every ledger, `consequences_2.py` on every ledger, `sameness_2.py` within each pair per translator and across translators per text; every output kept.
- **Reader**: Atria, one fresh call per report through the reader brief in the brief folder, on all forty reports (20 ledgers, old and new) in a shuffled order under neutral names; it never sees a passage or a ledger.
- **Markers**: the orchestrator against this table; then a fresh Opus agent given this plan, the outputs and the opened map, and nothing else; both markings kept.
- **Premise checker**: a fresh Opus agent, before the freeze.

## The texts, by observable features (the map is not consulted)
The corpus is `results/L82 Corpus - ten reported arguments/`, B01 to B10, hashes in P1. Read from the texts alone: B01 and B08 share their opening and B01 adds a rule ("always left that gate open only once ..."); B03 and B06 share theirs and B06 adds a rule ("always crosses the bar early once ..."); B04 and B09 share theirs and B04 adds a route ("since a bed near the desk let ..."); B05 and B10 share theirs and differ in what is denied (B05 denies the same cause it claimed, the bus route; B10 denies a different one, the entrance hall); B02 and B07 contain no because, since, so that or always. Call the passage of a pair with the added rule, route or different cause its **S-side** (B01, B06, B04, B10) and the other its **F-side** (B08, B03, B09, B05); B02 and B07 are the **controls**. In every passage the argument is a reported person's, inside a note, log, minutes or handover: under 39's attribution rule both translators are expected to put it in a told world.

## What is scored and what is only recorded
By L81: differences of wording, line form, standing where the text does not settle it, and Thing lines are recorded from the cross-translator sameness runs and not scored. What is scored is the report kind inside the world, the variation within pairs, the controls, the reader's trace.

## Near-miss cases that must not fire
The four S-sides (B2), the two controls (B4), and every old-driver report (B8).

## Not tested
The world half of the split; rig 2; a text whose argument is the narrator's own (Arm A's ledgers cover that); a reader other than Atria; the outside-line count at a nonzero value (no passage has a general line outside its world); which translation is right.

## Traps
- Reading the map before marking. It is opened after both markings of B1 to B9 are drafted, and used only to confirm the writer's intent matched the observable features.
- Scoring wording differences. L81 section 3.
- Reading B7's misses as the language failing. They charge 39's room for choice; B1 and B2 are where the language and the driver are charged.
