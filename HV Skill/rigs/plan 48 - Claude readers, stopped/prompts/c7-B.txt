They describe a way of judging an explanation. Use it on the case below.

The case. Someone offers this explanation:

Why do people become depressed, and why do the drugs work? Mood is carried by serotonin signalling between nerve cells. In depression the signal is too weak: too little serotonin is released, or it is cleared from the gap too quickly. Drugs that block the clearing leave more serotonin in the gap, the signal strengthens, and mood lifts over a few weeks. That is why the drugs help, why they take weeks (the receptors adjust), and why removing serotonin's building block from the diet lowers mood in people who have recovered. Remove the block and the drug does nothing. Block a different transmitter and the effect is smaller. So depression is a serotonin deficit, and the drug corrects it.

Reply in everyday words, in under 400 words, in this shape. (1) The parts of the explanation that are claimed to do the work. (2) For each part, one sentence: what holds it in place, or what could take its place just as well. (3) The weakest part, and one change to the situation that would show whether it does any work. (4) Your verdict, one of: RELY ON IT / RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION, and say which / SET IT ASIDE.
