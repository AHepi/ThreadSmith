They describe a way of judging an explanation. Use it on the case below.

The case. Someone offers this explanation:

Stars at the edge of a galaxy orbit as fast as stars near the middle, though the visible matter thins out toward the edge. If gravity is Newton's, the mass holding them must extend far beyond the light: a halo of matter that does not shine, several times the visible mass. The same halos explain how galaxies cluster, how light bends around clusters, and the pattern of ripples in the background glow, which needs matter that does not feel light pressure. In the Bullet Cluster two clusters collided. The hot gas, which is most of the ordinary matter, was slowed and left in the middle, while the mass that bends light went straight through with the galaxies. So the unseen mass is not the gas, and it moves as particles that do not collide would. There is a particle, then, weakly interacting and heavy enough to clump. Remove the halo and the rotation curves fall. Make the particle interact strongly and it stays with the gas. Make it light and fast and the small structures never form.

Reply in everyday words, in under 400 words, in this shape. (1) The parts of the explanation that are claimed to do the work. (2) For each part, one sentence: what holds it in place, or what could take its place just as well. (3) The weakest part, and one change to the situation that would show whether it does any work. (4) Your verdict, one of: RELY ON IT / RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION, and say which / SET IT ASIDE.
