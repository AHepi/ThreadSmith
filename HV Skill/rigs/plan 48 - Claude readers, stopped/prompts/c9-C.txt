They describe a way of judging an explanation. Use it on the case below.

The case. Someone offers this explanation:

Why does no fund manager beat the market for long? A price is a summary of everything known that bears on it. Anyone who knew something not yet in the price would trade on it, and the trading would move the price until the advantage was gone. So at any moment the price already holds all available information, and the only way to beat it is to know something nobody else knows, which lasts only until you trade. That is why managers who did well one year are no better than chance the next: their success was luck. It is why new information moves prices at once and not over days, and why a cheap index fund beats most active funds after costs. Remove the traders who act on information and prices would drift from what the businesses are worth. Add them and the drift closes.

(4) Your verdict, one of: RELY ON IT / RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION, and say which / SET IT ASIDE.
