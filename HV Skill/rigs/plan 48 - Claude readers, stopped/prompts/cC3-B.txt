They describe a way of judging an explanation. Use it on the case below.

The case. Someone offers this explanation:

Why do the finches on the dry island have deep beaks and those on the wet island slender ones? Birds vary in beak shape, and the shape is inherited. On the dry island the seeds are large and hard. A deeper beak cracks them, so deep-beaked birds eat more, live longer and leave more young, and the population's beaks deepen. Some say this is a circle: whoever survives is called the fittest. It is not. The account says in advance, from the seeds, which beak will spread, and that can be checked before any survivor is counted. In the drought of 1977 the finches on the large-seed island were predicted to deepen, and did; when small seeds returned, the shift reversed. Remove the inheritance and the shift does not carry to the next generation. Change the seeds and the direction changes.

Reply in everyday words, in under 400 words, in this shape. (1) The parts of the explanation that are claimed to do the work. (2) For each part, one sentence: what holds it in place, or what could take its place just as well. (3) The weakest part, and one change to the situation that would show whether it does any work. (4) Your verdict, one of: RELY ON IT / RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION, and say which / SET IT ASIDE.
