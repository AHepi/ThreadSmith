The case. Someone offers this explanation:

Why are there exactly the particles we see, feeling exactly the forces they feel, and why does gravity fit with the rest? Every particle is one vibration of a single kind of string, the way one violin string gives a whole scale. Different notes are different particles, and the graviton is one of the notes, so gravity is not bolted on but falls out of the same object as everything else. The strings need extra spatial dimensions, curled up too small to see, and the shape of the curling fixes which notes exist. That is why the particle table is not arbitrary: it is set by geometry. Remove the extra dimensions and the notes do not close into a consistent set. Change the curling and you change the particle table. The theory has no free constants of its own; every number in physics is to be read off from the geometry. That one framework yields both the graviton and the other forces, which no other candidate does, is the reason to believe the world is made of strings.
