They describe a way of judging an explanation. Use it on the case below.

The case. Someone offers this explanation:

Why did serious crime in the city fall by half in the nineties? Small disorder is a signal. A broken window left unrepaired says that no one is watching and no one cares. People who would otherwise hold back read the signal and act, and the people who would restrain them stay indoors. So disorder invites serious crime through a chain of signals. Fix the small things, repair the windows, remove the graffiti, arrest the fare-dodgers, and the signal is reversed. Serious crime falls, because the people most likely to commit it were the ones reading the signal. The city did this and crime fell, and the fall began in the districts where the policy was applied first. Remove the policing of small disorder and crime should rise again.

Reply in everyday words, in under 400 words, in this shape. (1) The parts of the explanation that are claimed to do the work. (2) For each part, one sentence: what holds it in place, or what could take its place just as well. (3) The weakest part, and one change to the situation that would show whether it does any work. (4) Your verdict, one of: RELY ON IT / RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION, and say which / SET IT ASIDE.
