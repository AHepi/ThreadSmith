**1. The parts claimed to do the work**

- A. Gravity is Newton's, out at the edges of galaxies and beyond.
- B. A halo of unseen mass, several times the visible, spread out past the light.
- C. The unseen mass is not the hot gas.
- D. It moves like a swarm of things that pass through each other without colliding.
- E. It is a particle, weakly interacting, heavy and slow enough to clump.

**2. What holds each in place, or what could take its place**

- A: Borrowed, not tested here. A changed law of gravity does the rotation-curve job just as well; what stops that swap is the Bullet Cluster (mass sitting away from the gas) and the ripple pattern, and they hold A only so long as no changed law can be tuned to fit them too.
- B: Held by the rotation curves and the light-bending together: remove the halo and both fail. "Several times" is loose, since a nearby number does as well; what is held is "much more than the light shows".
- C: Held tight by the Bullet Cluster: the mass that bends light is where the galaxies are, not where the gas stopped.
- D: Held by the same collision: had the unseen mass dragged like gas, it would have stayed in the middle. But "never collides" and "collides a little" both pass, so the line is coarse.
- E: Split it. "Heavy and slow enough to clump" is held by the small structures forming. "A particle" and "weakly interacting" are not: a swarm of early black holes, or any cold, dark, collisionless stuff, does every job listed just as well.

**3. The weakest part**

"There is a particle, weakly interacting." Nothing in the story tells it apart from other cold, dark, collisionless stuff. Change to try: put a large block of ordinary matter in its path, deep underground, and watch for nudges. A weakly interacting particle should now and then knock an atom; something that only pulls by gravity never will, and every sky observation above stays exactly the same either way. If the block stays silent for years across the whole range of masses that could clump, that part was doing no work in this explanation.

**4. Verdict**

RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION: that most of the mass in galaxies and clusters is unseen, cold, and passes through collisions without dragging, given Newton's gravity. Not for "it is a weakly interacting particle": that part is loose here and waits on a test of its own.
