**1. The parts claimed to do the work**

- A. Small disorder is a signal that no one is watching.
- B. Would-be offenders read the signal and act; the people who would restrain them stay indoors.
- C. Fixing small things reverses the signal.
- D. Serious crime fell because the likely offenders were the ones reading the signal.
- E. The fall began in the districts where the policy came first.
- F. Take the policy away and crime should rise again.

**2. What holds each in place, or what could take its place**

- A. Loose: "arresting fare-dodgers put more police on the street and caught people carrying weapons or warrants" fits every fact given, with no signal at all.
- B. Loose: nothing in the story shows anyone reading anything; a rival where the same arrests simply took the same people off the street does the job as well.
- C. Held only if A is held; on its own it is A said again.
- D. This is the conclusion restated as a starting point ("the ones most likely to commit it were the ones reading the signal"); it explains nothing.
- E. Held, and it is the one part that does real work; but it holds "the policy did something in those districts", not "signals did it". It also does not yet cover the cities that had no such policy and fell too, or whether the fall had started before.
- F. Fine as a promise, but untested, and the police-presence rival predicts exactly the same.

Flip: had crime risen, "disorder spread, the signal got louder" would have been told just as smoothly. The story covers both outcomes.

**3. The weakest part, and one change that would test it**

The signal chain (A and B). The change: in some districts, repair windows and remove graffiti with no extra police and no arrests; in others, add the arrests with no cleanup. The signal story says the cleanup-only districts should see serious crime fall. The presence-or-arrests rival says they should not. Until that is run, the two stories are one story at this level.

**4. Verdict**

RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION: "Did the district-by-district policy go along with, and likely contribute to, the local fall?" The timing in E supports that much, once the no-policy cities are accounted for. As an answer to "why did crime fall by half" via a chain of signals, set the chain aside: it is a label until the cleanup-without-police test is run.
