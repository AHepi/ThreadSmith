**(1) The parts claimed to do the work**

- A field with stored energy that made space swell enormously in the first instant.
- Because of that swelling, patches now on opposite sides of the sky were once neighbours, so they matched temperatures. That gives the uniformity.
- The swelling ironed out any curvature. That gives the flatness.
- The swelling also stretched tiny quantum jitters in the field to galaxy size, giving the ripples in the background glow, with a slight lean toward big scales that was predicted before it was seen.

**(2) What holds each in place, or could stand in for it**

- The field: nobody has seen it directly; it is inferred from the jobs it does, and a bounce before the bang, or simply starting the universe smooth and flat, would cover the first two jobs too.
- Neighbours-then-matching: the arithmetic works if the swelling was big enough, but the field itself has to start out smooth over the patch that swells, so some of the uniformity is assumed rather than earned.
- Ironing out curvature: solid geometry, any large enough stretch does it, but the amount of stretch is chosen to fit, not predicted.
- Jitters to ripples with a tilt: this is the one that stuck its neck out. The tilt was called in advance and found, the ripples are random in the expected way, and rival seeds like cosmic defects were ruled out by the pattern of peaks. Still, different versions of the field give different tilts, so the family is loose even if one member fits.

**(3) The weakest part, and a test**

The field itself. Its energy, when it starts, when it stops, and how smooth it begins are all dialled in to get the answer. One change that would show whether it is working: the simplest versions of the field also shake spacetime and leave a faint swirl pattern in the polarisation of the background glow, at a level tied to the field's energy. Find that swirl at the predicted strength and the field is earning its keep. Keep not finding it as the searches sharpen, and the simple versions are dead and the story has to be retuned again.

**(4) Verdict**

RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION. Rely on it for "where did the ripples come from and why do they have that spectrum and that tilt": there it made a risky call and won. Do not rely on it as the reason the universe had to be uniform and flat, because there it mostly restates what it was built to produce, and its rivals are not dead on those two points.
