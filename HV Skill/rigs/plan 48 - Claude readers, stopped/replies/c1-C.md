**(1) The parts claimed to do the work**

- A. One kind of string; each particle is one of its notes.
- B. The graviton is one of those notes, so gravity comes from the same object.
- C. Extra dimensions, curled up too small to see, are needed for the notes to form a consistent set.
- D. The shape of the curling fixes which particles exist.
- E. No free constants; every number is read off the geometry.
- F. No other candidate gives gravity and the other forces together.

**(2) What holds each in place, or what could replace it**

- A. Held only as "one stretched object with many notes": swap the string for a small sheet or loop and the story reads the same, so "string" is looser than it sounds.
- B. The tightest part: the sums say a closed string cannot avoid having a massless note that pulls like gravity, and removing it removes gravity; but that is held by borrowed mathematics, not by anything we have poked.
- C. Held by the same sums, but "curled up too small to see" could be swapped for "we live on a thin slice of a bigger space" with no loss, so the hiding is loose.
- D. Nothing holds it yet: there are vast numbers of allowed shapes and none has been shown to give our table. Flip the outcome, four families of particles instead of three, and the answer is "a different shape." A part that fits any table is a catch-all with no gauge.
- E. The freedom did not vanish; it moved from the constants to the choice of shape. As stated this part is idle.
- F. A count of rivals is not a job. Being alone in the race says nothing about whether the runner is going the right way.

**(3) The weakest part, and one change that would test it**

Part D. Name one shape before looking, read off one number that was not used to pick it, say the ratio of two known particle masses, and check it. Then switch to a neighbouring shape and confirm the number moves. If no shape can be named, or every shape can be tuned to match, D does no work and the "not arbitrary" claim falls with it.

**(4) Verdict**

RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION: "can gravity and the other forces come out of one object without contradiction?" Parts A, B and C answer that. For the question actually asked, why exactly these particles with exactly these forces, the parts that would do the work (D, E) are not yet held by anything, and F is not a test at all.
