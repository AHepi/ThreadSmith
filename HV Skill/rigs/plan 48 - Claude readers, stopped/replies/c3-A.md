**(1) The parts claimed to do the work**

- A. Gravity works the way Newton (and Einstein) say, even at the far edges of galaxies.
- B. Extra unseen mass, in a wide halo, keeps the outer stars moving fast.
- C. The same unseen mass explains clustering, light-bending, and the background ripples.
- D. In the Bullet Cluster, the mass that bends light passed through while the gas got stuck, so the unseen stuff is not gas and does not bump into itself.
- E. Therefore it is a particle: heavy, slow, and weakly interacting.

**(2) What holds each in place, or what could stand in for it**

- A. Held loosely: gravity is tested well in the solar system, but the galaxy edges are exactly where a modified gravity rule (extra pull at very weak accelerations) could stand in and fit rotation curves just as well.
- B. Held in place only if A holds; drop A and the halo is not needed for rotation curves at all.
- C. Held firmly: the background ripples and cluster lensing are hard for modified gravity to match without adding unseen mass anyway, so this part does real work on its own.
- D. Held firmly: the offset between gas and lensing mass is a direct picture, and modified-gravity accounts have to strain to reproduce it.
- E. Held only halfway: "weakly interacting and heavy" is what is left after ruling out gas, light particles, and strongly colliding stuff, but it is a description of a gap, not a thing anyone has caught. Massive black holes, or a particle far lighter than assumed (fuzzy, wave-like), could fill the same slot in most of the evidence.

**(3) The weakest part and a test**

Part E is weakest. It is an inference from absence, and decades of detectors have found nothing. One change that would show whether it does work: take a galaxy with almost no visible gas or dust and measure whether its outer stars still speed. A halo-of-particles story says yes, as long as the halo formed; a story where the effect rides on ordinary matter would say the effect shrinks. Better still, find a galaxy with no halo at all: if its stars orbit slowly as Newton predicts from light alone, the halo is a real ingredient that can be present or absent, not a fixed feature of gravity.

**(4) Verdict**

RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION: rely on it for "there is unseen mass that gravitates, clumps, and does not stick to gas" (parts B through D). Do not yet rely on it for "it is a heavy, weakly interacting particle" (part E); that is the best-guess label, not something the evidence has pinned down.
