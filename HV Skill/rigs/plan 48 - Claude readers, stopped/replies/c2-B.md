**(1) The parts claimed to do the work**

- A. A field with energy stored in it, early on.
- B. A stretch of space by a factor of ten to the twenty-six or more.
- C. Opposite parts of the sky were once in contact, so they reached one temperature (uniformity).
- D. Stretching flattens curvature, like a balloon (flatness).
- E. Tiny jitters in the field were stretched to galaxy size, giving ripples nearly the same at every scale, slightly tilted toward the large; the tilt was predicted first and then found.

**(2) What holds each in place, or what could replace it**

- A. Only the ripple job (E) needs a field; for uniformity and flatness any big early stretch would do, so on those two jobs "a field" could be swapped for any other cause of stretching, and the explanation admits it needs its own starting patch, which is the very thing it says it removes.
- B. The lower end is held by uniformity (the whole sky had to fit in one patch); the "or more" means the size is free upward, so the number is only half held.
- C. Held by the uniformity job, but borrowed from ordinary heat flow; the plain rival "it simply started uniform" does this job equally, and only E tells them apart.
- D. Held by the flatness job and borrowed from geometry; same rival as C, and the balloon picture works for any stretch, so it holds nothing about the field.
- E. This is the part that does the real holding: it is the added job the "started that way" rival cannot do, and the prediction came before the reading. But the field's shape can be tuned to give a tilt in either direction, so what is held is "ripples nearly the same at every scale", not the tilt.

**(3) The weakest part, and one change that would show whether it works**

Part A, the field itself, is close to a label: many fields fit the three jobs, and the same three jobs are done by "a big stretch of unknown cause". Change to test it: the field predicts a second kind of ripple, in gravity waves, with a size tied to its energy and to the tilt. Look for that imprint in the background glow's polarisation. Found at the predicted level, the field is doing work beyond "stretch happened". Absent down to where the simplest fields must show it, the field was decoration and only the stretch is held.

**(4) Verdict**

RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION: that some early stretch of at least this size happened and seeded the ripples. Set aside, for now, the claims that a stored-energy field in particular did it and that it removes the need for starting conditions.
