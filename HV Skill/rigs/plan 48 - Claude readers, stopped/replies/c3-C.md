**(1) The parts claimed to do the work**

- A. Gravity is Newton's, even at the edges of galaxies.
- B. A halo of unseen mass, several times the visible, reaching past the light.
- C. The unseen mass is not the hot gas.
- D. It passes through itself without colliding.
- E. It does not feel light pressure.
- F. It is heavy and slow, so it clumps into small lumps.
- G. It is a weakly interacting particle.

**(2) What holds each in place, or what could replace it**

- A: Borrowed and held. Swap in a changed law of gravity and the rotation curves still come out; what stops the swap is the collision, where light bends around a place with almost no ordinary matter, so a changed law has nothing there to act on.
- B: Held by the rotation curves, given A; "several times" is a loose number, and what is really held is "most of the mass is dark".
- C: Held by the collision: gas left in the middle, bending mass gone on with the galaxies.
- D: Held by the collision, but only as a ceiling: a little self-collision would pass just as well.
- E: Held by the ripple pattern.
- F: Held by the small structures forming, but only as a floor on how slow it must be.
- G: Loose. Swap "weakly interacting particle" for a swarm of small black holes, or a cold wave-like field, and every job above is done just as well; "weakly interacting" adds nothing the jobs ask for beyond "does not shine or scatter light".

Two whole-story checks. Flip: had the curves fallen, or the bending mass stayed with the gas, B or D would have had to be different, so the story sticks its neck out. Direction: the halo is read backwards from the curves, but it then does jobs it was never fitted on (bending, clustering, ripples, the collision), which is what keeps it from being a mere name for the missing pull.

**(3) The weakest part**

G. Every change on the list is a change in where mass sits, and any dark, cold, non-colliding stuff sits the same way. One change that reaches inside: look at the centres of the smallest galaxies. A heavy particle predicts dense, peaked centres; a wave-like field predicts smooth cores; black holes would show as brief brightenings of background stars. If "particle" fits whatever is found, it does no work.

**(4) Verdict**

RELY ON IT FOR A NARROWER QUESTION: that most of the mass in galaxies and clusters is unseen, is not the gas, does not shine or scatter light, passes through itself, and is cold enough to clump, with gravity unchanged at these scales. Set aside "it is a weakly interacting particle" until a change separates it from its neighbours.
