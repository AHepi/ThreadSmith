## 1. Case text (case book)

## O2 - The tide table with a real bell attached

**Situation.** Carla explains the harbour's tides with two parts. Part one is the printed almanac of high and low water for every day. Part two is a true local mechanism: when the float in the harbour gauge rises past a mark, it tips a lever and the harbour bell rings. She offers both together as her account of why the water is high at noon today. Take the bell mechanism away and what her account establishes does change, since it no longer says the bell will ring. The bell mechanism is physically real and independently grounded.

**Thoughtful person's verdict.** Carla has explained the bell. She has explained nothing about the tide. The almanac is the answer written down.
