## 1. Case text (case book)

## O3 - Nadia and the cards she has never seen

**Situation.** Nadia learns a card game only by watching cards being laid on the table. She is never shown a hidden hand and nobody tells her hands exist. For weeks she predicts the next card from the cards just played, and is often surprised. One evening she starts keeping a tally of cards that have yet to appear, and treats them as "waiting somewhere". Her predictions improve sharply, and she can now say why a player with few cards left must play a certain suit.

**Thoughtful person's verdict.** Nadia built something new: a part that stands for cards she has never observed. That is construction, and it is her own.
