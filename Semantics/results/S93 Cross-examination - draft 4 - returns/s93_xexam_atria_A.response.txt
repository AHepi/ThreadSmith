**1. X09 · (c2) and T3 — "established" does not settle whether a candidate's own structural failure counts, and the problems machinery needs it to.**

X09 makes `fits` load-bearing: X10 (line 317, which X09's `fits` feeds) says two rivals that both fit pose a problem, and a problem stands "until what is established leaves at most one of them fitting." X09 defines: "A result is **established** for an assessor who holds a usable receipt for it (Part IX)" and "A candidate **fits** … when no result established for that assessor shows it failing a condition of (E)."

But (E) has conditions a candidate can fail without looking at the world: non-circular dependence (line 255 — deleting block G must change the answer, evaluated on E's own boundary conditions), non-vacuity (line 257), and (F1) where a component is anchored to nothing (line 233 with line 103: an empty anchor imposes the full relation, so a component not imposing it fails (F1) from the candidate's own data). Whether such a failure is *established* is not settled. Part IX, which X09 points to, says "An evidence leaf is a reference to an event with an interpreted claim. A receipt is a derivation tree over leaves" (line 397), and (K3) (line 395) speaks of "the test that yields it" — both point at empirical tests. Nothing says a verification the assessor holds is a receipt.

Attacks I made and where they land: the word "result" (line 315) is unrestricted, and (K2) (line 389) makes usability turn on live premises, which a verification over definitions satisfies — so the framework is *capacious* enough to admit verifications. But capacious is not settled, and a notion that gates problems should not rest on an unstated reading. What turns on it: an assessor who has caught a circularity cannot be described as holding an established result against the candidate, so the candidate still "fits" and can pose or share a problem it should already be out of; and conversely, if verifications do count, the theory must say so. This is a gap in X09's own definitions, and it should be closed.

Proposed repair — insert after the `fits` sentence in line 315, before "No list of all rivals is supposed":

```text
A verification the assessor holds is a receipt for what it verifies. A failure of non-circular dependence, a contradiction inside the candidate, or a component anchored to nothing, is found by examining the candidate itself and not the world; the verification is an argument application whose premises are the definitions and the adopted physics it relies on, usable while those premises are live (K2), and telling against the candidate only together with them (K3). A candidate with such a failure established against it therefore does not fit, whether or not anything is established about the world.
```

This adds no record or log doing the explanation's work: the failure is in the candidate's own content, and the receipt is the assessor's evidence state, as with line 369's "with no record of which candidates failed before."
