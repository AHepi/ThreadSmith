# Report

**1. X17 · T1 — (EK) is placed on (P) but not on the obligations it quantifies over.**

The new wording (L526) gives (EK) the base "(G), (E), Deploy" plus "(P)". But (EK) is not Repair and nothing else. L446–L450:

> \(\operatorname{CreateEK}(s,\Delta,h,e)\iff\operatorname{CreativeCriticalEpisode}\land\operatorname{Repair}_{O,P}(\xi,\xi';\Delta)\land\exists o\in O_{\mathrm{ep}}\,\exists c,p_c,e_c\,[e_c\preceq_h e\land\neg o(\xi)\land o(\xi')\land\operatorname{Origin}\land\operatorname{Account}(c,p_c)\land c\in\operatorname{Result}(\Delta)\land\operatorname{Deploy}\land\operatorname{ProducesVia}(\Delta,c,o;\xi,\xi')]\)

The third line tests \(\neg o(\xi)\land o(\xi')\) over \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}\subseteq O\) (L443: "With \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}\subseteq O\) the epistemic obligations"). That is a *direct* use of the declared obligations and of the occasions they cover — the very phrase the entry now adds for (P). It also uses \(\operatorname{ProducesVia}\) (L453), which is not \(\operatorname{ProducedBy}\).

The item's own standard is set by its reason ("erratum") and by what it adds: the old entry "(P), (EK) depend on (G), (E), Deploy" was wrong because (P), L438, is defined with \(\exists o\in O[\neg o(\xi)\land o(\xi')]\), \(\forall r\in P\), and \(\operatorname{ProducedBy}\) as conjuncts. So a condition's declared inputs and named predicates must appear in the order. Under that standard "(EK) also on (P)" is false read as an exhaustive entry.

The dependence is not mediated by (P). Let \(O=\{o_1,o_2\}\), with \(o_1(\xi)\) false, \(o_2(\xi)\) true, both true at \(\xi'\), a protected \(r\) holding on every covered occasion, and \(\Delta\) such that \(\operatorname{ProducedBy}(\Delta,\xi,\xi';O)\) holds. \(\operatorname{Repair}_{O,P}\) holds either way. With \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}=\{o_1\}\), (EK) can hold; with \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}=\{o_2\}\), its \(\exists o\in O_{\mathrm{ep}}\) conjunct fails. (P) is untouched. So (EK) rests on the obligations and their occasions over and above (P).

Proposed new wording:

```
Ownership depends on histories and a declared boundary, and owned capability on Ownership, (CT1) and a declared continuity (Part XII). Build depends on histories, Ownership and (E). (N), (G) depend on Deploy and Build. (P), (EK) depend on (G), (E), Deploy; (P) also on ProducedBy and on the declared obligations with their occasions, (EK) also on (P), on the declared obligations with their occasions through \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}\), and on ProducesVia, and ProducedBy on histories and their active routes, and ProducesVia on those and on the binding of \(c\).
```

With the declaration extended to match:

```
Part XIV's dependence order now places Ownership on histories and a declared boundary, owned capability on Ownership, (CT1) and a declared continuity, Build on Ownership, (P) on ProducedBy and the declared obligations with their occasions, (EK) on (P), on the declared obligations with their occasions through \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}\), and on ProducesVia, and ProducedBy on histories and their active routes, and ProducesVia on those and on the binding of \(c\).
```

Unpressed: \(\operatorname{Result}\) and \(\operatorname{CreativeCriticalEpisode}\) for (EK), \(\operatorname{Attempt}\) for (G), the restriction operation for (S), \(\mathcal V\) for (D) — coarseness the order has always had about data and non-input predicates. Also note \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}\) itself appears in no list of declared inputs (L522 names only \(O\) and \(P\) with their occasions).

**2. X17 · T3 (directed) — the order and Part VI's new terms (L315, L317).**

Their bases: \(\operatorname{conflict}\) rests on (F1)–(A) and "relations of the target that the adopted physics admits (Part I)", i.e. \(\Theta\); **established** rests on "a usable receipt for it (Part IX)" and (K3); **fits** rests on established and (E); **rivals** on conflict and acts of offering; **problems** on rivals and fits.

(i) The order *stays true*. No entry is contradicted; nothing in Part VI feeds back into (O), (Q), (K), (F1)–(F2), (A) or (E); the closing clause survives, since the new notions rest on (E), \(\Theta\) and Part IX receipts, not on themselves, and Part IX's own guard is intact (L397: "A record reconstructed from the claim it is meant to support is not a receipt for that claim"). Nor does anything now depend on a predicate meaning "really explains": L315 defines fitting by absence of an established *failure of a condition of (E)*.

(ii) But the order does not *place* them, and it never placed (K2), (K3) or receipts either — although L598 rests on the order "which lists the declared indices and the declared inputs with the definitions that rest on them". With Part VI's new definitions in the text, the list is visibly not that enumeration. If it is meant to be one, it needs: "Rivals, conflict, problems and easy to vary rest on (E), on the relations the adopted physics admits, and on acts of offering; established and fits rest on receipts, (K2), (K3) and (E)." This is the same class of gap my point 1 presses at (EK).

**6. X16 · T1, T2.** Attacks made and failed. (i) *Could it be WORDING?* No: "declared" is a term of art (L155 "A **declared** contract is stipulated by the modeller"; L199 "the transport is entered into the model by its author"; L522 "Declared inputs. Besides the two primitives…"; L524). The old wording classified a *primitive* as declared, against L515–522, and let a reader take \(\mathcal N\) as a stipulation of the modeller, like \(\operatorname{Dec}(t)\) (L211: "it makes a modeller assert"). That conclusion is now withdrawn. (ii) *ORDER?* No: "taken as a substantive input" does restate L31/L518, but the item *removes* a claim, and ORDER covers no removal. (iv) *Does "taken" drop the duty to state the input?* No: L455's own first clause — "as an input and marks the place" — and L522 ("where the input is missing, the verdict is unsettled") carry it. T1: the new wording is true of the theory's terms (L518: "It is taken as an input and never derived").
