## (d) Section-4 items not varied, and why

- **D16.5 (Classes)** — pure extensions; FC110 (not tested) records that no membership is asserted, so varying them cannot move explanation's definition, only labels.
- **E9 (the two-layer episode)** — the owner's worked case; its t1/t1∘ψ pair is used here as a trace instrument (V4.4). Varying it would vary the case, not the semantics. The natural variant — admitting an edit that displaces only one thing, breaking ψ[C] = C — is left to Part B; FC103.new1 (c) (0 separating pairs) is its baseline.
- **D18.1 (dependence graph)** — the map itself; it is what the other variants are traced on (V4.1 moves an edge in it as an effect). Varying it first would erase the instrument.
- **D18.2 (bijections) / Argument 8** — a constraint every variant must respect rather than a variable; note V4.1 is safe (pin at one pair is carried along by φ), V4.3 is not obviously φ-invariant (Sel/CT are history facts, and D18.2 carries occurrences), which is a reason it was not pushed further.
- **(Elim) and (QF) shapes in D16.XV** — their Work, "fails to capture" and "not creative" are undefined by record (D0.2, r4); formalizing them would be new content, not a variation of a stated condition.

## (e) Inventions the variants rest on

- **V4.1**: Slot's quantifier reading (the four readings of D6.3, registered with FC23.new2). Other choices: "some", "some-exempt", "some-exempt-set". The trace is reading-independent (ℰ_one is a slot under all four).
- **V4.3**: provenance is per-holding and possibly absent (D12.3/D12.4, I90 as the program has it forces Dec/Con/Sel). Other choice: provenance total on every transport — V4.3 then changes nothing.
- **V4.4**: I196, the two readings of "not using (E)". Other choice: the symbol reading (current, r4).
- **V4.5**: preservability read at contracts C′ other than the question's own (D5.5 applied off-question). Other choice: only declared contracts of the question count as C′ — then the current (Nec) is closer to the variant than to its own wording.
- **V4.6**: I75/I183, 𝔈_Θ's membership condition. Other choice: require δ = the designation for *every* Acc-witness (stronger than either the current or the varied form).
- **V4.7**: I154, NQB's shape ("relayed from outside β" read at the realization). Other choice: NQB read at χ itself; both are deleted by V4.7, so the choice is idle here.
- **V4.8**: reading (Prov)(i)'s middle conjunct through D12.9 rather than as spelled inline in D16.XV. Other choice: keep it inline — then V4.8 changes Argument 3's wording only and (Prov)(i) stays closed.
