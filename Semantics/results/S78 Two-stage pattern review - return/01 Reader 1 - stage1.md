## The question, frozen

Asked: (1) internally, does each part do what its instruction says, as the S72 returns show; (2) as a test, can it fail a wrong theory and pass a right one, and is there a route by which a defect is reported rather than absorbed into a reading, a PARKED file or a held-back list; (3) as an explanation of "why this method finds what is wrong", is each part held by a job, so that swapping it for a near neighbour breaks something. The question is frozen on the pattern, not on whether file 10 is true.

## Overall
**Verdict:** makes sense with repairs

The pattern is a real test and it did real work: the audit moved O24 off the CHANGED list and O52 off its original pair, its 119-quotation check is honest and mark-by-mark checkable, and its fix card is the best-built part in the project — it runs the proposed change on the case that forced it (O48) and on two cases it must leave alone (O24, O11), and adds a new attack case with both halves of a poke. I hand-checked three rows against file 10 and the verdicts follow from the quoted sentences (O20's claim that ProducedBy is unexpanded is exactly right: the word occurs once in file 10, at line 441, undefined). But the two stages are not blind to the answer, and that is not an accident of this run, it is written into the instructions. Stage 1's brief names the rows it expects to find ("O24 and O48 against Derivation 3") and the run returned those two and nothing else. Stage 2's Part 1 orders it to print Stage 1's verdict first and its own reading second, and Stage 2's return says in its own words that it had seen Stage 1's answer before forming its expectation. S76 goes further: the prediction is inside the agent's brief, file 10's column-3 answers are handed over to be quoted, and the pack now carries the S72 audit return, which states the sampling rule row by row — so the next Stage 1 can read which eleven rows will be checked. Two structural holes complete the picture: a defect with no amendment to fix it has no home in the deliverable, because heading (11) admits only lines carrying "the Part 1 row that shows the earlier version's reading changing there"; and the table format drops the three devices file 24 uses to keep a verdict honest (expectation written first, condition by condition, best reply on the theory's behalf and whether the difference survives), leaving a mandatory defence of the theory and no mandatory attack on an agreement. Forty of fifty-two column-6 entries read "No disagreement". The repairs are small and the project already has the right wording in its own files: S76 Stage 2 Part 2 says "Make your own list ... then compare it with Stage 1's list", which is exactly what Part 1 needs.
