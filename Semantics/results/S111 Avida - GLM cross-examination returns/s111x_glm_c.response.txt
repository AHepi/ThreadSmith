## (b) Checked, no objection

C1/C2 (knockout logic, the 15/85 split, essential-site list against `default-heads.org`; `nop-X` flags-0 null as described); K1 (conservative: the test CPU's time cap could only hide slow copiers, and the in-world half needs no cap; death at update 66 ≈ 20×100 instructions at 30 cycles/update is internally consistent); K2; K4's attribution (under `DEATH_METHOD 0` nothing but overwriting removes, so the knocked-out organism's disappearance is the copier lineage's offspring; departure 7's correction is right); K5 as the no-change baseline (copy, divide-ins and divide-del all set to 0; other mutation channels are 0 by default); the `align()` implementation (Needleman–Wunsch with linear gaps, correct recurrences); the 27th instruction elsewhere (robustness scans use only the 25 real alternatives; K1's injected genomes are a–z); the low-beside-high rule as planned and its verdicts; the events files against the saves and counts used; the results-file numbers against the four summary tables (robustness means and ranges at all three saves; conservation "54 of 54"; tasks-per-run counts and the 10% selection; EQU 1.1–4.4%, 19–26 sites, 93–100% still copying; NOT 21%/46%); "5 to 44 of about 3,600" (consistent with the observed drops); S23/S43/S28/S21 compliance in both S111 files (Avida's `num_breed_true`/"breed true" and quoted source strings excepted).
