## The three that matter most

1. **Avida is not closed to outside data.** Our plain file 123 said "as it stands Avida cannot learn from outside data at all"; in fact stock Avida's `SetEnvironmentInputs` event puts any three tagged numbers into every program's inputs, so a file of measured data can be fed in with no new code (confirmed by run). What stock Avida lacks is a way to *score* a forecast of such data fairly, not a way to let the data in. (Row 3.)
2. **"Nothing constructs, so nothing explains" does not follow, and a selected program's reach is not "exactly" its history.** The semantics counts a selected correspondence that meets (E) as an explanation; Argument 3 says what a history leaves *open*, not that the program is wrong there; our own numbers (34 of 2,370 programs still doing NOT with two inputs swapped) contradict "exactly". (Rows 16, 17, 18.)
3. **Reading A does not make an evolved program's correspondence "selected" on its own.** D12.1 asks for a survival condition requiring fidelity on the history, and in Avida programs last without doing any task; our own S117 recorded this (unit B4), but the reader's guide and the assessment dropped it. (Row 15.)
