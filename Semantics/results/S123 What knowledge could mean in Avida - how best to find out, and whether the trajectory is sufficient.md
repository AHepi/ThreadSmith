## 0. In short

1. **The trajectory is not sufficient** to tell what knowledge could mean in Avida. It has measured one meaning well: information that causes its own copying, resists change and remains (S59), with Marletto's elimination signature (S111), and has said what that information is (a small circuit of Avida's own operations, a circuit only relative to Avida's instruction meanings, S112), how it arises (variation and selection, S113), and where its reach ends (exactly where its history ends: S116; S117, C3). It has not tested five things the question needs (section 3): (i) S76's clause, an effect on Avida itself, which no run was designed to test; (ii) a separation of adaptive information from explanatory knowledge: no set-up was built in which the second could appear, so its absence is by construction, not a finding; (iii) whether the execution environment, the selector, holds knowledge, rather than only the programs; (iv) learning from external data: no number any program has read came from outside Avida's own code; (v) since S118 every selector has been scored by how many capabilities became common, a measure that none of the candidate meanings of knowledge names. That last is where the trajectory has drifted.
2. **Is Avida the right environment?** For the meaning it already instantiates (the owner's three properties; Marletto's knowledge; Deutsch's non-explanatory knowledge), yes. For a learning machine fed constant external input, not as it stands: every number a program reads comes from Avida's own pseudorandom generator, three per cell in one fixed order (`cEnvironment::SetupInputs`, `main/cEnvironment.cc` 1252-1294; S116), or from a rule a designer wrote (S119 reply 01's streams; S121 reply 02's frames). It can be given outside data cheaply (a patch on the model of S119 reply 01 reading a recorded file; a driver between pieces; events that set inputs, resources or pay; a state grid loaded from a file); it cannot be given a second view of what the data are about, a world of lasting objects, or learning carried within a program's life and passed on, without being rebuilt into a different experiment (section 4). Other platforms are named in section 4 only as options with their costs.
3. **Which transports learning from external data needs to begin** (section 5), in the semantics' sense (L181-189, D5.1): a data transport from the world's process to the system's input states, faithful step by step (functional form, L353, FC64); an output transport from the system's states to outputs or actions that change the world (closing a loop); a model transport from the system's states to a content about the world, which is representation only if faithful and selected or constructed (L205-213, D12.5) and which makes prediction possible only if its codomain is a simulation layer over persistent things (L171-179, L217-221, D12.6, D12.7); a selector transport by which the selector reads its material against a standard (L195, D12.1's survival condition); and, on Pinker's account, a second channel to the same events and a fixed prior link from the learner's categories to the data (S124, T2 and T3). **Avida now has** the output transport (one channel, `IO`, checked by code), the selector transport (the checking code: declared on S117's Reading B, the world's rules on Reading A), and a data transport whose source is Avida's own code. **The patches add** a designer's rule as the data source (S119 reply 01) and order in time (S121 reply 02). **Missing**: a data source the selector does not contain, a loop through which outputs change the data, a model whose codomain is about anything outside Avida, a layer of lasting objects, a second channel, and any within-lifetime change.
4. **Six rival hypotheses** (section 6), each with the observation that would count against it: H1, the knowledge is the programs' circuits and is knowledge of Avida's own rules only; H2, the knowledge is held by the coupled system, selector and population, not by the programs alone; H3, information with the three properties is not yet knowledge until it acts on the execution environment beyond channels written for that effect; H4, Avida's knowledge is adaptive and non-explanatory, and nothing in Avida can be more because nothing in it constructs; H5, learning about anything begins only when a data channel from outside the selector is opened; H6, knowledge in a creative agent's sense begins with construction within one program's lifetime, on selected material.
5. **Four briefs** (section 7), each asking a different question that splits a different pair of rivals: 01 the transports (H5 against H1), 02 whether Avida is the right environment (H5 and H6 against Avida as it stands), 03 where the knowledge is and whether it acts on Avida itself (H1, H2, H3), 04 adaptation against explanation, and whether anything in Avida could be constructed (H4 against H6). If only one is run: 01.

## 2. What the trajectory S110 to S122 has established about each, and what it has not

**K-CT and K-O3.**

- *Established.* Avida programs show all three properties, each in a measured sense with two halves: the program's own instructions are a necessary part, the execution environment's rules the other (S111). Copying: 15 of the ancestor's 100 instructions are required; none of 10,000 random programs copied itself. Resisting change: 69 to 79 in 100 one-instruction variants of an evolved program still copy; the copying instructions are conserved across the population far more than the rest. Remaining: the line of descent remained in every run; no program identical to the ancestor did. Marletto's test on EQU picks out about 19 to 26 instructions per program, separate from the copying instructions; switched off in every program where they can be, EQU falls to 1 to 4 in 100 programs (S111, section 6).
- *What the information is* (S112): a small fixed circuit of Avida's own operations, from the input channel to the output channel; one instruction suffices to stop a task in 98 in 100 program-task pairs; under a changed meaning of `nand` the same sequences do other tasks (97 in 100 NAND programs do NOR). So it is information **of Avida's rules**: in K-CT's "of" place sits Avida's instruction meanings and the checking code.
- *Not established.* (1) Whether "causes itself" asks for more than "a necessary part" (S111; the GLM point that the information itself does no resisting stays the owner's). (2) S76's clause. The record (S76's answer) shows effects only through channels Avida's designers wrote: copies written into Avida's memory; task performance changing processor time; S113's COMMON TASKS PAY LESS, where performance drew down the execution environment's stores; S113's growing list, changed by a runner; S122 reply 01's selector, whose next pay depends on what the population did, through a driver people wrote (S122 plan, section 5). No run was designed to test the effect on Avida itself, and what "Avida itself" means has two readings (section 3).

**K-D.**

- *Established, consistently with Deutsch's non-explanatory kind.* Every new capability came by variation and selection (S113; S117, B6). Capabilities are hard to vary in the S112 sense (one instruction stops them). Their reach ends at the history: populations that do NOT in the world's input order split at orders the world never gives (16 of 18 populations; growing-list seed 2: 34 of 2,370 still do NOT with two inputs swapped; S116, S117 C3). That is Deutsch's "rarely have much reach beyond the situations in which they evolved" and the semantics' Argument 3 (L570-576) at once.
- *Not established.* Anything about explanatory knowledge. No set-up has contained what the semantics asks for it (a represented target in a history, D12.2; a recognized difficulty, L429) or what Deutsch asks (a conjecture that reaches unmet cases). Its absence is a consequence of what was built, not a test that could have come out otherwise.

**K-P.**

- *Established*: nothing directly; S124 brought the account in after the runs. Indirectly: every Avida program studied is replaced, not taught: its processor restarts at each division (S122 reply 02 check, rows 2 and 4), so nothing is learned within a lifetime. The nearest is S122 reply 02's pilot: in the paid interval run, 14 of the 50 most common sequences (71 programs) keep where the A came within a frame, earning more pairs than counting reads can. That is state carried across inputs, selected, not learning from data.
- *Not established*: any representation of anything outside Avida; any within-lifetime learning. Outside the project, a modified Avida evolved associative learning when "environmental patterns that vary across generations but remain consistent for periods within an organism's lifetime" were present **[checked: Pontes2020, abstract via PubMed]**; not reproduced here.

**K-Sem.**

- *Established* (S117): the structural half lines up (an Avida program is an organization; tasks are declared questions; kinds come apart under finer changes; Argument 3 computed on 16 of 18 populations). Whether an evolved program represents its task flips on S117's open reading: under Reading A (the checking code belongs to the world's rules) its transport is selected and, as one block, it passes (E) and represents its task on the numbers the world hands in; under Reading B (the checking code is an earlier occurrence representing the task) it is declared and represents nothing (S117, B1; MC1). Nothing in Avida constructs (D12.2: "nothing in Avida"); nothing predicts (no simulation layer, D12.6), so nothing is surprised (B7). S120: under S119 reply 01's patch, programs learned to hand back the next number of a "plus one" stream (95 in 100 programs by the end; at most 3 in 100 unpaid): the first selected transport whose codomain is a forecast of the next input, though over numbers, not persistent things. S122 reply 02: the first programs earning pay by telling the same events apart by order in time.
- *Not established*: any instance of Con, of (G), of (EX), of a recognized difficulty or of surprise in the semantics' sense; and nothing about the semantics' explanatory half, which the trajectory has not touched anywhere (S117: "every unit of that half either has no counterpart or fails a condition on the nearest one").

**The selector (S63, S72).** Established: the execution environment does all the choosing (S113, S117); a growing list chosen by the runner made most capabilities common (48 to 65 of 77) and every paid count levelled off (S113, S116); "any function counts, common ones pay less" gave 12 to 21 common functions, all easy (S118, grade 2); copy only after solving set a floor, not a drive (S118); selectors with memory (S119 reply 02; S121 reply 01) worked as written but made no clear difference at the pay tried (S120, S122); no grade 3 mechanism was found by any reply or source check (S120 reply 03; S122 reply 03). Not established: whether the selector holds knowledge in any of the meanings above.

## 3. Is the trajectory sufficient? What is missing, and where it drifted

**Not sufficient.** It is sufficient to say that Avida instantiates K-CT and K-O3's three properties, and Deutsch's non-explanatory kind, with numbers. It is not sufficient to say what knowledge could mean in Avida, because the rivals of section 6 make different predictions about things no run has measured.

**What is missing.**

1. **A test of the knowledge's effect on Avida itself (S76).** Two readings, for the owner, not chosen: (a) "Avida itself" is its rules (the C++ and the configuration): no program can change them in stock Avida, by construction, so the clause could only be met by a patch that lets program outputs edit the configuration, which would itself be a channel written for that effect; (b) it is the execution environment's state, including what the selector pays and requires: programs already change resources, occupancy and (under S113's growing list and S122 reply 01) the pay schedule. A test is then possible, and it needs a scale like the grades of naming. Proposed, not applied: **E1**, the code names which capability changes which part of the execution environment (the growing list: "when a top-level task is common, add the next level"); **E2**, the code provides a generic channel through which any capability affects a shared part (resource stores; cell occupancy; a selector reading the shares of all 77 detectors); **E3**, an effect on what the execution environment selects for, arising through no channel written to carry that effect. The elimination test can be turned outward: remove the circuit everywhere (as S111 did) and measure the execution environment's own later state, against a control in which the same pay is replayed blind (S122 reply 01's replay arm). Brief 03.
2. **A test separating adaptive information from explanatory knowledge.** Deutsch's line (p. 73; pp. 105-106) and the semantics' Argument 3 give it a form: a capability selected on a history H, tested on cases outside H, with the population's other survivors as the control: reach beyond what the population fixes would count against "adaptive only". The construction side: can anything in an Avida history meet D12.2 (a represented target in the episode that prepares the transport)? Under Reading B, the only represented targets in Avida's histories are the people's. Brief 04.
3. **A test of where the knowledge sits: programs, selector, or both.** S72 makes the execution environment the agent. On K-CT, what has to be eliminated to stop a reliable transformation can include the selector's rule or state (S113: the growing rule made 48 to 65 common, all 77 at once 13 to 41). Transplant tests answer it: an evolved population moved into a stock selector, and a selector's state moved onto a fresh ancestor population. Brief 03.
4. **Learning from external data (S78).** Not one input in the trajectory came from outside Avida's code. Brief 01 (transports), brief 02 (environment).
5. **A measure tied to a meaning of knowledge.** Since S118 every selector was judged by the count of common capabilities, which measures variety of adaptation (K-D's non-explanatory kind, at most). None of K-CT's test, K-O3's clauses, K-D's reach, K-P's held-out accuracy or K-Sem's provenance was the outcome measure of S118 to S122.

**Where it drifted, candidly.**

- From S57's question (when does the amorphous thing *in a creative agent* constitute knowledge) the work moved to Avida (S59), which contains no creative agent in the semantics' sense (nothing constructs), then to the selector's instinct (S71, S72), then to temporal selectors (S75). Each step was the owner's and each was answered; but the link back to "what would count as knowledge if this succeeded" was not restated after S112, so S118 to S122 succeeded or failed on grades and counts, not on any candidate meaning.
- The semantics offers Avida a place it has not been tested in: "A physical system may hold selected transports at the object layer and constructed transports at the simulation layer" (L201). Avida could at most be the selected lower layer of a creative agent, never the whole; the briefs ask Astra to say what would have to sit above it.
- Avida was inherited round after round as the testbed without the question S78 asked. S124 supplies one reason to ask it (Gold's result, Pinker's Input condition), and it is now asked (brief 02).

## 4. Is Avida the right environment? (S78, part i)

**What external input Avida supplies now (stock, as run in S111 to S118).** Read in the source at 47f13dad:

- **Three numbers per cell**, set by `cEnvironment::SetupInputs` (`main/cEnvironment.cc` 1252-1294) whenever a program is placed in a cell (`main/cPopulation.cc` 1365) or, if `RESET_INPUTS_ON_DIVIDE` is set (default 0, `main/cAvidaConfig.h` 382), when its parent divides (`main/cPopulation.cc` 916). Each is drawn from Avida's own pseudorandom generator, with the top byte fixed at 0F, 33 and 55; `IO` outputs a register and reads the next of the three, in rotation (`cpu/cHardwareCPU.cc` 4188-4201). So: a fixed set, always in one order (S116).
- **Specific inputs** set by an event (`SetEnvironmentInputs`, `actions/EnvironmentActions.cc` 999-1039; the three must begin 0F, 33, 55), optionally with a random mask (`SetEnvironmentRandomMask`).
- **Resources** with inflow, outflow, periodic and seasonal schedules and gradients, set in the environment file or by events (`SetResourceInflow`, `SetPeriodicResourceInflow`, `SetSeasonalResource`, `SetGradientResource` and others, registered in `actions/EnvironmentActions.cc`); read by programs only in instruction sets that include `sense` or `collect` (`cpu/cHardwareCPU.cc` 241-265), not the heads set used so far.
- **Pay changed during a run** by events (`SetReactionValue`, `SetReactionValueMult`), and by a driver between pieces (S113, S122).
- **Other programs**: messages (`send-msg`, `retrieve-msg`, `cpu/cHardwareCPU.cc` 583-584), shared resources, occupancy.
- **State grids**: a grid of states loaded from the environment file (`GRID` line, `LoadStateGrid`, `main/cEnvironment.cc` 1041, 1198), moved through with `sg-move`, `sg-rotate-l`, `sg-rotate-r`, `sg-sense` (`cpu/cHardwareCPU.cc` 330-333), with a path-traversal task (`main/cTaskLib.cc` 314, 3446).

**What this amounts to.** Nothing that carries information about anything outside Avida's own rules: every value is produced by Avida's code from its seed, or written beforehand by the person who set up the run. On Pinker's account, the Input and Equipotentiality conditions ask that the regularity to be learned not be built into the learner **[checked: P1979, pp. 218-219]**; mapped onto S72 (the execution environment as the learner, the programs as its hypotheses; S124, 3(a), Claude's reading), the execution environment has been checking its hypotheses against itself.

**What it could be given.**

- A **recorded external stream**, read by a patch modelled on S119 reply 01 (`ANTICIPATE_MODE`: a private stream per program, compared with the next number to be read; reproduced exactly in S120), with the stream read from a file of data not written for the purpose (a recorded physical signal, a text, a series from another process). Cheap: reply 01's patch is five files; a file reader is a few dozen lines (an estimate, not checked).
- The same through **events**: a script that turns a data file into `SetEnvironmentInputs` or resource-inflow events, one per update (stock; but only three inputs, with fixed top bytes, shared by the whole world).
- A **driver between pieces** that sets pay from outside data (S113, S122 runners); coarse (one decision per 1,000 updates, S122 reply 03 check, row 5).
- **Other programs as a source the selector does not contain**: pay for anticipating another program's outputs (messages); the regularity is then produced by evolving material, not by the selector's code (S120's "not tested" list).
- A **state grid built from data** (a map), giving positions and movement: the nearest stock Avida comes to an object layer.
- **Within-lifetime learning**, as in the modified Avida of Pontes2020 (patterns varying between generations, holding within a life) **[checked: abstract]**; that code was not examined.

**What it cannot be given without becoming something else.** (i) A **second channel** to the same events (S124, T2): Avida programs receive numbers with no context; adding a paired "meaning" stream is possible in code, but what makes Pinker's second channel work is that the learner can tell what the signal is about **[checked: P1995 §6.5; DP2023]**, which needs a world of things and events, not of numbers: that is building a different world inside Avida. (ii) **Live continuous input**: Avida runs in updates, not in time; any outside process must be serialised into a file or a driver's pieces. (iii) **Learning that is kept and passed on**: an Avida program passes on its instruction sequence only; its processor state is reset at division (S122 reply 02 check, row 2); anything learned within a life is lost at each copy unless the code is changed so that learned state is inherited, which turns digital evolution into a Lamarckian system (Deutsch, ch. 15 "The Evolution of Culture", p. 376, calls the idea that modified behaviour is inherited a form of Lamarckism; paraphrased). (iv) **Rules that programs can change**: Avida's C++ is fixed during a run.

## 5. Which transports learning from external data needs even to begin (S78, part ii)

**The semantics' terms.** A transport from an organization D to an organization E is t = (π, τ, σ, λ): π carries valuations, τ edits, σ boundaries, λ assigns each component of E a subnetwork of D with a port translation (L183-189; D5.1, core L186). It is faithful on a contract C when (F1) and (F2) hold (L233-245; D5.4, D5.5, D5.7). Its **functional form**: if π∘S_a = T_a∘π for every admitted generator a, the same holds for every finite composition (L353; FC64): the represented process tracks the target process step by step. Its **relational form**: a relation R that preserves transitions forward (T1, L355-359), with the backward condition for equivalence; relations **compose**, preserving both directions when the intermediate scopes agree (L361; FC65). An approximate transport accumulates error at a stated rate (T2, L363). A transport whose domain is physical has exactly one **provenance** (L191-199): **selected** (D12.1, core L454: a population 𝒯, a variation operator μ, a finite nonempty history H of pairs that actually occurred, survival requiring fidelity on H, and no earlier occurrence in its history representing t, H, the survival condition or its codomain); **constructed** (D12.2, core L458: prepared by a construction trace, with t or its codomain held as a represented target in the episode); **declared** (D12.3, core L462: neither; a relay keeps its source's provenance). **Representation** needs a faithful transport from the organization an occurrence instantiates to a content, with selected or constructed provenance (L205-213; D12.5). **Prediction** needs a transport to a simulation layer S over an object layer P of persistent things (L171-179; D12.6); a **violation** is a fidelity failure at an occurring pair; **surprise** is a violation of a selected transport at a pair outside its history (L215-223; D12.7), and needs H strictly smaller than C (Argument 4, L578-584).

**How they compose.** The system's correspondence with the world is the composite of L1 then L3 (data, then model): faithful only where both are faithful on agreeing scopes (FC65). In stock Avida the composite's domain is Avida's generator, so whatever a program comes to track, on either reading of S117, it tracks Avida's code. **For learning from external data to begin, at least L1 (from a source outside the selector), L3 (with H ⊊ C, so that unseen pairs exist) and L4 are needed; L2's loop and L5 make it more than fitting a stream; L6 and L7 decide what is said about the unseen.** Prediction and surprise in the semantics' strict sense need L3's codomain to be a simulation layer over persistent things, which no Avida set-up has had.

## 6. Rival hypotheses of what knowledge could mean in Avida

Each says what it predicts and what would count against it. They are rivals in the semantics' sense only where they conflict at some observation (L315); the pairs that conflict are named.

**H1. The circuit, of Avida's own rules.** The knowledge in an Avida run is the set of instructions the elimination test picks out for a reliably performed task, spread over the program population, and it is knowledge of Avida's instruction meanings and checking code, of nothing else (K-CT, with its "of" taken from S112). *Predicts*: eliminating it everywhere stops the task (shown, S111); under changed instruction meanings the same sequences do other things (shown, S112); off the history, correct answers are no more than the population fixes (Argument 3; shown for input order, S117 C3); a selector's state adds nothing that cannot be rebuilt from the population. *Counts against*: a capability right on unseen cases more often than the population's other survivors allow; or a capability whose loss and recovery depend on the selector's state rather than on the programs (then H2). *Conflicts with* H2 (where it sits), H3 (whether it is knowledge yet), H5 (whether an internal source suffices).

**H2. The coupled system.** Knowledge is held by the execution environment together with its program population: what must be eliminated to stop the reliable *production* of capabilities includes the selector's rule or state (S72's agent). *Predicts*: an evolved population moved under a stock selector acquires fewer new capabilities, or loses held ones faster, than with its own selector; a selector's state moved onto a fresh ancestor population speeds acquisition over the same selector wiped. *Counts against*: no difference between kept and wiped selector state in any transplant, at pay strong enough to make capabilities common (S122's pilot was a null at weak pay, which does not count). *Conflicts with* H1.

**H3. Not yet knowledge without an effect on the execution environment itself.** Information with the three properties is not knowledge in the owner's sense until it changes the execution environment beyond channels written for that effect (S76; E1 to E3 of section 3). In stock Avida only E1 and E2 effects exist. *Predicts*: eliminating the circuit everywhere changes the execution environment only through E1 or E2 channels; with pay replayed blind (the channel held fixed), no difference in the execution environment's later state. *Counts against*: an E3 effect: removing the circuit changes what the execution environment selects for later, through no channel written to carry that effect. *Conflicts with* H1 on whether S111's programs hold knowledge at all.

**H4. Adaptive, never explanatory, in Avida.** Avida's knowledge is Deutsch's non-explanatory kind: hard to vary on its function, reach limited to its history; nothing explanatory can arise because nothing constructs (no history in Avida holds a represented target, D12.2). *Predicts*: every capability's reach is fixed by its history and population; reach that appears beyond them is traceable to the instruction set or ancestor (L7), and changes when they change. *Counts against*: a selected program right on a class of unseen cases where its population held differing survivors; or any history in an Avida set-up meeting D12.2. *Conflicts with* H6.

**H5. Learning begins only with an outside source.** Avida programs and selectors hold knowledge only of Avida's own code, because every input is made by that code (L1's source); learning *about* anything begins only when a data transport from a source the selector does not contain is opened, and with that transport alone progress is slow and fixed by the candidate class (Gold, as Pinker uses it **[checked: P1979, pp. 225-232]**). *Predicts*: with a recorded outside stream, programs do better than chance on held-out stretches of it, and what they get right there depends on the instruction set (L7) more than on the length of the data; replacing the stream by Avida's generator with the same statistics changes what is tracked. *Counts against*: held-out performance no better than chance at pay that made S120's step common; or no difference between the outside stream and a matched internal one (then "outside" adds nothing in Avida). *Conflicts with* H1 on whether an internal source, such as other programs, already supplies what learning needs.

**H6. Construction within a lifetime is the threshold.** Knowledge in a creative agent's sense (S57) begins when a binding is built during one program's run against something it holds as a target ("Reconstruction by a learner is construction; relay is not", L405), on selected material (L201). Stock Avida never does this (processors reset at division; every correspondence selected or declared). *Predicts*: within-lifetime bindings appear only where patterns vary between generations and hold within a life (Pontes2020 **[checked: abstract]**); only those could meet D12.2. *Counts against*: within-lifetime learning that appears and whose binding meets only D12.1 (nothing held as a target in its own run), so learning does not cross the threshold; or D12.2 met in stock Avida. *Conflicts with* H4.

**How they bear on S117's open reading.** Under Reading A, H1's programs represent their tasks (selected) and, as single blocks, pass as explanations of them (S117, B1), which conflicts with H4's "never explanatory" unless the explanation the semantics grants is of Avida's own rule, which H1 and H4 then share. Under Reading B, every task-selected program is declared, and H1's "knowledge" has no representation at all. The briefs ask Astra to state its results under both readings; the reading itself stays the owner's.

## 8. Pinker against Deutsch and Marletto, where it bears

All from S124, marks kept.

- **Agreement that data alone do not make knowledge.** Pinker: a learner must bring prior constraints, "the data alone are insufficient" **[checked: P2004, pp. 949-950]**; Deutsch: knowledge consists of conjectured explanations, not extrapolations from sense data **[checked: Aeon2012]**. For Avida: an outside stream (L1) cannot by itself make knowledge on either account; what is said about the unseen comes from the candidate class (L7) on Pinker's account, and from conjecture on Deutsch's.
- **Agreement that inherited adaptations carry knowledge** (Deutsch, ch. 4, p. 105, quoted in section 1; Marletto's abstract catalyst; Pinker's selected faculties, **[checked: F1998; P2005, pp. 15-16]**). So K-CT and K-D's non-explanatory kind are common ground; Avida instantiates them.
- **Conflict on where the constraints come from**: Pinker, natural selection **[checked: P2004; Edge1997]**; Deutsch, creative conjecture with almost nothing built in **[checked: DP2023]**. In Avida terms: is L6/L7 selected (H6's "selected material") or is it what a creative layer replaces? Brief 04 asks.
- **Conflict on universality**: Pinker, intelligence is specialised and AGI incoherent **[checked: DP2023]**; Deutsch, universality guarantees some program meets the criteria **[checked: DP2023]**. The semantics abstains ("Recursion does not entail universality", L509). Not tested here; not asked of Astra beyond noting it.
- **Pinker's feedback point reverses Avida's**: Avida pays correct outputs and withholds pay from wrong ones, which is in effect negative evidence on every attempt (Gold's informant presentation **[checked: P1979, pp. 225-226]**; S124, 3(b), Claude's reading); a child gets no reliable negative evidence **[checked: P1995 §6.2]**. Avida's learning problem is not the child's; brief 01 asks for a positive-only version.
- **Unverified points not relied on**: HTMW's combinatorial representation, the Baldwin effect and the cognitive niche **[unverified]**.
