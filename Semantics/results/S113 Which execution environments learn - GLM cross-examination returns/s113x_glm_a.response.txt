## (b) Checked, no objection

Plan §0–§1 (the test matches S63/S64: same starting program, only the environment changed, capabilities counted over time; each environment's stated "answer" matches its configuration); plan §3 vs the runner and `runs/configuration/` (avida.cfg, instset-heads.cfg, default-heads.org copied unchanged from S111 into every piece; copy error 0.0075 and divide insert/delete 0.05 from S111's avida.cfg; world 60×60 toroidal; 50 pieces of 1,000; identical events files; Avida seed 1000·s+k as planned and said, including the seed sharing across environments); the six environment files against the plan's §4 table (values = levels; growing first piece = NOT and NAND at 1; equ_only = EQU at 5; common_pays_less resources: inflow 100, outflow 0.01, frac 0.0025, max 1.0, so a full resource pays exactly 2^level, and by the plan's stated Avida rule a task performed more than ~100 times per update draws the pool below 400 and pays less — the design makes a common task pay less); the growing rule's code (top level, ≥10% at the piece end, one level per piece, levels never removed) against the plan's rule, and its reading of "adds harder tasks as easier ones become common" as fair; value-0 rewards (2^0 = 1) leaving merit unchanged by construction; the equ_only ≡ no_rewards identity, stated and verified piece by piece; the ranking JSON against the plan's level table (2, 3, 7, 11, 12, 16, 12, 10, 3, 1 tasks; the nine two-input values equal S111's rewards; 3AB's 10 marked as a lower bound, and disclosed again as departure 4); expectations E1–E5, E7, E8 stated before running with "against" clauses and applied by the plan's three-seed rule in §8 (E4's "more" holds on both readings: 48>41 and 46>41); §12's remaining sentences (growing list's lead, keeping 73–100%, the counting fault's scope, the reuse caveat, "why is not shown here") match the tables and are hedged as the section's header requires;
