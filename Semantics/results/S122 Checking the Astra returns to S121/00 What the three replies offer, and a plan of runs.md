## 2. What was run here

- **Reply 01's own code**, unchanged, on stock Avida at 47f13dad: all its self-tests and tiny runs, identical to its pasted outputs (check 01, section 3).
- **The identical-present, different-history check** (`tools/s122_identical_present_different_history_check.py`, no Avida run): reply 01's selector fed the pilot's 19 real assays of run `full_2201` as they happened, or reversed, then the same present (piece 20). Its next pay differs by up to 0.29 doublings (NOT: 0.14 as it happened, 0.43 reversed), in 3 detectors; the reversed history opens 2 loss latches (capabilities that were common "earlier" are absent now). The memory-wiped selector gives the same pay after both, by construction. So reply 01's selector passes the minimum test; as check 04 says, this checks the plumbing, not a finding about Avida.
- **The pilot** of reply 01's temporal selector against its memory-wiped control (plan committed before running, 793c47b; continued after the restart, see section 4): 2 seeds × 2 arms × 20,000 updates from the task-free ancestor. All 80 pieces ran cleanly. Capabilities common in all six orders at 20,000: full 2 and 6, memory wiped 4 and 5 (seeds 2201, 2202); the easy ones first (NOT, NAND, ORN), a few three-input ones in seed 2202; no withheld capability ever common. Pay stayed at 18 doublings in total, between 0.117 and 0.341 for one detector; the two arms' pay to one detector never differed by more than 0.13 doublings. **No latch set in any run**, so latch, release and rebound were never tested. The one visible effect of memory is adaptation's own: in the full arm the first capabilities were held at lower shares (NOT in 6 to 31 of 100 programs over the last ten pieces, against 32 to 59). By the measures written before running: pay held, the count of capabilities held, the arms' counts within 2; but in seed 2202 the sets differ by 3 (which three-input capabilities arose), meeting the written mark against "alike": a lead, not a result, since once pay differs the runs follow different random paths. So at this pay the temporal selector acts like a mild "common pays less", and the parts that need a past did not come into play (check 01, section 6).
