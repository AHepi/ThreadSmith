## Method and scope

I checked every entry in Stage 1 file 05 labelled as earlier theory or recovered R2, including entries used only in its coverage file rather than its case table. A check is counted **per quotation identifier**, not per sentence, occurrence or distinct idea. Exact duplicate or overlapping passages under different identifiers still receive separate identifier checks.

For an earlier entry, the comparison is against the authority itself. For an R2 entry, the comparison is against the prior S62/S64 return that Stage 1 cites, not against Stage 1’s own repetition. VERBATIM means the quoted characters occur there unchanged after removal of Markdown block-quotation prefixes and ordinary file line-ending handling; punctuation, spelling, emphasis, formulas and wording were not normalized to obtain a match. A quoted excerpt can be verbatim without being a whole source section.

The three later-clause entries T-C01–T-C03 are checked separately as a supplementary provenance check. Additional primary and earlier-audit passages used in this Stage 2 return have their own A- identifiers and separate register. Neither group is counted as an R2 entry.

A verbatim R2 sentence can still presuppose a revised-only feature or fail to change a particular case. Text fidelity, authority applicability and argumentative effect are three different checks. In particular T-R27 is verbatim for both O24 and O48, although only O48’s claimed case change is confirmed.
