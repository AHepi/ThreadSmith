**S1 — the cut loop: well founded on finite histories, neither on ones the core admits.** D11.3 requires ≺_h *acyclic* only. Under the cut T′ the only Rep-in-Rep dependence is Sel's exclusion at o′ ≺_h o_t; on a finite (hence well-founded) history that is a well-founded recursion with a unique value — FC98 (c)/(d) and FC84.new1 (d) confirm (run: `--claim FC98 --claim FC32`, both "HOLDS ON ALL MODELS TRILS [sic] TRIED"; cycle (R)→Sel→(R) as worded; no cycle under K, T, T′). But D11.3 admits h with an infinite descending chain o_0 ≻ o_1 ≻ o_2 ≺ … below o_t. Witness (not run, infinite): all o_n held, Sel's other conditions met, no traces. Fixed points of R = {o : held ∧ (Sel ∨ Con)}: {o_1} and {o_2} are both fixed points (o_n ∈ R ⟺ no later o_m ∈ R), incomparable, and no least one exists. Rep, hence Sel, Con, Dec — and, since Q2, L17's Def condition — take different values at o_t. So: not a cycle, a well-founded recursion exactly where ≺_h is well founded below o_t, and *neither* on other admitted histories; L526's "no endless descent" fails on them. Fix: proposal P1.

**S2 — D18.1's "one edge set" is not the order the definitions have.** Three defects. (i) DEP has 28 nodes; D18.1 announces "Nodes: the symbols defined in §§1–16" (118 paragraphs). (S),(B),(D),(K3), Result, Episode, Scr, RC, UU, UC are absent, so FC98 (b)'s check "every sink is listed" sees only the subgraph's sinks. (ii) DEP["Sel"] = ["h","Θ",staged] omits Sel's own Faithful_H (→(F1),(F2)) and its ¬CT clause (→Build); no cycle is hidden (Build's cone does not reach Sel), but the entry is wrong. (iii) DEP["Build"] carries an edge to (E), matching L526 ("Build depends on histories, Ownership and (E)"), but D13.3's only route to (E) is ExplUse, which D0.2 declares a *primitive read through Θ*. Either the edge has no definition behind it or ExplUse is wrongly classed a primitive. Fix: P5, P7.

**S3 — one dependence L526 states and the core lacks.** L526: "(K3) on (K2)". DEP has no (K3) node; D9.9 depends on Forms_cl and MadeFrom as well. Same subgraph point as S2(i).
