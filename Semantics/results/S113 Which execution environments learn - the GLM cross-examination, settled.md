## 3. How the S114 restart audit bears

The audit (`results/S114 Restart audit - results.md`, e5802e6) measured runs in pieces against one continuous Avida process, for FIXED GRADED and COMMON TASKS PAY LESS, three seeds each. It was taken in as evidence; it was not rerun.

- **Xa7 (the S111 comparison)**: the audit is the proper check of the pieces for FIXED GRADED. Its numbers are readable as those of unbroken runs, within its thresholds and through the spread of three seeds. The S111 comparison only rules out gross differences.
- **Xa8 (what a reload loses)**: `results/S114 Checking GPT 6 Astra's reply.md` lists what a reload drops: processor state, the task record, the generation count, and merit averaged per distinct instruction sequence. The executed-instruction count and age (read here from `SetupInject`) are also reset. The audit's follow-up shows births and copy times after a reload are those of the unbroken run in both audited environments. So these resets did not change replication measurably there.
- **Xa4, Xb5 (resources)**: the audit found the carry exact at its 27 reloads, and this settlement found it exact at S113's own 147 reloads (1,323 values). The audit found the levels swinging for about 20 updates after each reload. The piece-end levels used for Xa4 are read 1,000 updates after the reload, outside the swing.
- **Xa5, Xb8, Xc3, E6 (COMMON TASKS PAY LESS against FIXED GRADED)**: the audit says COMMON TASKS PAY LESS is possibly lowered by the pieces. In all three seeds, below the thresholds, it had 5% to 10% fewer births and fewer common capabilities just after reloads. **So E6's "not separated" (17 / 9 / 8 against 7 / 8 / 8, a difference of 0 to 1 in seeds 2 and 3) and the after-the-fact per-program average are both to be read with caution; if the pieces lowered anything, it was COMMON TASKS PAY LESS.**
- **Xa3, Xb4 (the growing list)**: GROWING LIST was not audited; its rewards change only at the breaks, so the audit cannot separate it from them. This settlement shows only that the counting fault did not delay its additions; it does not show that the breaks left its course unchanged.
- **Xa1 and the levelling off**: FIXED LARGE LIST, the one run still rising, was not audited, and neither was GROWING LIST. For FIXED GRADED the levelling-off numbers are readable within the audit's thresholds. For COMMON TASKS PAY LESS, if anything, they are lowered.
- **Generations**: S113's results use no generation count, so the audit's warning (a reload resets them) changes nothing printed. The corrected results say they are not to be used.
- **S115 check 06** (a piece ending at label 1,000 runs 1,001 updates) is already departure 2 of the results; no objection turns on it. **S115 check 01** (a probe battery of about 10 s per saved population) bears only on Xd1: had every piece's population been saved, the test processor could have read all ten zero piece ends in minutes. They were not kept.
