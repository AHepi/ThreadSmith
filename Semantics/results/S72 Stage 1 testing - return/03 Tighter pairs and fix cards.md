### I72-T — The specimen and the inquiry
Written by: me

**What I expect.** Supplying a necessary specimen will count as the collector's contribution without transferring the expert's diagnostic interpretation or settling comparative credit for the whole inquiry.

**Aimed at.** I64's third sentence and the mistake of promoting a real contribution to every achievement merely because a work-related reason can be stated.

**The situation.** An expert compares three pump faults, devises a test and explains which metal fragment would distinguish them. Rani retrieves the fragment from a narrow drain that the expert cannot reach. The expert examines it and identifies the fault. Rani says the whole diagnosis was mostly hers because without her retrieval there would have been no specimen to examine.

**The thoughtful person's verdict.** Rani retrieved the specimen and made the examination possible. The expert devised and interpreted the test. The necessity of Rani's retrieval does not by itself establish that the whole diagnosis was mostly hers.

**Whether the earlier version already said this.** Earlier Part III, “The respect is the query,” states the question distinction [T-E03]:

> What a question asks — production, identification, obstruction, rule-status, purpose-achievement — is fixed by the type of \(\mathcal Q\) and the shape of \(C\), not by a label. A production question has a \(\mathcal Q\) that reads an output port and a \(C\) containing interventions on upstream ports. An identification question has a \(\mathcal Q\) that computes a fibre and a \(C\) containing edits to the observed value. An obstruction question has a \(\mathcal Q\) that returns a reachability truth value. Two questions with the same \(D\) and different \((C,\mathcal Q)\) are different questions, and an answer to one is not an answer to the other.

Earlier Part X, “Understanding, construction, and origin,” Construction, states the actual-history distinction [T-E04]:

> **Construction.** \(\operatorname{Build}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,h,e)\) holds when an actual subhistory owned by \(s\) and delimited at \(e\) prepares a represented organization for explanatory use of \(c\), contains a nontrivial binding construction relevant to that use, and is not a composition of content-preserving transfers. A construction witness identifies the controlled processes, the incoming carriers, the bindings constructed, and the resulting representation. Reconstruction by a learner is construction; relay is not.

These passages already permit different questions and contribution histories. I64 adds a concrete assisted-inquiry elaboration and a requirement to expose comparative reasons; it does not derive a ranking the earlier authority already supplied. Part 0 expressly leaves a ranking of thinkers unprovided. [T-E15]

The complete proposal below comes from S65, `02 Tighter pairs and fix cards.md`, heading “H65 — six-field fix card for the temporal scope of protection,” and is retained in S70. [T-C03]

> [yours] A protected obligation is assessed as the <u>same stated condition</u> over the <u>same stated occasions</u> in the before-and-after comparison; a claim extending protection across the intervening history must include that <u>temporal extent</u> in the condition.
>
> [yours] An interruption is a loss relative to that obligation exactly when it makes the condition fail on an <u>occasion the condition covers</u>; interruption and lack of use alone do not choose the protected condition.
>
> [yours] Where the case supplies no condition or scope <u>sufficient to settle</u> whether the interruption violates protection, leave that verdict unsettled while recording the interruption; a later choice of a <u>different condition</u> is a new comparison, not a vindication of the earlier one.

None of the new cases asks whether an intermediate interruption violated a stated protected service condition. In particular, O52's jam is failure of a proposed stopping route, not evidence of an unstated protection obligation. H65 is therefore inactive for these verdicts; its retention is not a new successful protection test.
