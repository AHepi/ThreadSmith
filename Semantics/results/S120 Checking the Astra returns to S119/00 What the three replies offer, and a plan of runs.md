## 2. The two pilots (reported as pilots, not as the experiments)

Plans and what would count against them were committed before running (`pilots - what would count, written before running.md`, 4bf8fef).

**Reply 01's anticipating execution environment, from the stock ancestor** (patched copy; 20,000 updates; R2 seeds 1 and 2, R1 seed 1; and, added after the first three had started, R2 seed 1 with no pay for 10,000 updates):
- In the world, matching passed 10 in 100 programs by update 600 (R2 seed 1), 800 (R2 seed 2) and 300 (R1), and stood at **95, 96 and 97 in 100 at 20,000**. Without pay it never passed 3 in 100.
- On the test CPU with fresh streams, R2 programs matched under R2 in 70 and 77 in 100 (93 and 95 among programs that can copy), under R1 in 30 and 32, under R0 in none; R1 programs matched under R2 in only 3 in 100. The most common R2 program in seed 2 contains `IO inc IO`.
- So **the pressure works and works fast**: an execution environment paying for "what comes next" made a program population that could not read or write find the step "add one" and keep it. It is one fixed short relation: a demonstration, not grade 3, and not growing difficulty.

**Reply 02's selectors, A and B against a fixed control** (stock; 20 pieces of 1,000 updates; one seed each): **no task became common in any arm in any piece**; at most 6 tasks present at once; A offered nothing at all in 5 of 19 pieces and never more than 0.03 of a doubling in total after piece 1. At this pay all three execution environments look like S113's NO TASK REWARDS. The reply's own assay agreed (0 common in every order). So the experiment the reply sizes would, as written, probably compare three execution environments that all do nothing.

**Departures.**

**What reply 04 offers the owner's question** (S63, S71, S72). It takes the brief item by item: eight things an agent's selecting has that the execution environment's selecting lacks (memory of what it selected, a model of the candidates, choosing the next test, holding a problem, criticism aimed at a part, expectation and surprise, rival candidates kept for one problem, findings that reach an actuator), each with what Avida has now, the smallest addition, its grade, a test with a control, and what would count against it. **What the check found.**
- **Source claims: 13 of 13 hold** (one with a narrowing: the counts that affect pay are the one program's counts in its current copy cycle, not a record of what the execution environment selected). Its eight "source_checks" outputs recomputed independently: **all eight agree**.
- **Something the reply missed:** stock Avida's live world can already run a program on its test processor before placing it (`REVERT_*`, `STERILIZE_*`; off in every project run), and injection uses the same measure. So the execution environment can look ahead at a program; it keeps no forecast and never compares one with what happens.
- **Its four corrections of S117** are aimed at the brief's summary sentences, not at S117's own lines; against the brief they are right, and none changes an S117 verdict. One note to S117 is proposed for the owner (not applied), and one reading is left open: whether a driver deciding between pieces counts as part of the execution environment (this bears on replies 02 and 04 and on S113's growing list).
- **The bounded option, run** (two saved programs from S113's GROWING LIST seed 2, all six orders; under 5 CPU-seconds): both programs hand out the same first six numbers, and the order-sensitive one still hands out NOT in every order. What fails under another order is **copying**, not NOT, so a pass/fail by task credit would read "fails NOT" where the program fails to copy. Programs that do NOT in all six orders are 23 of 3,489 in that population, and cheap (4 easy tasks against 46).
- **The combined design** is well formed (contract fixed before the run, removal arms, budget-matched controls, a stated null), with six flaws: the standing question aimed at the wrong capability; it may close early on the easiest task and then sit idle, with no rule for opening a new question; the 36 cells are one thin strip in a torus; the memory arm may change nothing; 12 seeds give only a coarse yes/no; and it runs through a driver (the open reading above).
- A note on reading the first flaw (added here, not in the check): the reply's contract says to report copying separately, to treat reaching the time limit as censored, and that "a timeout never closes it". A driver that follows this would record the four failing orders as "did not finish" rather than "fails NOT"; but then the question can close only on programs that also copy in all six orders, so as worded it asks about copying under a changed order as much as about NOT.

Reply 02's A pays nothing once nothing rises; reply 04's selector has the mirror risk of idling once its question closes.
