## The sentence and where it stands

L225 (whole line):

> Two responses to a violation are distinguished. A **selection response** extends the history \(H\) of a selected transport and lets \(\mu\) act: the transport is re-tuned within the population. A **construction response** introduces a new organization or a new transport with a construction trace. Only the second can be originative under Part X.

"The second" is the construction response. "Originative under Part X" points to (G), L421–L422: "\(\operatorname{Origin}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,p,h,e)\iff\operatorname{Attempt}(s,c,p,h,e)\land\operatorname{New}(s,c,h,e)\land\operatorname{Build}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,h,e)\)", and to the text's own uses of the word: L425, "the originative act is the finding of a question"; L588, "an episode whose originative contribution is a new contract meets (G)". The term "construction response" occurs nowhere in the text but L225, where it is defined as one of "Two responses to a violation"; a violation is defined at L220, "a **violation** occurs when fidelity fails at \((a,b)\)", for an edit–boundary pair "actually occurring" (L217).

What the sentence does: it ties the pair of Part IV to (G) of Part X. Of the two responses, the selection response cannot meet (G) and the construction response may. It is the forward half of L584: "The two responses, extend \(H\) and re-tune, or construct a new transport, are the difference between learning and creating".

## Is GLM's rival a rewording or a different claim?

A different claim, on the reading its own ground gives it.

1. **What the ordinal does.** "The second" presupposes an ordered pair, and "Only the second" means "of these two, this one and not the other". The comparison class of "Only" is the pair just defined; the sentence excludes the selection response and is silent on everything that is not a response to a violation. Mimo names this ("fixed by the two definitions above"), though without saying what it bounds.
2. **What the rival does to it.** "Only a construction response can be originative" gives no comparison class in its words. Taken on the defined term alone, as GLM asks it to be taken, it says: whatever can be originative under Part X is a construction response. Since a construction response is by definition a response to a violation (L225), and a violation is a fidelity failure at an actually occurring pair (L217, L220), the rival then makes a response to a violation necessary for anything originative under Part X.
3. **What that wider claim meets.**
   - (G) itself, L422: its conjuncts are Attempt, New and Build; none of them asks that a violation have occurred. The wider claim adds a condition (G) does not have.
   - L425 and Argument 5: "When it is a contract, the originative act is the finding of a question." (L425); "an episode whose originative contribution is a new contract meets (G)" (L588); "Finding a new question, by an owned construction (G), is a creative act, as answering one is." (L592). Nothing there asks for a violation, and the text's own case of question-finding in the case book, O12 (a team argues; Noor asks which tasks have no owner), holds none.
   - L405: "A first representation may be constructed from an available problem without prior observation of what it represents."
   - L315, conflict with a claim: "Such a conflict is found by argument, with no test against the target", and "a response that changes the candidate, the claim or the question is construction and repair (Parts X, XI)". A conflict found by argument is not a violation, which needs an actually occurring pair; a construction answering it can meet (G). The wider claim would deny that it can be originative. That also meets the owner's words of S27: "Explanations can contradict each other at the level of explanation without ever having to be tested against reality."
   - L317 and L429: a problem, or a conflict with a claim, "can be a recognized difficulty (Part X)", as a violation can (L223); and "A creative critical episode contains an instance of (G) connected to its inquiry." (L429), whatever kind of recognized difficulty opened it. The wider claim would narrow (G) to episodes opened by a violation.
4. **The other reading.** A reader who carries the pair over from the two definitions just before can read the rival narrowly, as "of the two responses, only the construction response". On that reading it says what the text's sentence says, changes no term the theory needs, and the sentence is easy to vary in the plain sense the brief asks about; that is recorded here. It is then not clearer: it trades a word that states the bound ("the second") for one that leaves the bound to context, and so opens the wider reading above, which the text's wording does not.

So GLM's argument that the rival "breaks nothing" holds only for the lines GLM checked, which cite the pair (L584) or cite nothing; it does not reach what "Only" comes to range over. Its claim that the rival does the work "at least as well" does not hold: the text's sentence states the bound, the rival does not.

## Is leaning on the order a fault in the sentence?

No fault is shown. The two definitions stand in the same line, immediately before, in the order selection then construction. The text keeps that order wherever it takes the pair up: L584, "extend \(H\) and re-tune, or construct a new transport"; L626, "**Selection:** … **Construction:** …". No reader showed a misreading of "the second". The dependence on order is local and costs nothing here.

A third wording was considered, one that names the term and keeps the bound: `Of the two, only the construction response can be originative under Part X.` It would be safe, and it would move no case. It is not taken: it restates in more words what "the second" already says on the same line, and no misreading of the present wording has been shown. Whether to prefer it is a choice for the person choosing (S21); on the reading rule's test it is not needed.

## The variations that change the claim, and what each meets

Each wording the readers tried is weighed on its own argument; none is a repair of a fault in the sentence, and none is taken.

- `Only the second is originative under Part X.` (Mimo, reply line 190; GLM, line 183). It drops the modality. A construction response can fail New, L416: "\(\operatorname{New}(s,c,h,e)\iff\neg\exists d\in R_{<e}(s,h),\ d\equiv_\ell c\)", as when it rebuilds what the system already held (GLM's point); it can fail Attempt; and its subhistory can fail Build by not being owned (L427: "Work supplied from outside that boundary … remains an outside contribution however it is executed inside"). The worked case keeps the condition: "If \(S_1\) was built by an owned subhistory … and \(S_1\) is not in the prior repertoire, then (G) is met." (L628). The break is real.
- `The second can be originative under Part X.` (Mimo, line 195). It contradicts nothing, strictly: L201, L405 and L584 stay as they are. What it gives up is the exclusion itself, which the text states elsewhere and ties to this pair only here: L13, "Creativity lives in construction."; L47, "Construction is a separate provenance with a separate trace, and every creative attribution requires it."; L411, "Construction is not selection." Mimo's "breaks" is more exactly a loss: without "Only", L225 no longer says which of its two responses cannot meet (G), and L584's "the difference between learning and creating" would stand without that tie at Part IV.
- `Only the second can be creative under Part X.` (Mimo, line 200). Part X uses "creative" of episodes, with a further condition: "A creative critical episode contains an instance of (G) connected to its inquiry." (L429). A response is not an episode, and "creative" would bring in the connection to inquiry, which (G) does not ask. "Originative" is the word the text ties to (G) directly (L425, L588). The variation says something else; it is not taken.
- `Only the second must be originative under Part X.` (Mimo, line 205). It says what must happen, against S21 ("Notice I never once claimed what must happen.") and L429 ("no argument makes the choice"), and against (G)'s conjuncts, which a construction response can fail (above). The break is real.
- `Only construction is creative.` (GLM, line 189). It has the modal break of the first variation, drops "under Part X", and puts "creative" for the term (G) carries; L592 ties the creative act to (G), "by an owned construction (G)". The break is real.
- `Only a construction response can be originative under Part X.` (GLM, line 195): weighed above.

## Failures of the sentence itself

None found. Sought:

- **Is "Only" sound?** Yes, by the text's definitions. Build (L405) asks for a subhistory that "contains a nontrivial binding construction relevant to that use, and is not a composition of content-preserving transfers". The selection response re-tunes a selected transport within its population (L225), and L411 says "Construction is not selection. A selected transport has no represented target in its history; a constructed one does."; L201, "Neither provenance is reducible to the other: a selected transport has no represented target and no criticism in its history; a constructed one has both." A selection response cannot meet Build, and so cannot meet (G). Two of the readers' grounds go past the text: GLM's "a selection history contains no representation at all" goes past L195, which says "No member of the history represents \(t\), \(H\), or the survival condition", not that the history holds no representation; and L626's "structural, not parametric" is said of the worked case's population, not of every selection response. The exclusion does not rest on either: it stands on L405, L411 and L201.
- **Is "can be" right?** Yes. The construction response is of a kind that may meet (G) and need not (see the first variation above). The modality is that of Part X's definitions, marked by "under Part X"; it is not physical possibility, so S25–S27 are not touched.
- **Mixed cases.** L201: "Construction may operate on selected material. Selection may continue to operate beneath construction." A response in which construction works on selected material is a construction response; selection going on beneath it is still selection. Nothing in the sentence denies either.
- **Vacuity.** Not idle: it is the one place that ties the pair of Part IV to (G), which L584 then names "the difference between learning and creating" and L626–L628 applies.
- **S20.** "Only" excludes one of two defined responses from (G); it does not list, count, grade or rank rivals.
- **S21, S28.** "Can be", not "must be"; nothing about what happens to anyone's candidates; nothing settled.
- **S23.** No word or idea S23 forbids.
- **S33–S34.** Nothing here bears on what hard to vary covers or on the placement of values.
