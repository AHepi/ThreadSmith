## O38 — Written on the job sheet
Written by: an outside author

**What I expect.** A protected condition expressly covering every moment will fail during the recorded four seconds, even when nobody draws water.

**Aimed at.** H row 83 beside row 82, the distinction between recording an incidental change and preserving a fixed protected condition; H65 is tested directly.

**The situation.** Before the repair, the job sheet states the protected condition: the kitchen tap runs at all times during the work. During the repair the tap stops for four seconds while the mains is switched over; nobody draws from it. The plumber's report lists the interruption as incidental.

**The thoughtful person’s verdict.** The condition was written down first, and it was broken, for four seconds. Calling it incidental changes nothing. Whether four seconds mattered to anyone is a different question from whether the condition held.

| Audit condition | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Component fidelity | The interrupted supply is a stipulated physical fact, but the case supplies no explanatory candidate or full match-up for the plumbing; that Account input is empty. |
| Question fidelity | The stated condition concerns running at all times during the work, so substituting nobody missing a use would answer a different question. |
| Non-circular dependence | The recorded stop defeats the universal temporal condition without taking the desired verdict as a premise; no independent mechanism explanation is supplied. |
| Non-vacuity | The described repair and interruption are possible within the case, but a complete explanatory change list is empty; the protection comparison itself has an explicit temporal scope. |

**The theory’s verdict under the earlier version with the proposals.** Under earlier XI with applicable R2 H, the S62 H clarification and H65, the claimed continuous protection fails. The repair may still have fixed another intended obligation, but it is not a repair preserving this stated protection. **Do they differ?** No. The four-second breach and its possible practical importance are different questions, and the theory can preserve both facts.

**Best reply on the theory’s behalf.** Earlier XI fixes the obligations for the comparison; it does not choose their temporal meaning. Here that meaning is supplied before the work.
