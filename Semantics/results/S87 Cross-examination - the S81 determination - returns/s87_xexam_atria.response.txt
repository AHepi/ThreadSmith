### O30 — The routine uploaded that morning

**1. The SILENT reading, tried from the boundary clause.** f11 L465 requires a boundary "declared before the attribution, not chosen after it," and f11 L514 makes the boundary a declared input. The case states no boundary; it says only that the routine was "uploaded to it" and runs inside the robot. On a strict reading the input is missing and the verdict is unsettled (1C A's reading).

**2. Why it fails.** f11 L465 defines the boundary as "which processes and resources are the system's." **3. **4. Why it fails.** The outside-work clause names products handed in for the task: a diagnosis, a decisive question, an instruction. An uploaded routine is not a product handed in; it is a process that runs inside and computes today's result. More decisively, the "whoever wrote it" clause must have application, and it applies precisely when a process was written by someone else. If writing a process outside made its run "work supplied from outside," the clause would have no case at all. So the comparison is the robot's own work today, and the authorship is separate — the fixed verdict's two halves, reached by the sentence's own two clauses. The routine reached the robot by relay, and "relay is not" construction (f11 L401 s3 = f10 L414 s3, extended).
