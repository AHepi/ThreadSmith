## A. V5 in the theory's terms

### A.1 The carried state: a representation of what, on which contract, with which admitted changes

- **What it is.** "a union of joint rectangle pairs tied to the current correspondence nodes" with "velocity conditional on each surviving supplied law" (VR L70, L7); "exact conditional velocity and joint position rectangles per surviving law, current visual cover, terminal correspondence nodes and a one-way input-lineage hash" (RM L90).
- **Of what (the content c, D11.2).** The set of world states (law, position of the controlled reference, position of the other reference, velocity) compatible with every public packet of this episode, **conditional on** the supplied family, rest at the episode start, a stationary other reference, the covers and the correspondence (RM L94, VR L45). It is the content "the world is in one of these states, if the family is right". By L211 a system "can represent a theory in error": the carrier-to-content transport faithful while the content-to-target transport fails. The attack is exactly that: "the private physical state is already outside the represented support from 17" (VR L190) while V5 agrees with the ledger (VR L161).
- **On which contract (C_c).** Forward questions only: forecasts of explicit force programs of 1 to 16 steps, bounded English endpoint relations, candidate authorization, with observation indices 0 to 31 (RM L80 to L88; VR L57). "The summary is sufficient only for the admitted future questions. It is not a general episodic record" (VR L240). Retrospective questions are outside it (RM L38).
- **Admitted changes (A, the edits).** Issued forces (the controlled reference's acceleration, `a = gain * force + bias`, VR L63) and the arrival of the next packet. **Not admitted**: a change of law, contact, object birth or death, re-identification, missing observations, noisy sensing, arbitrary initial velocity (VR L59; RM L94). The hidden kick and hidden stop are by design "adversarial changes outside the supplied dynamics" (VR L59): edits the contract does not admit, so the carried state's content cannot follow them, only be refuted by them.
- **Provenance, part by part (D12.4).** The format, the semantics of a rectangle, the recurrence, the covers and the correspondence entered β_M whole (RM L22, L42; VR L72, L76): handed in at β_M (I200), however exactly they are run inside. The per-law velocity is "worked out from the supplied recurrence" (VR L63) by the builders; at run time it is the answer of a handed-in organization evaluated on issued forces, which is use of an account (L255's Dependence: "the answer follows by evaluating E"), not construction of one. The one part the world decides is **which laws survive**: that is selected (A.3). So the state is best read as a **record (L211) of a selection's outcome, kept in a handed-in format**. Whether a strict reading of (R) lets the state itself *represent* at β_M turns on whether a record of selected survivors carries their selected provenance (L211 says a record carries its source's provenance), so: yes for the survivor set, no for the format.
- **Layers (D12.6).** V5 holds a **simulation layer** S (predictions under issued forces) over a **thin, handed-in object layer** P: "two persistent conditional references, one controlled reference, a stationary other reference" with correspondence supplied by V18, "not established general object identity" (RM L94; VR L72). Of P's admitted edits (displacement, occlusion, re-identification, L175), only displacement is in V5's contract. S128 found an S-layer with no P-layer in Astra's forecaster; V5 has a P-layer in name, two slots wide, supplied, with no occlusion and no re-identification.

### A.2 The family and the learned subset: a population of rival accounts

- **Rivals in D10.1's sense?** Each law is an explanatory candidate for the motion question: an organization (the recurrence) with components (gain, retention, bias, beta), a transport to the scene, and active commitments; deleting carried velocity changes the moving forecast (VR L119, L266), which is (E)'s Dependence shown for one component. Two surviving laws that predict different covers under some admitted force program **conflict at a pair of C**; neither is ruled out by anything V5 can use (its only test is fit to past packets). So the surviving set is a set of rivals, and where they conflict there is a **problem of the first kind** (D10.1) at β_M, with V5 read as the assessor by hand (Θ, as S134 3.4 read the bounded constructor).
- **Held by whom.** The family is held by V5 at every step ("The full 648-law bank is retained", VR L7); the problem is **held but never addressed**: V5's answers consult only the learned subset (RM L5), abstain when it is empty (RM L86), and have no step that acts to decide between survivors. The full-family rivals are used for one thing only: to tell "learned mismatch" from "full-family contradiction" (VR L74).
- **Where the family and the subset come from.** "a fixed supplied affine family" (RM L94); "Supplied finite family and inherited learned membership" (VR L268); the learned subset is "the preserved V2 left/mass-1 checkpoint" (RM L42) with "Unchanged base-model training evidence" as "a separate dependency" (RM L90). How the 648 were chosen, and how the two were fitted, is in V2, V21 and the Pinker project's sources, which we do not hold. At β_M both entered whole: **handed in**. At a boundary taking in V2's training, the learned subset may be selected (a fit by search) or, if fitted to supplied answers, constructed on the core's present reading (S128's warning; S131 item 3, the owner's open question); this file cannot say which.
- **Naming (S118).** The family is named by its writers as an enumerated class, **grade 2 for the family**; which member survives is fixed by the world's packets, not by code, **grade 3 for the member**, as S134 read the bounded constructor's first enumeration.

### A.3 Filtering: elimination of rivals by observation

- **Selection at β_M (D12.1).** Population 𝒯 = the family; history H = this episode's packets (pairs that occurred); survival condition = exact compatibility with every packet, a requirement (one of S84's graded conditions); variation μ = the identity (nothing new is ever proposed: "Select only existing states", VR L85; it "never fills a hole between correlated alternatives", VR L87). Nothing earlier inside β_M represents a law, H or the survival condition except holdings that entered whole, which represent nothing there (I200), so D12.1's exclusion does not block: **the survivor set is selected at β_M**. At β_D the builders represent the family and the survival condition, so the exclusion blocks selection there and the survivor set is **declared at β_D** (S134 3.5's verdict for route (a), carried over).
- **Surprise and its response (D12.7, D12.8).** Each packet that a surviving law fails is a violation of a selected transport at a pair not in its history: surprise in the theory's narrow sense. V5's response is always the selection response (extend H, drop the law), "it does not learn a replacement from the mismatch" (VR L246). The theory: "Only the second [the construction response] can be originative under Part X" (L225).

### A.4 Certificates: an account's test conditioned on a declared family

- A certificate is "an exact affirmative answer over the surviving learned subset" (RM L72), "conditional on that subset, not every surviving law in the wider bank" (RM L5). In the theory's terms it says: **at this question and this force program, the handed-in learned rivals do not conflict**, so no problem arises for p among them (D10.1 needs a conflict at some pair). It is not (A), question fidelity, of any account against the world: it is agreement among rivals, conditional on one of them being the world's.
- It ignores full-family rivals that still survive. In the attack the learned certificate said yes at 15, 16 and 18 while the world had left the represented support from 17 (VR L190): a certificate issued on a theory in error. The report says so; it does not say whether the full-family survivors agreed at 15 to 18 (searched VR L159 to L192).

### A.5 learned_model_mismatch and full_family_contradiction: a recognized difficulty?

- **Violation (D12.7).** Each latch marks that every member of a set has been violated at an occurring pair. The violation of the learned laws in the world began at 17 (truth outside support); V5 marks it at 24 (VR L166 to L167); the full family was violated in the world from 17 and **never marked through 31** (VR L168). Violation needs no one to compute it; a recognized difficulty needs the system to represent it (L429; D13.8: "a represented failure of a claimed aim").
- **Is the latch a represented failure?** It is computed by a rule the builders wrote, which entered β_M whole; on the strict reading (Rep, with provenance) the latch **represents nothing at β_M** (I200), and the difficulty is recognized only at β_D, by the builders. On the lenient reading the core uses for a target (Held, no provenance; D18.1's T′), the latch is held and the difficulty counts as recognized. Either way it **opens nothing**: "Once a resource, tracking or full-family failure is latched, later observations do not silently reset the episode. Recovery … require[s] separate interfaces" (VR L246; RM L94). S128 read a latch as a set-and-reset bit with the shape of a problem's status (open, solved); V5's latch is **set-only**, so a problem it marks can never return to solved. S88's reading put it plainly: a world in which a found rule stops working is where a constructor would have to begin. V5 builds that world (the hidden kick contradicts the whole family at 6, VR L131) and stops at the door.

### A.7 Verdict, by S86

- **Worked out by the machine: nothing.** No subhistory in V5 prepares a new binding for a held target (B below).

## D. What the theory would ask V5 for next, toward created knowledge

**The smallest addition that gives V5 an episode** (D13.8's complete critical episode; D14.7's created explanation as the aim):

1. **A recognized difficulty with a held target.** On `learned_model_mismatch` or `full_family_contradiction`, keep the failed law(s) and their predictions as the target, held inside β_M before the repair (I201). The latch already marks the moment; what is missing is holding, not discarding.
2. **The criticism, retained.** Keep the public packets of the episode (or since the last cut) in a bounded journal used **only** by the repair route. V4 had one (VR L39); V5 traded it away (VR L41). Without it no new candidate can be checked against what V5 has seen (B2).
3. **A conjecture computed from the failure, not looked up.** From the conflict between the held law's predictions and the retained packets, solve for a modification **not in the bank**. For the hidden-kick and hidden-stop scenes: a change point k and a velocity change Δv, solved exactly from the covers that broke the law, as S135's machine solved its 405 sums for the rule that repaired its own ledger errors. A larger pre-enumerated bank of change-point laws filtered by fit would be the same selection with a bigger 𝒯.
4. **Check, deploy, use.** Check the conjecture against every retained packet. Resume continuation under it (the "recovery after a latched stop" the report defers, VR L246). Forecast and certify with it. This is ProducesVia: an active route from the repair to the restored aim through the new binding (D14.6). The explanatory aim is "a deployable account of the mover's motion under the declared question" (D14.5); the protected aims are the in-family scenes' answers and no false certificate.
5. **Tests that separate construction from selection** (S134 3.5, S135): unseen hidden changes (other k, other Δv); knock-out, sham and restore of the repaired law; order permutation of the candidate modifications; the same journal without the failed rows; and the comparison S135 warns of: if the repair's outputs equal a filter over an enlarged family, only the trace separates them (Argument 9).
6. **The limit to state in advance.** The class of modifications (a change point and a velocity jump) is named by its builders: at most **construction within a named class** (S118's grade 2; L405, "a small binding newly prepared inside received content is construction of that binding"). Whether that counts as worked out is the owner's open question from S134.
7. **Cost, roughly.** As a V6 increment by the outside agent on the owner's computer: by the size of V5's own added-case work, likely several agent-days and CPU-hours, since V5 already runs about 40 times slower than simulated time; uncertain, because the code is not here. As a toy here, in plain Python in the shape of S135 (one dimension, a small affine family, interval covers, a change-point repair): about 800 to 1,500 lines and CPU-minutes, but then it is our machine, not V5.

**Would the recommended active force probe be working out?** The report's probe is "prospectively frozen … specify a probe intended to separate the surviving affine alternatives from the changed world" (VR L306). It is specified by the experimenters, so the probe is **handed in**. Its outcome eliminates members of the supplied family, so what it yields is **identification within the supplied family by a designed probe: selection**, grade 3 for the member, at most. Even if V5 chose the probe itself, by computing where its surviving rivals' forecasts disagree most (a test of a problem it holds, L317), the knowledge gained would still be *which member*, which is found knowledge. Choosing would also need a generator of force programs, since candidates now come in the request (RM L69, L72). The probe's real value for the owner's aim is different: if it exposes the stop, the whole family is contradicted and V5 reaches a **recognized difficulty** it never reached through 31 (VR L168). That is the occasion on which steps 1 to 5 above could begin. So: worth running, but as the first half of an episode, not as working out.

**One further use, for S57.** Because V5 is exact, it can serve as the oracle for (R)'s fidelity condition on an amorphous learned state. A trained, inexact memory given the same packets could be checked, question by question, against V5's exact support: which of its answers are faithful on the contract, and where it invents cross-pairs. The report points this way: "useful as a reference against which a faster or learned representation could be checked" (VR L304). That would be one concrete way to ask when an amorphous state "constitutes knowledge" of a stated content.
