## What "meaning" and "scope" are measured as

- **MEANING** is what the definition of explanation says: which conditions, on what (the question, its contract, the transport, the commitments, the designation, the stated scope, the history), with what provenance clause. It is recorded, for each variant, as **the changed formal statement beside the old one**, for each part of the explanation definition that moves: (E) and its conjuncts, what each conjunct reads, Dec (the provenance clause), being an explanation (Account ∧ ¬Dec(t)), (Suff), (Nec), unfolded down to the varied item. A variant whose statement changes while no case computed moves is recorded as "meaning moves, scope unchanged on every case computed", with the cases named.
- **SCOPE** is which things count as explanations, **computed**, the variant off against on: (1) the text's worked cases; (2) **the owner's own cases**: the two-part sign (FC23.new2; the one-part sign of FC23.new5 is the program's companion case, not the owner's), the weathervane (FC22 (b), the program's vane), the bridge (FC84.new1) and the student's copy (FC30.new1 (d)), each as meeting (E) or not and as an explanation or not, before and after; (3) made-up candidates: FC-E1 to FC-E5, CT1 to CT8, and the generated worlds at scale 4 (how many of each kind enter and leave, and the smallest witness of each); (4) **which program claims move**: the whole suite with the variant on, the claims whose status changes.

## How the replies will be read

1. **Nothing is opened until all four calls end.** No reply, receipt, reasoning file, attempt file, stream file or guard log of Part B is opened until every job has ended its last allowed pass: the run log holds `s109_glm_loop: every pass ended; …` and `loop ended <UTC time>`, and the process in the pid file has exited. The progress lines may be watched; they hold no reply text. If a call is still running 24 hours after the launch, the runner may be stopped by its exact PID and the calls not ended recorded as not returned.
2. **What counts as a reply.** As Part A's: only `<tag>.response.txt`; failed calls count neither way; connection failures are not answers; a job failed on answers after pass 3 is final; a key in the output or a sandbox not unchanged is reported before anything else is read.
3. **A reply is a set of variants and predictions, not a result.** A prediction of meaning or scope is the reply's claim until computed; a "not run" case is run; an edge is a claim until the computation settles it. Silence is not agreement.
4. **Tabulation first**, by the one Opus agent, before any computing: for each variant its section and id, the free item(s), kind, the carry-over it takes up, old and new formal statement whole, old and new sentence, the predicted meaning (the parts that move, as stated), the predicted scope (with each of the owner's four cases), the claims it expects to move, its small cases and whether run, every edge it names, the owner's decisions it names, and its inventions. It **flags** (records, and does not implement): a variant that changes a FROZEN item beyond the formalization of the free words it varies (out of Part B); one that changes another section's free item (implemented only as far as it stands on its own section's items); one that repeats a Part A variant; one that adds prose beyond the changed-sentence record (S40); one that would move where values are placed. **A variant that departs from an owner's decision is not flagged out**: it is computed, and its candidate definition is flagged for Part C (S52). It lists, per section, the free items bearing on the explanation definition that no variant reached, and the carry-overs not taken up. It writes `results/S109 Part B round 1 - tabulation of the replies, before any ruling.md`, created at once and filled as it goes (lesson S28), and rules on nothing.
5. **Implementation and computation**, by the same agent, in copies: the program after round 4 (`results/S107 Round 4 - maths after the reading/model after round 4/`) copied into `results/S109 Part B round 1 - computation/section B<n> model/`, one per section; the original and Part A's copies are never written. Each in-scope variant, and each class-a carry-over whether or not a reply took it up, becomes a reading the program can switch on and off (the current definition the default), and is computed for **meaning** and **scope** as above. Where the owner's case turns on a reading the owner has not settled (the change as an edit or a boundary; D6.3's quantifier; whether a question's history is recorded; which history the bridge has), it is computed under each reading and reported "on that reading". Where a variant cannot be implemented as written, the nearest reading that can be stated is implemented and recorded as an invention with the other choices (S36).
