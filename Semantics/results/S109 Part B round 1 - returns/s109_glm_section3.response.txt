## (b) Meaning

In all eight, **Expl = Acc(ℰ) ∧ ¬Dec(t) and (E) itself are unchanged in form**, and (Suff)/(Nec) keep their D16.XV shapes; what moves is Dec, or what Dec's conjuncts read, or nothing.

- **PB3.1.** Dec's inheritance rule moves: prov(t,o′) := prov(t,o) part by part → whole. Dec := ¬Sel ∧ ¬Con unchanged; D12.3's gloss "part by part (D12.4)" would read "whole" (constrains, below). (Suff)/(Nec) ranges move with Dec (FC30.new1 (b),(g)).
- **PB3.2.** What Sel's exclusion reads moves: D12.1's ¬∃o ≺ o_t Rep(o, x) now bans every earlier *faithful holding* of t, H, surv, cod t (Rep = Held); (R) stops being "a fidelity relation with a history". Dec's form unchanged.
- **PB3.3.** Sel's survival conjunct moves: surv's witness H must cover C ∩ Occ(h(t)); Dec := ¬Sel ∧ ¬Con unchanged in form, harder to escape.
- **PB3.4.** Dec's history parameter moves: per holding (h(t, o_t)) → per transport (h∪(t)); "exactly one of three" (L193.s1) then needs a precedence rule Sel/Con (edge below).
- **PB3.5.** What Sel's H-clause reads moves: "its pairs having occurred in h(t)" becomes admission × actual boundary; occurrence stops being Θ's primitive.
- **PB3.6.** What every history is built from moves: Occ ⊆ β(s); h(t) shrinks to inside-boundary occurrences, so Sel's exclusion and Con's represented-target clause read less.
- **PB3.7.** No part of the explanation definition moves: ≡_ℓ is read by New (D13.5) only, and New by (G), (EX). Meaning moves for *created* explanation (Origin's New conjunct), not for being one.
- **PB3.8.** No part moves and no computed value moves: (G) reads Build (D13.3) only, so "only the second can be originative" is carried by the formalization, not the sentence; the drop is a meaning-only change (the text no longer claims it).
