## (b) Meaning

**PB1.1.** NonVacuous moves: `Sol_D(1,b0) ≠ ∅ ∧ Stated(C,Σ)` → `Sol_D(1,b0) ≠ ∅` (second conjunct vacuous). (E) as a formula is unchanged (D6.7 FROZEN); one of its five conjuncts loses half its content. Σ leaves the argument list of Acc (D6.7 says "(D, C, b0, Q, δ_D, E, t, Γ, δ_E, Σ)"; with Stated always true, Σ is no longer read — a change *with* FC30's list, not of it).

**PB1.2.** No conjunct of (E) changes; what (E) is quantified over shrinks: every C in every Question(p) now holds only Θ-admitted edits. Being an explanation (Acc ∧ ¬Dec(t)) is unchanged in form, its domain narrowed by the physical module. This is the only variant of the nine that lets an import read what a question is.

**PB1.3.** What (A) and Dependence compare moves: answers live in X_δD ∪ {⊥}, not Y_p ∪ {⊥}. (A): `Ans_E(τ(a),σ(b)) = Ans_p(a,b)` unchanged in form; the space of legitimate Ans shrinks. D6.4's Contrast/Lost (FROZEN) unchanged in form, its `Y_p` now fixed. Queries returning anything but a value of the designated port (fibres, {reachable, unreachable}) name no question.

**PB1.4.** No part moves: no conjunct of (E) reads a kind. The meaning that moves is Argument 1's: "of one kind" no longer up to a relabeling of footprints; D4.3's cross-candidate comparison loses β the same way.

**PB1.5.** Found(p) moves from "constructed only" to "selected or constructed". No part of (E), Dec, Expl, (Suff) or (Nec) moves: Acc takes no provenance of the question (FC30).

**PB1.6.** Dependence moves in content, not in form. D6.1 (B2, FROZEN) reads "E−G as in D1.3"; under D1.3.b6, Sol_{E−G}(y) = ∅ for every nonempty G whose footprints are nonempty, so Ans_{E−G}(y) = ⊥, so Lost(E,G;x) holds whenever Ans_E is determined at x or x0. NC2 collapses to nearly Contrast alone:
old: ∃(a,b)∈C, G ⊆ Γ nonempty: Contrast(E;x) ∧ Lost(E,G;x);
new (equivalent under b6): ∃(a,b)∈C: Contrast(E;x) ∧ [Ans_E(x) ≠ ⊥ ∨ Ans_E(x0) ≠ ⊥].
Dependence stops asking that the *commitments* carry the contrast; it asks only that E determine an answer somewhere and the question have a contrast.

**PB1.7.** No conjunct moves. Question(p) gains a clause; the class of questions shrinks by those whose contract holds no edit other than 1. L257's frozen exclusion ("admits no candidate that meets (F2), (A) and Dependence") becomes vacuous on them: what was a fact about candidates becomes a fact about questions.

**PB1.8.** No part of (E) moves (no conjunct reads a signature). What moves is (K) itself and everything defined on it: kinds become finer (two components of one kind on C that differ in solution values separate); Argument 1's bridge (L556.n2, FC17, FC18) now compares signature-with-solutions against D4.4's projection-of-solutions, which already reads solutions — (K) and D4.4 align, which is what V1.1 found they did not (e1.03).

**PB1.9.** No part of (E) moves. Kinds stop being contract-relative: ~_{A×B} is fixed by the level's whole edit set, not by C. Meaning moves in Argument 1 and in D4.5's Changes/Inv, which keep their C (a re-order: kinds no longer vary with the contract that (F1) is judged on).

## (f) Inventions the variants rest on

- **PB1.1** drops I27 (Excl a primitive). Other choice kept: I27 as is. Settles I27 by deletion; I85's default becomes the only reading.
- **PB1.2** rests on Θ_admits as a predicate of a bare edit (from R2V1.9, not the text's). Other choices: Θ_admits(a) only of an edit *of an attributed organization at a grain* (the text's words, L159.s3), under which PB1.2 bites only on physical targets — the gentler choice; or Θ read of the pair (a,b).
- **PB1.3** settles I21 (Y_p free) one way. Other choices: Y_p free (as now); Y_p := any set with ⊥ adjoined.
- **PB1.4** drops I10 (β). Other choices: β as now; β required to preserve port domains *and* an ordering (a middle reading).
- **PB1.5** rests on I127's three values unchanged; the invention is only which two count as found. Other choice: all three (declared too), which would make Found vacuous.
- **PB1.6** invents the empty-relation reading of deletion. Other choices: the full relation (the text's, L103.s1); deletion removes the component from J (so D−G has fewer components — closest to "absent"), which would need D1.1's J fixed under edits (I03) to move too, and so is out of reach here.
- **PB1.7** invents "at least one edit other than 1" as the clause. Other choices: "some edit of C is no relabeling" (D6.9's, weaker, and D6.9 is FROZEN — constrains); "C holds two pairs with different answers" (strongest, reads Q).
- **PB1.8** invents the fourth signature component as Sol_D(a,b)|_{V_j}. Other choices: Sol of the subnetwork N = {j} alone; solutions at the baseline only.
- **PB1.9** invents A×B as the kind index. Other choices: C (the text's); any C′ with C ⊆ C′ ⊆ A×B.
