## (b) Meaning

- **PB2.1**: no conjunct of (E) moves — (E) keeps (A) as its own conjunct (D6.7 FROZEN reads `A_C` either way). What moves is **Dec's** feed: `Rep_ℓ` (D12.5) and `Held_ℓ` read `Faithful_C`, so `Rep :⟺ ∃t[F1∧F2∧Sel∨Con]` → `∃t[F1∧F2∧A∧Sel∨Con]`; `T′` (Con's represented target) and Sel's exclusion harden likewise. Being an explanation, `Acc ∧ ¬Dec(t)`, keeps its shape; the class of transports with `¬Dec(t)` shrinks by exactly the transports that fail (A) somewhere on a contract a provenance reads.
- **PB2.2**: (E)'s second conjunct: `F2_C = F2eq_C ∧ Hom(τ)` → `F2eq_C`. (F2) becomes pointwise; it no longer asks that τ respect composition or the identity.
- **PB2.3**: (E)'s first conjunct: `∀k ∈ Γ` → `∀k ∈ J_E`. (F1) now reads the named background (L231.s2), not only the commitments offered as doing the work.
- **PB2.4**: (E)'s third conjunct: `∀(a,b) ∈ C` → `∀(a,b) ∈ Det_C`. (A) stops holding E's silences to the target's: an answer where the target gives none is no longer a failure of (A).
- **PB2.5**: Dependence (D6.5 FROZEN `= NC0 ∧ NC2`) unchanged in shape; its first conjunct changes from a vacuous truth to the boundary clause. Net: `Dependence = NC2 ∧ (no answer written into a boundary slot)`.
- **PB2.6**: NonVacuous loses its second conjunct: `(E)` no longer reads the stated scope at all; the only non-relational conjunct of (E) goes (L265.n1 FROZEN keeps its point, now vacuously).
- **PB2.7**: (E) unchanged as a formula; **what a conjunct reads** moves: (A) now reads D6.3, so `Acc ⇒ ¬Slot_C(ℰ,k)` for every k — the written-in test re-enters (E) through a conjunct, not through D6.7.
- **PB2.8**: no part moves — (S), (B), (D) are read by no conjunct of (E), by Dec, or by (Suff)/(Nec) (Part A's map: D7.x nodes feed no part).
- **PB2.9**: no conjunct's form moves; what (F1), (F2) read (a transport, D5.1) narrows at the type: `t` exists only with total π. Meaning moves while, on every case whose π is already total, no value does.
