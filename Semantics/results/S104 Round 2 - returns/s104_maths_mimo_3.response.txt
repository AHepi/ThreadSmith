# (d) Attacks on the claims that held

**FC03 · L119.** Under I10(b) the relation is not transitive. Ports p,q,r each ∈ {0,1}; j on (p,q), j' on (q,r), j'' on (p,r); C = {(1,b0)}; \(L_j=\{(0,0),(0,1)\}\) on (p,q); \(L_{j'}=\{(0,0),(1,0)\}\) on (q,r); \(L_{j''}=\{(0,0),(1,0)\}\) on (p,r). β (p↦r, q↦q) carries \(L_j\) to \(L_{j'}\); β' (q↦p, r↦r) carries \(L_{j'}\) to \(L_{j''}\); the only bijection that is the identity on \(V_j\cap V_{j''}=\{p\}\) is (p↦p, q↦r), giving \(\{(0,0),(0,1)\}\neq L_{j''}\). So ~_C is not an equivalence relation under I10(b); it rests on that alternative, which L119's words rule out. FC03 stands as the maths has it.

**FC15 · L554.** σ(b0) need not be E's baseline: E with boundaries \(\{b_0^E,b_1\}\), σ(b0)=b1, C = {(1,b0)} gives τ[C] = {(1,b1)}, which holds no baseline of E and is not a contract in L141's sense.
