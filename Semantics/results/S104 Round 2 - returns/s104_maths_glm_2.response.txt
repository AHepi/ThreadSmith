## (b) The counterexample

**FC08 · L127.** The witness (a component whose relation is constant in a port it reads, meeting both clauses of L124 while its reading does not follow that port) is a counterexample to the **text**, not to an invention. It rests on no choice that an invention fills: under either reading of the observation edits one can add a recalibration and the mismatch stands, and the mismatch is between two sentences of the text itself — L119's "not from the values its ports take in a solution" and L127's "change the part and the reading follows". A measurement that can ignore what it measures is also not what L124 should allow. The change that removes it, and that also writes I07 into the words and closes U5:

```
> L124 | - a **measurement** has, for a port it reads and does not itself determine, a signature invariant under interventions on that port and variable under edits to the measuring relation, and a reading that depends on that port in the relation itself;
```

"Does not itself determine" uses L109's own output definition, which kills the U5 case (a component always determines the port it assigns). With the dependence clause the gloss's second clause becomes true wherever it can be, and L127's wording should say so:

```
> L127 | In particular, a part that reads or reports another part has a measurement's signature on a contract that holds an edit altering its reading: change only the reading and the part it reports stays as it was; change the part and the reading follows where the reading depends on it.
```

## (c) The inventions and unrecorded choices

**I06 · L109, L127.** The text settles the identification of the observed port: on the alternative (a) reading (the port as the thing reported), the "that is" clause — "the component assigning it has a measurement's signature" — could not hold, since a component that assigns a port determines it and cannot measure it. The text does **not** settle R-i against R-ii. It should take R-i: L103 makes an intervention on a port a *replacement* of the reporting component, and a replacement alters the relation; and R-i alone gives the pole's C2 the reading the worked case wants (the shadow a measurement of H and θ) where R-ii makes all three components causal and none a measurement. Wording, writing I06's R-i into the text:

```
> L109 | A port is an **observation** when \(A\) contains an edit that alters the relation reporting it without altering what it reports — an edit that sets the port among them — that is, when the component assigning it has a measurement's signature (below).
```

This is the sentence of L109 that the definition D2.4 formalizes; the rest of the line stands.

**I07 · L124.** The text does not settle uniqueness ("the measured port") or the case of several read ports. L127's "a part that reads or reports another part" points to per-part measurement relations, which is I07's choice; the rival (b), a separate measuring component, would let the reading component's own signature stay fixed while "the measuring relation" varies, which L124's grammar ("its signature … variable under edits to the measuring relation") forbids. Adopt the L124 wording above; it writes I07 in.

**I08 · L125, L347.** "The rule" as the component itself is settled by L347's wording (edits to \(C_r\), interventions on \(Z\)): the rule is the thing edited, the world the thing intervened on. "The world" as all ports j does not assign, versus the ports j reads only, is not settled — but given D2.1 the two differ in nothing observable (an intervention on a port j does not read cannot alter j), so it should stay open and be recorded in I08 as making no difference.

**I09 · L123–L125.** Same-boundary comparison is settled by L119 (above). Vacuous invariance and the existential "changes" are not settled; both should be written in, at L119:

```
> L119 | An edit that sets a port replaces only the component that assigns the port (above), not the relations of the components that read it. A signature changes under a class of edits on a contract when some edit of that class on the contract alters it, and is invariant under the class when none does — invariant under a class the contract does not touch.
```

This writes I09 into the text (named). The existential reading of "changes" is the one that keeps FC10's implication of L57 clean under either reading of L57.

**I102 · L109, L123.** The union rule for several assigned ports is unsettled and reasonable. The undefined-`asg` clause has the U5 consequence and should be replaced by determination (L109's own notion), as the L124 wording above does on the measurement side; on the causal side "its output port" is H02's question.

**U5 · I102.** As above: record the consequence in I102, and close it with L124's "does not itself determine". A component measuring the port it assigns is against L109's output definition, so the text already settles it against the maths.

**H01 · L103, L119, L125.** The record is right that the default follows most literally from L119's "only" (a composite replacing two components does not replace *only* the assigner of either). But L91's closure puts composites into A, and the default then classes them as rule edits for every component they touch, collapsing the families on C2* — and the first recorded rival collapses them too (no family at all under R-i). The stable choice is the unrecorded third one: a composite is a joint intervention, an intervention on none alone, and an edit to no rule. Writing H01 into the text, as a further sentence of L119:

```
> L119 | An edit that sets a port replaces only the component that assigns the port (above), not the relations of the components that read it. An edit that replaces several components together sets no port, edits no rule, and is an intervention on none of them alone.
```

(If both additions to L119 are adopted, they stand as consecutive sentences.) Under this, C2*'s families are C2's, and FC06 holds of composites as well.

**H02 · L123, L109.** The text settles it, against the maths as written: L109 defines an output ("A port is an **output** of component \(j\) when its value is determined by \(L_j\)…"), and "its output port" in L123 should mean that, not `asg`.
