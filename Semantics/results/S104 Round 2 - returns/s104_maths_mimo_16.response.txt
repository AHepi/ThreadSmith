**FC91 · L466, L471.** The equivalence is exact under I61. Without I61's restriction of F's quantification to the task's inputs it breaks. **FC92 · L471.** Monotonicity and the greatest-fixed-point construction survive every reading of "complete" and every size of \(Z\) (Knaster–Tarski on \(\mathcal P(Z)\)); nothing breaks them. Then \(F(X)=\{1\}\cup(X\cap\{0\})\) and \(\mathrm{gfp}(F)=\{0,1\}\): state 1, which cannot perform the task at all, is in the greatest retained set — against `> L469 | deadlock is not a vacuous performance of the task`.
