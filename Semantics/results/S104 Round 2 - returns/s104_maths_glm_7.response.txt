**FC26.** Two attacks. First, the non-vacuity leg: under I85 the scope statement states every excluded pair by default, so "not silently" is never tested; that leg is holds-by-construction in effect, and the text should say where the scope is stated (I27's part). Second, the NC1 leg — a hand model against D6.3's every-pair form. D: ports H, L ∈ {1,2}; B = {b0}; edits {1, e} with e = set(H := 2) replacing c_H; components c_H (H = 1), c_L (L = H); Q reads L; Ans_p(1,b0) = 1, Ans_p(e,b0) = 2. E: port L; components m, n on {L}. m: full relation at (1,b0), {L = 2} at (e,b0). n: {L = 1} at (1,b0), replaced by the full relation at (e,b0) (τ(e) is that edit, τ(1) = 1). Then Ans_E = 1 at the baseline, 2 at the edited pair: (A) holds, Contrast holds, and with G = {m} the answer at the baseline is lost under deletion (n gone, L free), so NC2 holds. NC1 holds too: m is a slot at the edited pair but not at the baseline, and D6.3 asks for every pair. Yet m's relation at the edited pair is exactly the target's answer — the answer appears as a component, contra L255's sentence. Under the wording proposed in (b), m is a slot at that pair and is not replaced by the contract's edit there (τ(e) replaces n), so NC1 fails and E is excluded, while the forward organization's c_L at settings of L is replaced by the contract's edit and stays exempt — the computed results stand. Rests on the unrecorded every-pair form (kin to I24), I12, I21, I03, I04.

**FC27.** The divergence at h ≠ u_H is definitional: E_rev's c'_L reads the boundary whatever the edit does to H. A non-identity τ mapping set-H elsewhere would fall at "translates C" before (F2) is reached. Nothing found beyond the bounds.

**FC28.** Extending the contract to vary u_θ as well (which also "alters the observed L") leaves E_rev faithful: c'_L gives L = u_H cot u_θ, c'_H gives H = u_H, and the fibre is {u_H}. So L325 is false for any contract that both varies boundaries and holds a setting — the wording proposed under I65, which excludes edits that set any port, removes this without restricting the boundary variation.
