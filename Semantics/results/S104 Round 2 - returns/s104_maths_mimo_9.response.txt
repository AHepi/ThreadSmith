**FC75 · L375 (attack: dependence outside the route).** Occurrences: \(i\) (port \(x\in\{0,1\}\)), \(c\) (passes \(i\)'s value on), \(m\) (passes \(c\)'s value on), \(r\) (takes \(m\)'s value). Connections: \(i\to c\), \(c\to m\), \(m\to r\), and \(i\to r\), which \(r\) ignores. Contrasts \(K=\{(0,1)\}\). Take \(R=\{i,r\}\): connected, every occurrence of \(R\) on a \(\prec_h\)-chain from \(i\) to \(r\) inside \(R\), each meets the relation it is given, and setting \(i\)'s port to \(0\) and \(1\) gives different values at \(r\) — via \(c,m\), outside \(R\). D11.4 counts \(R\) active although nothing \(R\) carries produced \(r\)'s value. Rests on I46 (the clause reads the endpoints only) and I95 (setting the port recomputes all later values). **FC75 · L375 (attack: side component).** Occurrences \(i\) (port \(x\)), \(c\) (constant source), \(m\) (takes both), \(r\) (takes \(m\)'s first component); connections \(i\to m\), \(c\to m\), \(m\to r\). \(R=\{i,c,m,r\}\) is connected, joins \(i\) to \(r\), and the value at \(r\) depends on \(i\)'s port. **FC37 · L305 (beyond the bounds).** Hand proof for all finite \(\Gamma\): if \(d\in\bigcup\min\mathsf S\), the minimal \(W\ni d\) has \(W\setminus\{d\}\notin\mathsf S\) (no proper subset of \(W\) is in \(\mathsf S\)); if \(CB(\{d\};W)\), a minimal \(W'\subseteq W\) cannot avoid \(d\), for then upward closure would put \(W\setminus\{d\}\) in \(\mathsf S\). And \(\Gamma\setminus\{d\}\notin\mathsf S\) iff no minimal member avoids \(d\). The bound of I77 does not bind this claim.

**FC41 · L313 (beyond the bounds).** NoWork(\(d\)) gives \(V\setminus\{d\}\in\mathsf S\) for every \(V\in\mathsf S\); so for any \(W\subseteq\Gamma\), \(W\cup\{d\}\in\mathsf S\) iff \(W\setminus\{d\}\in\mathsf S\) (apply it to \(W\cup\{d\}\) or to \(W\setminus\{d\}\)). A finite block of no-work commitments is removed one at a time with membership kept, so it is critical in no route. Holds for all families, not only \(|\Gamma|\le 4\).

**FC40 · L311 (beyond the bounds).** For arbitrary subsets of \(\mathbb N\): \(W\) is a route iff its index set is unbounded; \(CB(B;W)\) iff the index set of \(W\setminus B\) is bounded; a critical block is infinite (removing finitely many indices keeps unboundedness); each \(d_n\) does no work (adding or removing one index keeps it). I91's restriction does not bind.

**FC38/FC39 · L307/L309 (realizations).** FC38: question "is \(x=0\)?", \(a\) and \(b\) each the constraint \(x=0\). Then \(\mathsf S=\{\{a\},\{b\},\{a,b\}\}\).
