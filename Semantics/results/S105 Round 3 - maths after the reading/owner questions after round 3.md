## A. Blocking (1)

**R3-Q1 (was OQ-R3A2-1; area 2, W3). L255, D6.3, I136: when does a model count as simply having the answer written into it?**

- **Blocks:** L255 (held; no text change was proposed there); D6.3's quantifier. Meanwhile the maths and the program keep side 1 (`core.SLOT_QUANTIFIER = "every"`).
- **Why the owner's:** L255 "does not appear … as a component" names no range over the question's cases; the owner's words say nothing on it; the computation shows what each side keeps and does not choose (FC23.new1).

| side | formal (D6.3) | in plain words | everyday example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (the maths now) | Slot :⟺ one part gives the answer by itself at **every** case of the question that has an answer | only a part that gives the answer in all such cases counts as the answer written in | A shop sign is red on Mondays, blue on Tuesdays. A model with one part saying "red" (in place on Mondays) and another saying "blue" (on Tuesdays) is **not** counted as having the answer written in: no one part gives it both days. A vane that points anywhere in still air and north when someone turns it north by hand: a model whose one part is "north when turned" **is** counted as written in, since that part gives the only answer there is. |
| 2 | Slot :⟺ one part gives the answer by itself at **some** case, unless the change the question asks about put it there | any part that simply gives the answer in any one case counts, except where the change asked about put it there | The two-part sign model **is** counted as written in (on Mondays the "red" part simply gives the answer). The hand-turned vane's "north" part is **not**, since the turning, the change asked about, put it there. |

What the computation shows: the text's own cases come out alike under both sides (the pole on its production contract, the owner's weathervane with the wind (Q15), the lookups M1–M3, FC-E1–FC-E5, CT1–CT8); M5 and FC23.new1 (b), (d) separate them; FC62's encoding of L339 separates them, a second encoding of the same construction does not (FC23.new1 (g)). Not offered: "some" without the exception (W3 as written), which counts the pole's own mechanism as written in whenever the question includes setting the shadow's length directly (FC23.new1 (a), (c)).
