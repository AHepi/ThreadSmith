Let me look at the actual math. Setting: approximate transport between a target's dynamics S on X (or on states of D) and a represented dynamics T on Y, with transport map π: X→Y. One-step discrepancy: d(π(Sz), T(π(z))) ≤ ε. n-step discrepancy: e_n = d(π(S^n z), T^n(π(z))).
