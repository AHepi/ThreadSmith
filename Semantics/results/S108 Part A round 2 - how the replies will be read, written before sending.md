The progress lines may be watched; they hold no reply text. If a call is still running 24 hours after the launch, the orchestrator may stop the runner by its PID and record the calls not ended as not returned.
2. **What counts as a reply.** As in round 1's rule 2: only `<tag>.response.txt`; failed calls count neither way; connection failures are not answers; a job failed on answers after pass 3 is final; a key in the output or a sandbox not unchanged is reported to the orchestrator before anything else is read.
3. **A reply is a set of variants and claims about them, not a result.** A trace is the reply's claim until computed; a "not run" case is run; an edge is a claim until the computation settles it; a settlement a reply proposes for a round-1 edge is a proposal until computed.
