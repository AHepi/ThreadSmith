## 2. The reply's argument (point 3, task (b))

> "The second element is stated at Part IV: 'a selected transport has no represented target and no criticism in its history.' The word 'blind' appears nowhere in the body. Part IV defines selection as 'a population \(\mathcal T\) of candidate transports, a variation operator \(\mu\) on \(\mathcal T\)' and constrains the history ('No member of the history represents \(t\), \(H\), or the survival condition'). These rule out guidance by representation and criticism. 'Blind' in the strict sense — variation undirected in *any* way — is stronger: it also rules out a variation operator that responds to environmental cues without any represented target or criticism."

> "If read strictly, 'blind' adds a claim the body does not state and the ORDER classification is wrong. If read loosely as a conventional shorthand for 'not directed by representation or criticism,' it restates Part IV and the classification holds. The sentence's contrast with 'constructed, produced by an episode of conjecture and criticism' favours the loose reading; the text does not force it. I flag the gap and let the change stand."

> "*Proposed repair.* Delete 'blind,' leaving: 'produced by variation and survival on a history of encountered changes, with no represented target in that history.' This is then unambiguously ORDER — every element is stated at Part IV."

## 3. Reading

**Where the body speaks of selection.** In the revised text, with the file-11 line in brackets:
- L195 (f11 L197), Part IV, **Selected**: "a population \(\mathcal T\) of candidate transports, a variation operator \(\mu\) on \(\mathcal T\), a finite history \(H\subseteq C\) … and a survival condition requiring fidelity on \(H\) … No member of the history represents \(t\), \(H\), or the survival condition."
- L201 (f11 L203): "a selected transport has no represented target and no criticism in its history; a constructed one has both."
- L405 (f11 L403), Part X: "A selected transport has no represented target in its history; a constructed one does."
- L475 (f11 L473), Part XII: "a physically admitted variation operator, and a survival condition enacted by the environment."
- L53 (f11 L55) and L155 (f11 L157) name "a variation operator" and "a variation-and-survival history" and set no condition on it.

**What these lines constrain.** They constrain the history: nothing in it represents the target, and it holds no criticism. They set no condition on \(\mu\) beyond its being a physically admitted operator on \(\mathcal T\). Nothing in Parts I–XVI says the variation is undirected, random, or independent of the environment. File 10's body is the same at f10 L210, L216, L416 and L482, and f10 L13's "blind" was never glossed there. The reply's reading is correct: the body neither states nor excludes a variation operator steered by a cue or a gradient that represents nothing.

**Does NEW force the loose reading?** No. The clause "with no represented target in that history" attaches to "a history of encountered changes". It is a condition on the history and does not gloss the adjective. "Blind" stands as a separate adjective on "variation", which is exactly where the body is silent. The contrast with "constructed, produced by an episode of conjecture and criticism" makes the loose sense likely, but it does not force it. The drafter's intent, to pin "blind" to the body's sense, is not carried by the syntax. Taken strictly, "blind" lets a reader conclude something the current text leaves unconcluded: a correspondence shaped by cue-steered or gradient-steered variation, with no represented target, is not *selected*. Part IV's definition would admit that correspondence, so Part 0 would then state a narrower class than Part IV. Under the brief's own test for ORDER ("states, in its place, only what the text already states elsewhere") the word fails. The record shows that this is not an idle worry. Four of four S81 readers read the same word as a claim (M2), R1 named the gradient case unprompted, and the drafter wrote that the entry is CLAIM "under the strict rule".

**The cases.**
- *The reply's own case* (a variation operator that responds to environmental cues and represents nothing). On Part IV, it can be selection. On strict Part 0, it cannot. This is the undeclared difference. No fixed verdict in either book turns on it, and the entry does not intend it.
- *O11* (the apprentice). "Monday is blind fitting." Monday's trying of keys has no cue and no represented target, so it is selection on either reading. Wednesday picks a key "saying it looks like Tuesday's". She uses a represented content, so the body already makes her no selection history, and the verdict "neither" holds on either reading. S81 found O11 AGREE in both files with identical passages, and file 11 has no "blind" at all. Deleting the word cannot move O11 away.
- *N9* (dark moths). Inherited variation, a survival condition enacted by the birds, and "No moth has any idea of the colour of the bark … nobody planned the change". This is selection on either reading. It holds.
- *N11* (Jana and Kasia). "The teacher's choice among her drafts" is ruled out as selection in the theory's sense because the chooser represents what she wants. The clause "with no represented target in that history" does that work, and "blind" adds nothing to it. Jana writes "without knowing what the teacher liked", which is blind in the loose sense. On the strict sense, her own purposeful drafting might not count as "blind", but the verdict does not turn on that: "Every sentence in each final essay was written by the student". It holds either way.

**What the word adds.** The substantive gain of W37, that Part 0 cannot be read as calling criticism-by-choice "selection", is carried entirely by the clause, which copies L405. The word "blind" contributes only its link to file 10's L13 and its ordinary strict sense, and that strict sense is the undeclared claim.

## 4. Ruling: FIX

The argument succeeds on the point it names. Its conclusion STANDS rests on a reading the text does not force, and the reply says so itself. Keeping "blind" would leave a reasonable strict reading on which the kind ORDER is false and no declaration covers the change. It is also the reading that the S81 readers took of this same word. Declaring CLAIM does not help. The prepared line ("… as Part IV states") says the content is only Part IV's, so it would not state the strict claim, and the semantics does not intend that claim anyway. Dropping the whole entry would lose the clarification that does the real work, and the sources note's pointer "(Parts 0 and IV)" would then need its fallback. So the fix is to delete the one word.

- **NEW (corrected):**
  ````text
  A correspondence can be *selected*, produced by variation and survival on a history of encountered changes, with no represented target in that history;
  ````
- **OLD:** unchanged.
- **KIND:** ORDER (unchanged). With the word deleted, the change only adds the clause, which states what L195, L201 and L405 (f11 L197, L203, L403) already state.
- **DECLARATION:** none (unchanged). The prepared fallback line should become: "If a checker rules CLAIM: 'Part 0 now says that a selection history holds no represented target, as Part IV states.'" The old fallback begins "Part 0 again calls selection blind", which would now be false.
- **REASON WORD:** erratum → **clarification**. The change no longer restores the dropped word. It is the clarification half of W37's handling.
- **Title:** "Restore 'blind' …" is no longer true. It could be changed to "W37.1 — Part 0's account of selection: no represented target in the history". The id is kept.
- **Recorded as after the cross-examination.** The reason to record reads: after the cross-examination (Mimo, part A1, point 3), "blind" is deleted, because its ordinary strict sense (variation not directed in any way, for example by a cue or a gradient) says more than Parts I–XVI state (S81 03 M2, R1's close call), and the clause alone states the body's sense.

**What is lost.** Part 0 no longer uses file 10's word, and the GAIN's "in the same word as file 10" goes. The theory's text no longer echoes the verbal link to O11's verdict ("blind fitting"). No case depends on that link, because O11 was AGREE under file 11, which never has the word. Nothing is lost in the theory's claims.
