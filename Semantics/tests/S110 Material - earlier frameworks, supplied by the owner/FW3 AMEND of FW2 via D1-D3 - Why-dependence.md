## A merged semantic theory of knowledge, adequacy, and work, as a candidate amendment layer to FW2

### Purpose and standing

This document merges the results of three negative deductions performed on *FW2*: on knowledge, on the `Account` relation, and on the word "work." Each deduction collected every exclusion FW2 states or entails, reduced the exclusions to prohibitions, checked satisfiability, and reported what survives. This document integrates what survives into one layered theory, states the amendments to FW2 that the deductions require, and records what would count against each new claim.

**Standing.** This is a candidate amendment layer. It withdraws no displayed definition of FW2. It adds one declared primitive, a layered set of definitions above it, twenty structural results, five terminological disambiguations, seven textual repairs, one signature generalization with FW2's display as its special case, a specification discipline, and refutation conditions. It is canonical only for a specification that explicitly adopts it; adoption of FW2 alone does not adopt it. Authority fixes meanings; it confers no immunity from criticism.

**The shape of the result.** The three deductions descend: knowledge is not a thing, status, quantity, or state, but a relation with a problem argument; the adequacy relation among those relations, `Account`, has one positive content, work; and work has one positive content, why-dependence, which FW2 characterizes only negatively and by an open list of modes. So the theory has a single explanatory primitive, "because," and everything above it is definition, index, projection, or exclusion. This document is organized on that descent, from the primitive upward.

**Interpretive commitments carried over.** I1 unqualified "knowledge" is explanatory knowledge; I2 exclusions are marked stated or derived; I3 where FW2 uses one word in two senses the displayed definition governs; I4 K-REAL is about contents under interpretation, not occurrences; I5 `Account` unqualified is the displayed relation; I6 `FreeVariation` is a diagnostic of work, not its definition; I7 "why-dependence" names the relation K-REAL invokes and does not analyze.

# Part I. The primitive and the layered definitions

## I.1 The primitive: why-dependence (K-BECAUSE)

A feature `f` of a problem `p`, against background `b`, within scope `Σ`, has its character because of the content of a set of commitments `W`. Write

`Because(W, f; p, b, Σ)`.

This is the theory's one explanatory primitive. It is what K-REAL invokes when it says an explanation attempts to say "why the relevant phenomenon or relation has the character it does." It is not defined. It is bounded by seven prohibitions (N3 §2), each of which FW2 states or entails:

1. It is not fit: not the observations `W` matches, the responses it produces, the predictions it yields, correlation, or symmetric counterfactual dependence.
2. It is not a verdict or a count as warrant: not approval, preference, a check, a computed intersection, or a count that yields probability.
3. It is not the vehicle: not wording, exposition, a proposed connection, a derivation, a lower-level redescription, or an occurrence.
4. It is not enabling or presence: not fixing which instances can begin, supplying evidence or attention, causal involvement, background presence, standing, retention, self-preservation, or physical transformation.
5. It is not the thinker: not inferential machinery, conscious use, deployment, recognition, or contribution.
6. It is not a truth- or modal-surrogate: not the truth of `W`'s ontology, uniqueness, necessity, non-free-variation, hard-to-vary, or sufficiency.
7. It is not index-free: it is at `p`, for `f`, in a respect, within `Σ`; it is not reach or a free consequence.

**Properties.** `Because` is asymmetric (`f` is as it is because of `W`; not the converse), recognition-independent (it can hold before any agent identifies it), wording-independent (preserved by content-preserving recoding, destroyed by content-changing substitution), and ontology-tolerant (a commitment can carry a why-dependence in scope while stated in a false or idealized ontology).

**Modes.** `Because` may hold in any mode: mechanism, geometry, constraint, structure, intention, institution, or another. The list is open because mode is posterior to work: a mode is appropriate for a feature iff commitments stated in it stand in `Because` to that feature. No criterion of appropriateness prior to work is needed or given (N3 §3).

**Status.** Declaring a primitive is not a reduction and is not emptiness. The seven prohibitions decide cases FW2 did not consider (Part IV: asymmetry, overdetermination, dependence without role) and exclude every published reduction of explanatory relevance (N3 §7). The primitive is regulative in the way "truth" is regulative for a correspondence theory. K-RECURSION applies to it: a claim that `Because` holds is a content eligible for the kernel.

## I.2 Work, minimal working subsets, constraint, and reach

Let `e` be an interpreted content with commitments `Γ` (explicit or inexplicit), and let `f` be the relevant feature of `p` in the stated respect.

**Minimal working subset.** `MWS(W, e, f; p, b, Σ)` iff `W ⊆ Γ`, `Because(W, f; p, b, Σ)`, and no proper subset of `W` satisfies `Because` for `f`. There may be several minimal working subsets for one feature (overdetermination); there may be none (the explanation does no work on `f`).

**Work.** A commitment does work iff it belongs to some minimal working subset:

`Work(d, e, f; p, b, Σ)` iff `∃W [d ∈ W ∧ MWS(W, e, f; p, b, Σ)]`.

Work is contributory, not necessary: a commitment in one of two overdetermining subsets does work and is not essential to the account as a whole. This replaces the singleton reading of "the explanatory work done by the commitments" (FW2, II attempting) with a set-level one, and it is what makes the diagnostic below sound (N3 §4, §5 B3).

**Constraint (projection onto details).** `Constrained(d, e, f; p, b, Σ)` iff `Work(d, e, f; p, b, Σ)`. A content-bearing detail is constrained iff varying it changes the set of features reached. "Hard to vary" is the condition that every content-bearing detail of `e` is constrained; padding is a detail in no minimal working subset. This is FW2's "which content-bearing details are constrained by the work the explanation does," stated as a projection of work rather than as a separate topic (N3 §3).

**Reach (projection onto further problems).** `Reach(e, p′; b′, Σ′)` iff some minimal working subset of `e` for a feature of `p`, with commitments unchanged and background extended to `b′`, is a minimal working subset for a feature of `p′` within `Σ′`. Reach is work seen from the further features it runs to; constraint is work seen from the details it runs through. They are two projections of one relation, which is why FW2 can say in one sentence that an account "does more explanatory work and is harder to vary" (N3 §3, §6 C4). An application claim must state the unchanged commitments, the added background, and the explanation of relevance; a bridge theory is an additional contribution.

**The diagnostic.** `FreeVariation_V(e, D; p, b)` for `D ⊆ Γ` holds when a change to `D` within family `V` preserves the claimed account without a new reason or compensating explanatory change. FW2's display `FreeVariation_V(e, d; p, b)` is the singleton case. The family must contain informative contrasts and must range over subsets, since singleton variation calls each overdetermining commitment free (N3 §5 B3). The diagnostic is comparative, respect-relative, and fallible; it is evidence about `Work`, not `Work`.

**Transport.** Work is the unit of transport. A contextual transport `T: ⟨p, b, k⟩ ⇝ ⟨p′, b′, k′⟩` carries minimal working subsets across a theory change; "what work survives or is superseded" (FW2, II progress) is which subsets are preserved, revised, or abandoned. A surviving subset stated in a superseded ontology is the ordinary case (Part IV, case 9).

## I.3 Adequacy: the `Account` relation

`Account(e, p, b; Σ)` holds iff:

- (A1) `Γ` has at least one minimal working subset for the relevant feature `f` of `p` against `b` within `Σ`;
- (A2) no member of that subset is selected by `e`'s own conclusion (non-circularity; FW2's two-balances case);
- (A3) the indices `p`, `b`, `Σ` are the ones stated, and `b` includes every element of `K` that is `Essential` to `e` (the under-indexing repair, N2 §5 B1);
- (A4) the work is distinguishable from associating an input with an output (FW2, II attempting), which A1 guarantees by prohibition 1 on `Because`.

Everything else FW2 says about `Account` is exclusion (N2 §1: seventy-six items reducing to seven prohibitions), index, or non-restriction. `Account` is:

- a fact (model satisfaction), independent of every judgment and vehicle (K-REAL; FW2 standpoints);
- non-comparative: two independently reasoned explanations can both be in `Account` for one `p`;
- recognition-independent and attempt-independent: it can hold for a problem nobody has posed (via reach) and fail for one someone has deployed an account of;
- factive over work and not over commitments: it holds when a minimal working subset carries the why-dependence in scope, whatever the ontology the subset is stated in; a commitment's falsity defeats `Account` exactly when it defeats the work in `Σ`, and otherwise is a criticism of quality (N2 §5 B4);
- non-retroactive under supersession: a later theory that accounts for more, and for why the earlier one worked, does not make the earlier one not to have accounted within its scope;
- undefined for nothing: when `p` is ill-posed and has no relevant feature, `Account` fails rather than lapsing, and the progress available consists in exposing `p` (decision adopted here; N2 §5 B5, §9 Q3).

**The interpreter's obligation is not a condition on the relation.** "An admissible interpretation must identify the explanatory work done by the commitments" (FW2, II attempting) is an obligation at the attribution standpoint. Read as a condition on `Account`, it would make adequacy depend on someone's having identified the work, violating recognition-independence (N2 §6 C9).

## I.4 Instances of `Account` with other explananda (conjectural)

Three of FW2's other substantive relations have the form of `Account` with a different explanandum. They are stated here as conjectures, because adopting them changes the count of primitives in the model signature from several to one (N2 §6 C6; N3 §5 B7).

**Bearing.** `Bearing(g, δ, z, p, b)` iff `Account(⟨g, λ⟩, p_δ, b; Σ)`, where `p_δ` is the problem "why does target `z` have defect `δ`, relevantly to `p`?" A criticism is a conjecture about a defect; its bearing is the why-dependence of the defect on the grounds. If this holds, the three non-criticism mechanisms of FW2 (prediction error, consensus by suppression, blocking) fail `Bearing` for the same reason they fail `Account`: nothing in them stands in `Because` to a defect.

**Contribution.** `Contributes_β(s, x, h, e)` iff there is an account `a` with `Account(a, p_x, b; Σ)`, where `p_x` is "why does `x` have the problem-specific organization it has?", and `a` attributes that organization to the activity of `s` at boundary `β`. This is FW2's "an account of the contribution" (II authorship) read as an `Account` on provenance. "Mere causal involvement is not sufficient" is prohibition 4 on `Because`.

**Progress.** `Progress(ξ, ξ′; Δ)` iff `ξ′` contains, attributably to `Δ`, an instance of `Account` or `Bearing` absent from `ξ`, or a reformulation of `P` under a valid transport that preserves the difficulty and admits such an instance, with losses exposed and changed standards displayed. FW2's five kinds of improvement (replacing a false mechanism, explaining why an approximation worked, finding a conflict was an artifact of interpretation, finding a better question, recognizing a limit) are each a gain in `Account` or `Bearing` somewhere in `ξ′` or a change in `P` that makes such gain possible. No common scale is imposed; the conjecture supplies the form of an improvement, not a measure.

**The single-primitive conjecture.** If all three hold, `Account`, `Bearing`, `Progress`, and authorship are why-dependence with stated relata, and the model signature `⟨S, C, H, X, Rep, Prov, CF, Account, Bearing, Progress, Capacity, Θ⟩` has one explanatory primitive. `Capacity` is modal and is not an instance; it remains a separate substantive relation.

## I.5 Merit and created knowledge

Two further relations stand above `Account`, and FW2's uses of "knowledge" name one or the other of the three (N1 §3).

**Merit.** `Merit(e; ξ, Σ)` iff `Account(e, p_ξ, b_ξ; Σ)` and `e` withstands the criticism applicable in `ξ` sufficiently well to merit preference among the actual alternatives `A_ξ` within `Σ`. This is FW2's "current good explanation." Merit is comparative and situation-indexed: it changes when an alternative is invented, with no change in `e` or in the world. `Account` is one conjunct of it and does not change.

**Created knowledge.** `Created_β(x; ξ, ξ′, h, e)` iff there is a connected creative critical contribution `Δ` containing `x`, `Progress(ξ, ξ′; Δ)`, the attribution of `Δ` to the system at `β` is accounted for, and `x` is available for use or reconstruction by the attributed system or identified community after `e`. Availability is repertoire-availability (deployability in an explanatory role), not standing, not endorsement, and not perpetual: loss does not make creation not to have occurred.

**Three relations, one word.** FW2's "knowledge" names adequacy (`Account`), merit, or created knowledge, and the three come apart: "not comparative" holds of adequacy only; "can be lost" of created knowledge only; "can be false" of created knowledge and merit, and of adequacy only in the sense that its commitments need not be true. A specification adopting this layer states which relation each of its uses of "knowledge" projects.

## I.6 Standing, custody, deployability, and operative role

Four relations that FW2 keeps separate from the explanatory relations are displayed here because the deductions show each is regularly confused with one of them.

**Standing.** `Standing_j(d; u)`: `d` is treated as an available premise for application `u` in appraisal `j`. Conferred by an appraisal act; indexed by use and respect; deontic, not epistemic. An appraisal act needs no prior standing to confer standing; this is how K-RECURSION breaks the regress ("at some point a rule is executed rather than consulted"). Standing is never derived from `Account`, `Merit`, `Created`, or a record state, and none of those is derived from standing (N1 §6 C3).

**Custody.** `Custody(s, o, h, e)`: system `s` holds occurrence `o` in history `h` at `e`. Transmission moves custody. Custody of an occurrence is not deployability of its content.

**Deployability.** `Deployable(s, x, e)` iff `x ∈ R^{ℓ,β}_{<e}(s, h)`: `s` can deploy `x` in a relevant explanatory role. This is FW2's repertoire. Storage of a string is custody, not deployability.

**Operative role.** `Deployed(d, κ, r)`: content `d` occupies role `r` (premise, standard, interpretation, comparison, method) in instance `κ` of the kernel relation `K`. This is a record fact. Target closure ranges over `Deployed`, not over what `κ`'s outcome depends on (Part II, T14). Write `DependsOn(κ, d)` for the latter; it is not a record fact and is not what "doing work in an instance" means in FW2's kernel and hierarchy sections.

# Part II. Structural results

Each result follows from the definitions of Part I together with displayed definitions of FW2. Derivations are in the notes.

**T1. No explanatory relation has a knower argument.** `Because`, `Work`, `Account`, `Merit`, and `Created` have a problem or problem situation as an argument and no believer, endorser, or judge. The knower enters only through attribution (`Authors`, `Contributes`, `Deployable`), which is kept separate. (N1 §3)

**T2. No existential closure.** None of the explanatory relations collapses to a one-place predicate on contents. Closing over `p`, `Σ`, `β`, or `ℓ` hides what FW2 requires to be displayed ("every use... displays s, β, ℓ, the history, and the contribution index"). Explanatory knowledge is therefore not a sortal over contents; physical knowledge (Marletto) is a one-place predicate on information; the two differ in logical type. (N1 §3, §6 C1)

**T3. Acquisition is creation.** If `Deployable(s, x, e)` for explanatory `x`, then an originative act at `s`'s boundary precedes `e`: understanding is reconstruction, reconstruction is authorship, and reconstructed content is new relative to the acquirer's own baseline. FW2 says this ("creates knowledge for the learner") and its definitions entail it. Transmission moves custody; it never moves knowledge. (N1 §6 C2)

**T4. Knowledge and standing are orthogonal.** A system may withdraw standing from `x` everywhere by mistaken appraisal and adopt a worse `x′`; `x` remains created knowledge and `x′` is not. A system's knowledge and its usable premises can be disjoint. (N1 §6 C3)

**T5. Explanatory and physical knowledge are extensionally independent.** A false belief spread by uptake is self-preserving information without explanatory knowledge; a lost explanation was explanatory knowledge without surviving physical knowledge. They meet only in realization. (N1 §6 C4)

**T6. `Account` is independent of `Attempt`, `Progress`, and `Standing` in both directions.** An attempt can fail to account; an account can hold for a problem nobody has posed; progress can consist in a better question with no new account; an account can be possessed without progress. (N2 §3, §6 C2, C3)

**T7. `Account` is non-comparative; `Merit` is comparative.** A "best current explanation" record records merit and must expect to change without any change in the explanation. (N2 §6 C4)

**T8. `Account` is factive over work, not over commitments.** Supersession is non-retroactive. (N2 §5 B4, §6 C5)

**T9. Asymmetry.** `Because` is asymmetric where derivation is symmetric. The flagpole's height accounts for the shadow's length; the shadow's length entails but does not account for the flagpole's height. Covering-law theories are disposed of without argument. (N2 §4, §6 C7)

**T10. Overdetermination.** Work is attributed by minimal working subsets; two independently sufficient mechanisms both do work and neither is essential; singleton variation misclassifies them, subset variation does not. (N3 §4, §5 B3)

**T11. `Essential` entails `Work`; not conversely.** `Essential(d, u)` is the case where `d` belongs to the unique minimal working subset of `u`'s application. "Shown to do no work" and "withdrawn as essential" are different operations: the first is a criticism of quality, the second removes usability. (N3 §5 B4)

**T12. Constraint and reach are projections of work.** An explanation that reaches more is, by the same fact, harder to vary. (N3 §3, §6 C4)

**T13. Mode is posterior to work.** The open mode list is a consequence, not a gap. "Mood" fails for the seasons because it reaches nothing and constrains nothing. (N3 §3, §6 C3)

**T14. Target closure is closed over roles, not dependencies.** FW2's argument that E1 is not supplied by E0 ("target closure ranges over contents doing work in instances and a generator constraint fixes which instances can begin") succeeds only if "doing work in an instance" means `Deployed`, since the instance's outcome does depend on the constraint. Everything an instance depends on but does not deploy escapes closure: generator constraints (converted by E1), executed rules (conceded by K-RECURSION), and the instance boundary (eligible only once represented). The extensible class is the class in which the generator constraint has been converted from `DependsOn` to `Deployed` with return relevance to its standing. (N3 §2, §5 B1, §6 C6)

**T15. Operative role is a record fact; work is not.** A host can maintain the target-closure set (`Deployed` per instance) as a record; it cannot maintain the working set. E0 is trackable; E1 requires that a dependency the host cannot record become a role it can. This is the engineering shape of the E0/E1 gap. (N3 §6 C7)

**T16. The licensed count.** A count of conjecturally independent features reached orders accounts for preference, hence for standing in the next appraisal, and for nothing else. It is the only count in the theory with an effect, and the independence is itself a conjecture. This refines, without withdrawing, N1's prohibition on aggregation. (N3 §5 B6, §6 C8)

**T17. Warrant is eliminated.** No relation tracks truth by degree. What survive are adequacy (fact), merit (comparative fact), standing (conferred), and preference (the attitude an appraisal takes). "Why act on the preferred explanation" is not a warrant question; any answer is itself an explanation subject to the kernel. (N1 §6 C5)

**T18. Occurrence-level questions are ill-formed.** "Is this string knowledge?" and "does this string account for `p`?" have no answer until an interpretation `(ℓ, context, attributed system)` is fixed, and fixing it is a conjecture. K-REAL supplies facts about contents, not occurrences. (N1 §6 C6)

**T19. Roles do not sort knowledge.** A problem, criticism, standard, method, or limitation is knowledge relative to the situation it improved. "Knowledge" is not the role "explanation." (N1 §6 C7)

**T20. The single-primitive conjecture, if it holds, leaves one explanatory primitive.** `Account`, `Bearing`, `Progress`, and authorship are `Because` with stated relata; `Capacity` and `Deployed` are the two non-instances (one modal, one a record fact). (I.4)

# Part III. Disambiguations and repairs to FW2

## III.1 Five terms with two senses each

| Term in FW2 | Sense A | Sense B | Adopted terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| possession | custody of an occurrence (III "copier") | deployability in an explanatory role (K-ORIGIN, II newness) | custody / deployable |
| available | as a premise, i.e. standing (K-STANDING) | for use or reconstruction, i.e. repertoire (II progress) | premise-available / repertoire-available |
| reconstruction | first-layer regeneration of a conventional system, no kernel (II hierarchy, III discarding) | explanatory reconstruction that is understanding, kernel required (II UU, K-ORIGIN) | regeneration / reconstruction |
| account | the relation (II attempting) | a content deployed as an account, i.e. an attempt; also the interpreter's or application's obligation ("an account of the contribution") | `Account` / attempted account / the interpreter's explanation |
| work | why-dependence (`Account`, quality, reach, progress) | role occupancy in an instance (K-RECURSION, kernel, methods, hierarchy); also a contributor's contribution (II authorship) | work / deployed / contributes |

The first three doublings cluster at the occurrence/content seam; the last two at the object/meta seam. Both are where FW2's own commitments (K-PHYSICALITY's type/token distinction; K-RECURSION's self-application) predict least care.

## III.2 Seven textual repairs

Each is a change to prose, not to a displayed definition, except R6, which generalizes a display with FW2's form as its special case.

**R1. "Possession without authorship" (Part III, "Explanation, creativity, and success do not collapse").** Replace with "custody without authorship." Under FW2's definitions the copier either understands, and so reconstructively authors, or holds only an occurrence; on either reading the original label is false. (N1 §5 B1)

**R2. `Account`'s background argument (Part II, "Attempting an explanation and actually explaining").** Add: "`b` includes every element of `K` essential to `e`; a specification records these explicitly." FW2's two-balances defeater is the selection of a standard by the conclusion; standards live in `K`, which the display omits. (N2 §5 B1)

**R3. "A relation among representations" (same section).** Replace with "a relation of probabilistic dependency among hypotheses." As written the phrase would exclude `Bearing`, `Essential`, `Usable`, and `Account` itself, all of which relate contents. (N2 §5 B3)

**R4. The interpreter's obligation (same section).** Mark "An admissible interpretation must identify the explanatory work done by the commitments..." as a condition at the attribution standpoint, not on the relation. (N2 §6 C9)

**R5. K-RECURSION headline and the kernel, methods, and hierarchy sections.** Replace "doing explanatory or adjudicative work" and "doing work in an instance" with "deployed in an explanatory or adjudicative role" and "deployed in an instance." Reserve "work" for the `Account` sense. The E0/E1 argument depends on this. (N3 §5 B1, B2)

**R6. `FreeVariation` (Part II, "Explanatory quality, constraint, and reach").** Generalize the display to `FreeVariation_V(e, D; p, b)` for `D ⊆ Γ`, with the singleton as special case; state that the family ranges over subsets. (N3 §5 B3)

**R7. Prediction as ground versus constituent (Part II, criticism and attempting).** Add: "Predictions are grounds for `Bearing` and diagnostics for work; they are never constituents of `Account`." (N2 §5 B6)

## III.3 Two additions to the commentary on K-STANDING

Standing rests on appraisal acts that need no prior standing; the regress is broken by execution, not by a first standing. And preference, including preference licensed by the count of T16, licenses standing in the next appraisal and nothing else; "merits" in FW2's quality section is to be read as this and not as residual warrant. (N1 §5 B4, §6 C3, C5)

## III.4 One addition to Part II, "Physical realization"

Constraint 3 (retention) already requires that a realization "represent the set of live alternatives and the currently deployed one as an object." Add: the set of contents deployed in an instance is a record fact of the realization; the set of contents the instance's outcome depends on is not; and the difference between them is the set of dependencies that escape target closure at that boundary. (T14, T15)

# Part IV. Discriminating cases

Stated in FW2's form. Cases 1 to 4 are FW2's own, re-read; 5 to 12 are new.

**1. Seasons (holding witness for `Account` and `Work`).** Axial orientation, orbital relations, and illumination geometry form a minimal working subset for the seasons' phase: asymmetric, geometric mode, holding unrecognized, surviving recoding, destroyed by substituting mood, constraining "the hemispheres are out of phase," reaching the solstice dates and the polar day. Not fit, not a count, not the wording, not necessary alone, not true in every detail (circular orbits idealized). Every prohibition and every positive clause satisfied at once.

**2. Two balances (failure witness; feature-specificity; essential `K`).** "Trust balance B because its reading agrees with the proposed mass" is an attempt whose auxiliary is selected by its conclusion; `Account` fails by A2, whether or not the colleague objects. The readings do work for `b_B − b_A` and none for `x`: same commitments, two features, work for one. The defeater lives in a standard; R2 makes it visible.

**3. Relay and reconstructor (custody versus deployability; T3).** A relay emits a received explanation at `e_1` without reconstructing it and develops the understanding at `e_2`. At `e_1` it has custody and not deployability; at `e_2` it has deployability and an originative act at its own boundary. "Possession without authorship" describes `e_1` only if "possession" means custody (R1).

**4. Inherited target, original criticism (`Bearing` as `Account`).** The investigator's explanation of the inherited theory's defect is an account whose explanandum is the defect. If I.4 holds, the creative critical episode contains one relation twice, with different explananda.

**5. Flagpole (T9).** **Claim.** `Because` is asymmetric where derivation is symmetric. **Construction.** Height and sun's elevation entail shadow length; shadow length and sun's elevation entail height. The first is a minimal working subset for the shadow's length; the second is not one for the height, since nothing in it says why the flagpole has its height. **Consequence.** `Account` decides a case FW2 never discusses; derivability is excluded as sufficient.

**6. Two mechanisms (T10).** **Claim.** Work is attributed by subsets. **Construction.** One explanation offers `d` and `d′`, each sufficient for `f`. Singleton variation: removing either preserves the account; each is "free." Subset variation: removing both destroys it; `{d}` and `{d′}` are each minimal working subsets. **Consequence.** Both do work; neither is essential; the explanation is easier to vary than one with a single mechanism, which is the correct quality verdict.

**7. Generator constraint (T14).** **Claim.** An instance's outcome can depend on a content that does no operative work in it. **Construction.** The constraint fixes which conjectures can be formed; the outcome depends on it; it is deployed in no role within the instance. **Consequence.** `DependsOn(κ, d) ∧ ¬Deployed(d, κ, r)`. Target closure does not reach it; E1 converts it. Executed rules and boundaries are further members of the escaping set.

**8. Mistaken appraisal (T4).** **Claim.** Knowledge and standing can be disjoint. **Construction.** The system withdraws standing from `x` everywhere and adopts `x′`, which makes the situation worse. `x` is retained without standing and is created knowledge; `x′` has standing and is not. **Consequence.** No record state, and no appraisal, is knowledge; a specification derives neither from the other.

**9. Newton and general relativity (T8, transport).** **Claim.** Work survives supersession of ontology. **Construction.** The inverse-square dependence is a minimal working subset for closed orbits within the weak-field scope; action at a distance is not in it. General relativity's weak-field limit preserves the subset and supersedes the ontology; it also accounts for why the earlier account worked. **Consequence.** Newtonian mechanics accounts for orbits in its scope, and did not cease to; the transport carries the working subset and abandons the rest.

**10. Spreading falsehood and lost explanation (T5).** **Claim.** Explanatory and physical knowledge are independent. **Construction.** A false belief that spreads by uptake preserves its instantiation and accounts for nothing. An explanation that accounted for its feature and was lost preserved nothing. **Consequence.** Neither notion entails the other; they meet only in realization.

**11. Mood for seasons (T13).** **Claim.** Mode is decided by work. **Construction.** Substitute "the sun's mood" for axial tilt. The wording of an account is preserved; no minimal working subset survives; the antipodal phase is unreached. **Consequence.** "Mood" is not an appropriate mode for the seasons because commitments in it do no work, not because of a prior criterion of modes.

**12. Ill-posed problem (I.3, last clause).** **Claim.** `Account` fails, not lapses, when `p` has no relevant feature. **Construction.** An attempt is deployed as an account of a difficulty that a later appraisal shows to be an artifact of interpretation. There was no feature to do work on; A1 fails. **Consequence.** The available progress is a `Bearing` instance (the criticism of `p`) and a reformulation of `P` under transport; a specification records "failed," not "undefined."

# Part V. Specification discipline

A specification adopting this layer distinguishes, as FW2 does, a semantic assertion, an attribution judgment, a record fact, and a machine-check result. The deductions settle which of the relations above fall on which side.

**Record facts a specification may hold.**

1. Custody: occurrences with provenance.
2. Deployed roles: for each instance of `K`, which contents occupied which roles. This is target closure's domain and is machine-maintainable (T15).
3. Standing: per appraisal act, per use and respect.
4. Record states: no case, positive, negative, both; optional descriptions of the record.
5. Expositions: `⟨W, Γ, Δ, Σ, B_E⟩` as the proposer states it, with `Σ` and the essential elements of `K` recorded (R2).
6. Dependencies: `Essential(d, u)` per application.
7. Variation results over declared subsets of `Γ`, with the family's informativeness argued (R6).
8. Independence conjectures behind any count of features reached (T16).
9. Transport accounts: for each theory change, which minimal working subsets are claimed to survive, be revised, or be superseded.
10. Claims: appraisals alleging `Account`, `Work`, `Bearing`, `Progress`, `Merit`, or `Contributes`, each a content without standing until appraised.

**Fields a specification may not hold.** `knowledge`, `accounts_for`, `does_work`, `warrant`, `probability_of_truth`, or any field whose truth would be a semantic assertion. Each such claim is projected through an explicit bridge whose adequacy is open to criticism.

**Derived rules.**

- (S1) A record state describes the record, never the content.
- (S2) No record state is promoted to standing without a named appraisal act.
- (S3) Standing is never derived from `Account`, `Merit`, `Created`, or a record state, and none of those from standing.
- (S4) A change in `p`, `b`, `Σ`, or an essential element of `K` invalidates every claim indexed to the old values.
- (S5) "Shown to do no work" and "withdrawn as essential" are different operations with different effects on usability.
- (S6) A count of independent features reached licenses preference and nothing else.
- (S7) The target-closure set of an instance is its `Deployed` set; the escaping dependencies at the adopted boundary are listed with their conversion status (converted by E1, executed, or represented).
- (S8) An occurrence-level question is rejected until an interpretation is fixed as a conjecture.
- (S9) "Knowledge" in any projected claim names adequacy, merit, or created knowledge, and says which.
- (S10) A "best current explanation" record is a merit record and may change with no change in the explanation.

# Part VI. What would count against this layer

For each new claim, the condition under which it is refuted. Renaming an inconvenient case or changing scope silently would not answer any of these.

**K-BECAUSE as primitive** would be withdrawn if a reduction of why-dependence to a displayed relation (counterfactual, probabilistic, derivational, or interventionist) were found that survives asymmetry (case 5), overdetermination (case 6), ontology-tolerance (case 9), and mode-openness (case 11).

**Work by minimal working subsets** would need revision if a case were found in which subset variation still misclassifies a working commitment, or in which a commitment does work while belonging to no subset that stands in `Because`.

**`Account` factive over work, not commitments** would be withdrawn if it were shown that every case of work by a false commitment is a case in which a true sub-commitment does the work in the same mode, so that "the content that does work" is always true in scope (which would make the relation factive over the working content after all), or if FW2's five kinds of improvement could be read coherently under strict factivity without collapsing "worked" into prediction.

**The under-indexing repair (R2)** would be unnecessary if it were shown that every defeater of `Account` is representable within `B_E` alone; it would be insufficient if a defeater were found in `V` or `J` that neither `b` nor `B_E` can carry.

**Closure over roles (T14)** would be refuted by an organization shown to criticize, with return relevance, a dependency it never deploys and never converts to a deployable content.

**Acquisition is creation (T3)** would be refuted by a defensible case of deployable explanatory understanding acquired without reconstruction at the acquirer's boundary.

**The independence results (T4, T5, T6)** would each be refuted by an entailment in either direction: standing entailing knowledge, physical knowledge entailing explanatory or the converse, attempt or progress entailing `Account` or the converse.

**`Bearing` as `Account` on a defect** would be refuted by a criticism that bears without any why-dependence of the defect on its grounds. **`Progress` as gain in `Account` or `Bearing`** would be refuted by a defensible improvement that is neither, nor a reformulation admitting one. **Contribution as `Account` on provenance** would be refuted by a defensible attribution of authorship with no account of why the content has its organization.

**The licensed count (T16)** would be refuted if a count of independent reach were shown to be preference-irrelevant, or if some other count were shown to license more than preference.

**Mode posterior to work (T13)** would be refuted by an appropriate mode in which no commitment does work for any feature, or by a feature for which commitments in an inappropriate mode do work.

# Open questions

Consolidated from the three notes; those decided in this layer are marked.

1. Is why-dependence primitive by design, or does FW2 intend a reduction it has not stated? Its open mode list and its Part IV remaining question ("how do its parts do the claimed work") suggest the former. Open; the refutation condition above states what would settle it.
2. Should "available for use or reconstruction" in created knowledge be repertoire-availability (deployability by the attributed system) or the weaker availability that admits a library with no readers? This layer adopts repertoire-availability (I.5) and marks it as a decision.
3. Does `Account` fail or lapse when `p` is ill-posed? Decided: fails (I.3, case 12).
4. Do inexplicit contents satisfy the medium constraint of FW2's realization section, or is inexplicit inquiry always attributed at a boundary that includes explicit resources? Open.
5. What does preference license beyond standing in the next appraisal? Decided: nothing (III.3), pending a counterexample.
6. What is the feature, and hence the work, in the aesthetic case? Open, pending "the further aesthetic interpretation."
7. Do `Bearing`, `Contributes`, and `Progress` reduce to `Account`? Stated as conjectures (I.4); each has a refutation condition.
8. Which dependencies besides generator constraints escape target closure, and does each need a conversion clause like E1? Executed rules and boundaries are named; whether others exist at the boundaries of interest is open.
9. Is the count of independent features reached the only count the theory should license? Decided provisionally: yes (T16), pending a counterexample.
10. Should `Essential` be redefined as the singleton case of a minimal working subset, making the argument-side and explanation-side dependency notions one relation with a uniqueness condition? Proposed (T11); adoption requires checking every use of `Usable` in FW2.
