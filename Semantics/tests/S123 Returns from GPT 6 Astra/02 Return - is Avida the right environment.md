# Avida as an environment for investigating knowledge

Execution of Brief 02, dated 2 October 2026. Method: hard-to-vary assessment of the supplied claims, source inspection, and proposed discriminating tests. No Avida experiment was run.

**For the owner**

Avida can host tests of information that helps programs keep copying and persist. That does not yet establish learning during a program’s life, or the creation of explanations. A constant live feed is not essential: recorded observations can teach a system something it did not already contain. What matters is whether experience changes later behaviour, surviving tests that rule out copying answers or responses that ignore experience. Several supposed limits are features of the current setup, not limits of Avida: its source supports additional inputs, feedback, and optional inheritance of working state. Persistent moving objects and a record of how new connections are built need further work. The criterion differs for each meaning of knowledge. Avida remains an available test environment; the evidence does not yet select a platform for explanatory knowledge.

**Scope and evidence**

The question is whether an environment can supply the conditions for each proposed meaning of knowledge, and what observation would establish an instance at a stated scope. It is not a decision about which environment to adopt. The selector under test is the entire simulated Avida world, as the owner stipulates. Inspecting a program is an instrument for testing that world’s effects; it does not replace the owner’s system boundary.

**Premises that change the assessment**

The demand for *constant* external input is too strong as a universal condition. Consider a recorded sequence from an outside apparatus. Disconnect the apparatus after recording, and let experience with the recording alter a learner’s later responses. The contact occurred earlier; its causal contribution remains. Conversely, an uninterrupted sequence of independent random values can provide no useful regularity for the particular prediction being tested. This is an inference about necessity and sufficiency, not an experimental result. Continued informative contact becomes necessary for tracking changes that are both relevant and unavailable from retained information; its required frequency depends on those changes.

“Outside” needs a causal boundary. Messages from other programs in the same Avida world are outside one program but inside the owner’s selector. A recording of a physical apparatus originates outside that selector even after samples enter its buffers. A separate synthetic process may also be outside, if it is excluded from the declared boundary. A seed or independently written generator is not automatically outside merely because the learner cannot inspect it. Moving an unchanged generator between processes changes an attribution, not necessarily the learning problem.

The source contradicts the platform-wide claim that only instruction sequences can be inherited. `EPIGENETIC_METHOD` defaults to zero but supports inheritance of registers and local stack state; `DIVIDE_METHOD` also controls parent reset. This is a **checked source capability**, not evidence that useful learned content was inherited in any supplied run. Likewise, multiple input channels already exist. Their presence does not give their values meaning or supply a represented target.

Pinker’s argument that data underdetermine generalisation concerns constraints on candidate hypotheses **[checked: P2004, pp. 949–950]**. Avida already imposes constraints through its instructions, memory, variation and execution rules. Whether these are suitable constraints is a testable question; “no constraints” is not an adequate description. The second-channel claim in the attached note is explicitly Claude’s extrapolation from a language-learning case. A separate channel carrying meaning is not established there as a requirement for every kind of learning. Two logical channels can also be multiplexed through one interface. [P2004](https://stevenpinker.com/files/pinker/files/jcl_macwhinney_commentary.pdf)

A further tension belongs to the supplied record. The shared context says explanation has no instance in Avida, whereas the reader’s guide, section 6, reports a whole-program account of a task under Reading A. These can concern different questions and grains, but the broad negative cannot simply be inherited. This report makes no new attribution of explanation and does not execute Brief 04.

**Hypothesis and its rival**

The working hypothesis is that the current setup limits what the project can observe more narrowly than Avida’s substrate does. Stock channels can admit outside observations and retain working state. A bounded extension could add delayed outcome checking and an object-world assay without replacing the instruction machine. These changes would make tests of external-data adaptation and within-life learning possible. They would not, by themselves, establish construction, explanatory knowledge, or an unnamed standard of success.

This is a design hypothesis, not a result. Its operational part would be challenged if a hand-authored virtual program could not receive separated cue and outcome information, retain a distinction, and use it on a later probe under the stipulated stock configuration. The “bounded extension” part would be challenged if the assay required replacing the scheduler, instruction meanings or population model instead of adding a data interface and observable state. The hypothesised learning mechanism would be challenged if retained-experience and erased-experience conditions behaved alike after interface and feedback checks succeeded.

The rival is that performance comes from responses that ignore experience, leaked answers, the update clock, event order or a knowledgeable external driver. It predicts that the apparent advantage survives removing the informative past, or follows those incidental variables rather than the source relation. Identical current inputs after different informative histories, plus targeted state erasure and sham erasure, discriminate these explanations. A fixed learning algorithm can pass those tests: being fixed does not prevent it from learning. An inherited adaptive learner may still fail the separate Build criterion. Failure to evolve a learner in a limited run challenges that search configuration; it does not establish a substrate impossibility.

None of the four alleged impossibilities follows from the stated reason alone. The genuine boundary is not the name “Avida”; it is which substrate and causal commitments the experiment promises to preserve. This report proposes an explicit compatibility contract: retain the virtual instruction semantics, replication inside the simulation, and the population/scheduling model, while adding bounded observation, action and record interfaces. This contract is **added**, not the owner’s settled definition of platform identity. Changing instruction availability already creates a new experimental condition even without recompilation.

A mechanism can be emergent without escaping implemented causal routes. If “no channel written to carry that effect” means no implemented route whatsoever, an effect through that absent route is unavailable in the model. If it means no task-specific route naming the successful capability in advance, generic resource competition or interpreted rule data could qualify for investigation. This is a distinction between the two readings, not a decision to adopt either. Grade 3 cannot be inferred from an unfamiliar output, a generic equality comparator or a rule stored in mutable data.

**Tests to hand back for execution**

All tests below are proposals. No stream, patch or experimental configuration was implemented in this execution. Their controls are intended to discriminate causes, not to add up confirmations. Freeze the source, build, active instruction list, population, seeds, event schema, dataset split, reward rule and evaluation contract before the first measurement. Record first outcomes and retain later repairs as new versions. Interface acceptance uses one separate development fixture, followed by untouched evaluation records, including an uninformative case.

**Outside-input test**

Use an independently collected recording of an outside apparatus that emits a cue followed by an observable binary outcome. A simple apparatus with two cue classes and an unknown cue-to-outcome relation is sufficient for an initial assay. Its relation must not be implemented in the Avida task checker. Retain the raw recording, collection provenance and fixed conversion to events. Reserve later recording sessions for evaluation; splitting neighbouring frames at random would permit temporal leakage. This tests learning a particular outside relation, not open-ended learning.

For ingress alone, schedule tagged inputs through stock events and inspect a virtual program’s receive trace against the recording. Do not reward a standard NAND task and relabel it external learning. For the learning assay, a small adapter should expose the cue, accept one committed forecast, then disclose the recorded outcome. The output-first order of `IO` must be respected; the first output must be ignored or explicitly assigned to a previous frame. Per-program sample advancement prevents faster replication, extra reads and skipped difficult records from changing which cases are scored. A non-answer consumes its allotted opportunity and is recorded.

The outcome channel must be inaccessible before commitment, including through buffers, reward timing, episode IDs, file offsets or diagnostic data. Use bounded pay per completed record with matched processing opportunities. The read-only comparator is allowed to contain the success criterion; it must not compute the source’s cue-outcome relation. Under the whole-world boundary it is part of the selector. This distinction tests externally supplied data, not absence of all designer choices, and leaves Reading A/B intact.

Expected result under the working hypothesis: exposure to the informative recording produces a retained cue-dependent advantage on separate sessions over the actual starting population and matched controls. Controls preserve cue/outcome marginals and timing while breaking their association, withhold informative pay, or yoke pay to another run. A constant-output and a read-counting predictor are explicit rivals. A copied population evaluated without further selection distinguishes retained adaptation from improvement occurring during evaluation. **Learning within a program’s life**

Use the same frozen instruction sequence and identical initial writable VM state for paired evaluations, with inherited working state disabled or explicitly matched. At birth assign an association between two arbitrary cues and two actions; keep it stable during that program’s life. Early trials disclose action consequences. Later probes present only the cue, with feedback delayed until after commitment. Pair two instances with opposite early associations but the same current cue, age, number of reads and computational allowance. They should act differently for a reason located in retained experience. Disable replication and instruction variation during these evaluations.

During evolutionary search, compare an association fixed across births, one resampled at each birth but stable within life, and one resampled on every encounter. The first permits fixed responses; the second makes experience potentially useful; the third removes the predictive value of earlier associations. Add a later reversal within life only as a separately declared condition, not a rescue after a failed test. Keep per-program episodes private: global events alone do not give independent birth-relative cue mappings to asynchronously dividing programs.

Controls erase the particular candidate memory after learning, perform a sham erasure of matched unrelated state, or replace feedback with a yoked sequence. Probe a later first action rather than eventual task completion, which a fixed error-recovery routine might achieve. For a content-preserving recoding, translate the relevant carriers, stored bindings and transport consistently, or provide the decoding convention. Changing only incoming cue symbols tests a new association instead. Memory erasure must not destroy instruction flow or general execution; otherwise it tests a damaged machine rather than dependence on learned content.

Expected result: the life-stable condition permits an experience-dependent response; the across-births fixed condition can be solved by reflexes; the encounter-random condition should not show a systematic benefit from earlier associations. These are mechanism predictions, not a prediction that evolutionary search must find learners in a given budget.

**Persistent objects and surprise**

The initial test is a thought experiment and interface design. Use two objects on a short line of cells, with positions, velocities and a visibility mask. Moving an object changes its future position; occluding it changes observation while leaving its motion intact. Commit the forecast before the next observation. Vary displacement, velocity, occlusion length and source encoding. Keep IDs and hidden positions available to the auditor but out of the program’s observation. Merely handing over those values would establish a supplied object description, not its acquisition.

Stock state grids can support spatial samples and program movement, but a grid does not by itself supply moving objects, independent occlusion or a learned object layer. A small world-state extension could provide those target conditions. To claim the learner has P and S, identify its retained components and show appropriate displacement responses, continued motion through occlusion, and invariance under a change that affects visibility alone. This mapping is separate from the experimenter’s knowledge that the world contains objects.

For a D12.7 surprise trial, first identify a forecast transport selected on visible-motion histories H. Freeze a wider contract C that already includes occlusion, then expose an occlusion absent from H. If the forecast fails when the object reappears and the layer/provenance conditions hold, this is surprise under Reading A. If it predicts correctly, the trial contains no surprise. Under Reading B a declared forecast can fail but does not meet that selected-provenance condition. A later failure of a constructed forecast is a violation under the definition.

The discriminating control is not merely a longer blank interval. Construct two histories with identical final w observations during occlusion but different earlier position or velocity, requiring different next observations. A deterministic predictor restricted to those w observations must answer alike in both, so cannot answer both correctly. This is the limited-population obstruction in Argument 10. It does **not** apply to all Avida programs: a program with persistent memory may retain earlier information. Fix and inspect the claimed population restriction before using the argument.

A trajectory swap that leaves all admitted observations and predictions unchanged supplies no evidence of unique identity binding. Add an independently visible distinguishing feature or an identity-sensitive intervention only if identity discrimination is a new, explicitly stated job. Plain replay, a source-side object table and a hand-written persistence rule remain controls, not construction. Evidence of Build would additionally require the trace described in the criteria table.

What counts against the world interface: a supposedly visibility-only edit actually deletes or changes the target object. What counts against a claimed persistence model after repair: its forecast treats occlusion as destruction, depends on leaked IDs, or is unaffected by erasing the alleged operative persistence state. A failure under recoding is diagnostic only when carriers, stored bindings and transport have been translated consistently. Failure of the initial S0 forecast under occlusion is instead the proposed surprise event; it is not itself an interface failure. Failure of learning after the interface witness passes leaves the search and representation assumptions open.

New C++: **probably required for dynamic objects and an independent visibility mask in this specific grid interface; an external world adapter is another route**. CPU-hours for the thought test: **0**. A proposed first dynamic pilot of eight 20,000-update runs has baseline `2.8 × r₃` CPU-hours, not a prediction of its actual cost. Time the object update and trace overhead first. No migration decision follows from this budget.

Several whole-claim checks matter. Flip the outcome: neither learner emergence nor failure can be explained simply by saying “Avida has no outside world.” The external-data hypothesis predicts particular losses under informative-history removal; if either outcome can be relabelled a success, the criterion has drifted. Reverse the causal test: copying a successful program into a fresh world, retaining a driver’s state, and resetting a program’s state are different interventions. The first can locate retained adaptive information, the second can expose an outside learner, and the third can isolate a within-life contribution. None is performed here.

The starting points must not contain the answer. A task checker that encodes the source relation, hidden outcome IDs, an object identity fed straight into a register or a driver-written persistence model can satisfy an output test without the alleged learning or construction. Gauge these possibilities by auditing exactly what each component receives before commitment and which component updates after a failure. Generic correctness pay still names a standard and does not become grade 3 through generality.

There are also interacting costs. Stronger feedback makes the cue task learnable but can disclose the answer. Richer sensors can make prediction easier but bypass the object-layer question. Inheriting working state may aid retention while confounding acquisition within the new program’s life. Preserving Avida’s instruction/population semantics aids comparison with saved populations but constrains an object-world design. These tensions are handled by explicitly separated conditions, not by assigning one global score to environments.

The supplied H5 also needs a logical correction. Failure to learn after outside input is added counts against its sufficiency in that setup; it does not refute the claim that outside input is necessary. A controlled learning case without the proposed outside source challenges necessity. Equal results for causally equivalent internal and external streams show that source location does not by itself explain performance. For construction, unfamiliar-case success does not defeat the selected alternative: Argument 3 explicitly allows unseen values to be constrained by the population. Trace evidence remains indispensable to the stipulated provenance distinction.

The audit leaves the exact meaning of knowledge, Reading A/B, the driver’s boundary and S76’s state/rules reading open. It does not establish an instance of explanatory knowledge, a platform impossibility, or that the hard-to-vary method certifies a theory. Its next proposed action is one bounded outside-input witness: retain a raw recording, establish output-before-outcome ordering, and test informative versus scrambled histories before commissioning a population run. That is a proposal for the next experiment, not an instruction to launch costly runs or select a platform.
