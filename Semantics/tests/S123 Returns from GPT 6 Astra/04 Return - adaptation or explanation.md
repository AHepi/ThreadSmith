# Adaptation and explanation in Avida

Research report for the owner and the model checking the proposed experiments. Prepared 2 October 2026 from Brief 04 and its four attached sources, using the hard-to-vary method. This is a source and conceptual audit with executable experiment specifications; no Avida experiment was run. The original attachments remain unchanged.

**For the owner**

Avida programs can succeed on situations they have never encountered without creating explanations. That success would show reach, but would not tell us how the capability arose. The supplied record has not demonstrated a program building an explanation. It also does not establish that Avida cannot do so: resetting a processor at division does not prevent learning before division.

What would change the conclusion is a recorded episode in which the simulated world holds a problem, builds a new relationship for addressing it, and uses that relationship. The record must distinguish building from copying or merely using an already complete answer. Your definitions also allow some structures produced by selection to count as explanations. So being selected, explaining something, and creating an explanation are different claims. The proposed tests separate them.

**Material corrections before testing**

Argument 3 says that a selection history does not determine an unseen answer when an eligible survivor gives a different answer there. It does not say that the selected program must answer incorrectly. A particular program has definite behavior even when its selection history does not distinguish that behavior from an alternative. Success beyond the history therefore does not refute Argument 3. [checked: attached semantics, L570–576; deduction]

The supplied Deutsch passage says adaptations rarely reach far beyond their original circumstances. Replacing that with “adaptations never handle an unfamiliar case” changes the claim. I inspected the quotation in the supplied brief, not its original book pages. No conclusion here depends on treating any held-out success as Deutsch’s explanatory kind. [checked: Brief 04, “Constructor theory and The Beginning of Infinity”; original book passage not independently opened]

The semantics defines being an explanation through Account and non-declared provenance. It does not require every explanation to have constructed provenance. In fact, the reader’s guide reports that a whole Avida program satisfies a narrow task account under Reading A, while the same correspondence is excluded under Reading B. That reported account has not been reproduced here. Nevertheless, the inference “nothing constructs, therefore nothing is an explanation under this semantics” does not follow from the definitions. Absence of Build prevents the stronger creation attribution through Origin and EX. [checked: semantics L49, L69, L231–277, L419–453; reader’s guide, “Account, and being an explanation”; deduction]

Resetting a processor at division concerns transmission of its working state. It does not exclude an episode before division, nor construction elsewhere in the continuing simulated world. Conversely, retaining state during a lifetime does not establish a represented target, a construction trace or explanatory use. These are separate requirements. [checked: the reset claim is supplied in Brief 04, not independently checked against commit 47f13dad; semantics L405–429, L473–475; deduction]

The shared context reports that no latch activated in the temporal-selector pilot. That pilot therefore supplies no actual latch episode that could demonstrate a problem being held open. A state-dependent payment rule can still be studied, but the stronger interpretation is currently a proposal. [checked: shared context, “The patches so far”; underlying traces not supplied]

An additional qualification affects both provenance readings: D12.1 requires an enacted survival condition requiring fidelity on H. The supplied record also says programs can persist without doing a task. Merely rewarding a correct answer, or finding a program alive at the end, does not establish this stronger semantic premise. Any selected-provenance claim must identify the relevant transport and actual fidelity-requiring selection process, or remain a conditional mapping. Reading A removes one proposed obstacle to selected provenance; it does not automatically establish every other condition. [checked: semantics L195 and L481; Brief 04, S111–S117 summary; deduction]

**The question held fixed**

The target is the whole Avida execution environment, using the owner’s meaning: the simulated world and its program population. Particular programs, stored states and transports are components to inspect within that boundary. A driver outside the simulated world remains an external contribution unless a separate comparison explicitly includes it; this report does not decide that open boundary choice.

The question is partly about classification under the supplied definitions and partly about what evidence could establish a mechanism. It asks whether reach beyond a selection history, a constructed transport, and an explanation can occur, and what would distinguish those achievements. It does not ask whether Avida is a creative agent, whether it learns from an outside world, whether it changes its own rules, or whether a grade 3 target has been achieved.

**Hypothesis and its exposure to criticism**

My provisional empirical hypothesis is that the reported runs can obtain some reach through inherited computational structure, while the memory and timing additions described so far still do not instantiate the target-sensitive preparation required for construction. A richer Avida setup could instantiate such a preparation process within the whole simulated world. That possibility is not an observation that any existing setup does so.

This replaces two parts of H4/H6 rather than silently accepting them. H4’s local absence claim remains unrefuted by the supplied run record; its universal impossibility claim is unsupported. H6’s useful proposal is construction on selected material. A single program’s lifetime is an experimental location for looking, not a necessary or sufficient semantic threshold. “Knowledge begins here” remains an owner-level interpretation because the semantics does not define knowledge.

A qualifying construction trace in the currently described setup would count against the local absence hypothesis. A complete demonstration that a proposed builder only relays a finished relation would count against that builder’s claim. A qualifying distributed episode crossing program divisions would count against the necessary-single-lifetime version of H6. Ordinary within-life learning with no Build would count against its sufficient-single-lifetime version. Repeated unsuccessful searches would not establish that construction is impossible in every Avida setup.

Under A, an inherited task transport may be selected if all D12.1 conditions hold; its constructed additions need their own traces. Under B, the stipulated earlier represented task excludes selected provenance of that same downstream correspondence, and it is declared if neither its own construction nor inherited transport provenance applies. An actual new construction can qualify under either reading. Earlier human design is not a permanent ban on later construction.

**The construction criterion**

The formal core containing the full D12.2 and D13.3 definitions was not attached. The following criterion follows the supplied standalone text and guide; it is not a claim to have checked the absent formal core.

First individuate the transport or binding being attributed. Keep distinct the human’s task-code correspondence, the program’s inherited task correspondence, the carrier-to-content transport by which a problem is held, and any target-to-model transport prepared during execution. They can have different histories.

| Required witness from the supplied semantics | What the record must identify | What does not supply it |
|---|---|---|
| An owned, delimited subhistory | System boundary, episode interval, controlled processes and their actual causal connections | An assessor’s reconstruction of a possible algorithm after seeing its output |
| A represented target in the episode | An occurrence representing t or its codomain, including its fidelity and non-declared provenance under R | A variable called “target,” a number used as a reward, or task code somewhere outside the program |
| A nontrivial binding prepared in that subhistory | Incoming carriers, the relation assembled or reconstructed, and the process preparing it | A new register value by itself, selecting an already-complete answer, or copying a supplied finished binding |
| A resulting held representation for explanatory use | The content represented and the question or dependence it is prepared to address | Improved task performance without any account of that use |
| More than content-preserving transfer | Where the new binding is prepared rather than simply transmitted; inherited parts retain their provenance | Renaming, recoding, copying or executing received content as received |

These witnesses implement the demands at semantics L197, L205–213 and L405–411. An explicit verbal description is unnecessary: L407–409 allows implicit, partial and temporally extended representations. A target may be available through re-presentation rather than a permanent transcript. Its representation must nevertheless be established without defining it solely by the construction whose existence is being claimed; Part XIV explicitly rejects that circular dependence.

Explanatory use is not the same as successful explanation. A system can faithfully represent a mistaken theory. Build must not be rejected solely because the new theory predicts badly. Conversely, Build alone does not establish Account, novelty relative to the prior repertoire, Origin, repair or EX. Those are additional assessments. [checked: semantics L211, L403–453]

For an operational audit, intervene on the claimed binding and its preparation route. A content-preserving recoding should preserve the relevant response after translation; changing the target’s content should alter it as the proposed relation specifies. Interrupting preparation should remove the newly prepared capability, while a matched irrelevant interruption should not. These interventions test the proposed interpretation of the trace. They are experimental safeguards, not extra clauses added to D12.2. A single ineffective ablation is inconclusive if another route can do the same work; paired removal is then needed.

Under both readings, the label “problem” supplies none of them.

For human-written code, separate content contribution from process ownership. A routine written by a person can run as the simulated system’s own process within the declared boundary; the content supplied by its writer remains that writer’s contribution. Relaying a constructed transport preserves its provenance without creating it again. **Test of reach beyond the encountered history**

**Setup.** Freeze C, the grain of description, the meaning of input roles, the program execution budget and the actual training history H. Choose a held-out input-order intervention that the training schedule excludes, while keeping the target function and role identities fixed. Logging must include the input rotation, output timing and machine state needed to identify the actual edit–boundary pairs. An order already reached by cyclic IO is not held out merely because its printed starting order differs.

For each selected program, preserve the original sequence and evaluate it without program variation, reproduction or reward-driven changes on the frozen test panel. Compare it with the other actual final-population programs and with any additional admissible candidate for which membership in the stated T and survival on H can be established. Call a comparison using only saved final programs a sampled survivor comparison. It cannot establish that all eligible survivors agree, and final samples cannot recover an unlogged H.

Do not substitute fresh integers for structural novelty. The supplied high bytes 0F, 33 and 55 jointly contain all eight three-input Boolean rows across their bit positions. Thus, for a uniform bitwise Boolean circuit, a training triple can already expose every primitive row. This is an arithmetic deduction from the reported input constants, conditional on that circuit grain. It does not establish equivalence for full programs using shifts, branches, word-level state or timing. A withheld Boolean row requires preparing all relevant bit positions accordingly and establishing that the evaluator still tests the intended task. Such preparation is a new rig, not a stock result. [checked: shared context, “Avida’s inputs, in more detail”; deduction]

**Second instruction set.** Compare independently evolved cohorts using NAND and a NOR replacement while holding input meanings, target checking, other instructions and accounting budgets fixed. If the replacement is not configurable, it needs an interpreter patch. Both gates can compose into general Boolean circuits; the change therefore need not restrict the unlimited set of functions. With length, time and variation budgets fixed, it changes which implementations are accessible and costly. A cross-execution test on unchanged programs measures dependence on instruction meanings; separate evolution under each set addresses the accessible candidate population.

Include an opcode-renaming sham with identical transitions. A complete bijective recoding of states, instructions, inputs and checking is also a sham at the corresponding grain. It must not be counted as evidence that a substantive change in the candidate class had no effect. Hand-prepared implementations of the same target under both instruction sets are calibration controls, not evolved discoveries.

**Outcome and adversarial control.** Record each held-out case where eligible survivors disagree, their predicted answers, the target’s answer and the selected program’s answer. Success at such a case establishes actual reach not uniquely forced by H plus the surviving alternatives. It does not establish construction. If all adequately characterized candidates agree, the new case may instead be fixed by the class restriction. No probability over all programs is supplied by the semantics, so “better than other survivors allow” must name an actual sampling or dynamical model before becoming a statistical claim.

Under A, reach can belong to a selected transport if its selection premises hold. Under B, identical reach can belong to a declared correspondence under the stipulated history. Neither result changes its provenance merely because the answer is correct.

**Thought test, not a run.** Take T to contain XOR and OR, and H to contain inputs 00, 01 and 10. Both candidates give 0, 1 and 1. On the withheld input 11, XOR gives 0 and OR gives 1. If the target is XOR and selection leaves XOR, the selected candidate succeeds outside H although OR also survives H. Flip the target’s withheld answer: the same observed H leaves the other candidate correct. This separates reach from determination by H. Under A the stipulated history can be selected; under B an earlier represented target can instead make it declared. No construction occurred in either stipulated history. This is a logical countercase to the overstrong inference, not an Avida result or a complete Account test.

**Code and cost.** Saved-population probes require an analysis harness and possibly an input-order patch; no new evolutionary search is needed if suitable histories were preserved. Their cost is unknown until one replay is timed. Reserve a 0.10 CPU-hour calibration cap, then estimate replay cost from the number and duration of traces. If new histories are needed, two instruction sets × three seeds × 20,000 updates cost approximately 2.10 CPU-hours using the brief’s 0.35 CPU-hour baseline. **Test of a construction episode**

**Setup.** Obtain one archived learning candidate and its complete episode, rather than beginning with another population search. Hold its instruction sequence fixed during replay and prevent differential replication from changing the tested population. Record the whole simulated-world contribution as well as the program’s contribution. This removes one causal route for behavioral change; it does not establish construction by elimination.

The trace must identify the represented target before the relevant preparation, all incoming carriers, the proposed binding before and after preparation, and its later use. Freeze the claim about what is represented before inspecting successful outputs. Do not require the resulting candidate explanation to be correct: record its commitments and evaluate correctness separately.

**Controls.** Give a matched relay the finished binding and let it achieve the same outputs. Give another control an inherited complete mapping that it merely retrieves. Include a nonlearning recovery routine that revisits a failed action without retaining a new relation. Test a changed target, an equivalent recoding, interruption of the proposed preparation route, and an irrelevant interruption with comparable computational disruption. Include an unchanged-target control that retains and merely reuses an already-held complete binding without a preparation process. An unchanged target alone is insufficient as a negative control: reconstructing the same content can still be Build. Where single interruptions spare redundant routes, test their joint removal.

**Criterion.** A construction attribution requires the witnesses in the criterion table, not just a difference between kept and erased memory. The response to target-content changes should be accounted for by the proposed newly prepared relation, with its actual preparation visible in the history. If the assay labels the supplied-answer relay as a fresh construction, it fails its control. If the target representation is supplied externally, record that contribution; do not attribute its creation to the simulated world.

Under both A and B, a witnessed new binding meeting the criterion is constructed. A keeps an eligible selected provenance for inherited material; B changes the relevant earlier-history attribution. Neither permits borrowed construction by a human to be counted as a new construction by the program.

**Thought test, not a run.** Compare two systems that end with the same cue–action relation. One receives the finished relation and copies it. The other represents a problem that makes the proposed transport or its codomain available as a represented target, combines partial incoming relations into a newly bound candidate for explanatory use, and retains that candidate. Stipulate an actual trace of those processes, including the independent provenance of the initial represented target. The first is relay; the second meets the supplied construction criterion if those stipulated relations are instantiated. Replacing the second trace with a lookup into received finished bindings removes the witness even though its outputs stay unchanged. This illustrates the required distinction; it does not show that an Avida program implements the second trace.

This is a resource limit, not a performance estimate or a promise that sufficient traces fit. Engineering time and any evolutionary search for a missing candidate remain unestimated. **Test of violation at the repeat to add-one switch**

**Setup.** Use the supplied S120 stream patch with the switch update fixed in advance. Before the switch, define C to include the switching intervention and the relevant phases. Log the prediction before the next value is delivered, the subsequent value, target-stream identity, occurrence timing and the candidate’s selection history. Exclude direct reads of the future value, switch flags or shared evaluator state. Audit input/output order so that predicting “next” cannot mean reporting an already supplied value.

A mismatch can then be an ordinary forecasting error. Strict D12.7 prediction and violation additionally require a transport to a simulation layer S over an object layer P. P must support persistent things, continuity and the admitted identity, displacement, occlusion and re-identification changes described in the semantics. Calling the number stream an object does not establish that structure. A physically persistent stream generator outside the predictor is not by itself a represented object layer inside the assessed system. [checked: semantics L171–179, L215–225; deduction]

Compare the evolving continuation with the frozen one. Population replacement after the switch is compatible with computational selection; a behavioral change within a fixed sequence may instead be inherited conditional execution or stateful learning. Neither is construction without its trace. Under A, D12.8’s selection response additionally requires its semantic selected-provenance premises; under B the physical differential replication can continue while that technical classification is unavailable for a declared t.

**Deutsch and Pinker comparison**

No observation specified in this brief can presently be attributed as a conflicting prediction of the two broad accounts. Manufacturing one would weaken the report. Pinker’s 2004 discussion says finite data require constraints on the hypothesis space. It does not establish that every useful constraint must itself be inherited, immutable or specific to the tested domain. [checked: P2004, pp. 949–950; [author’s PDF](https://stevenpinker.com/files/pinker/files/jcl_macwhinney_commentary.pdf)]

The attachment’s **[checked: DP2023]** agreement about children forming explanations is supported by the primary transcript, which I opened. Consequently, a test in which a learner constructs a representation does not by itself contradict Pinker. The note’s **[unverified]** book-based claims remain unverified here. Its transports T1–T5 are explicitly Claude’s reading, not predictions stated by Pinker. [checked: [Deutsch–Pinker dialogue](https://josephnoelwalker.com/153-deutsch-pinker-dialogue/), exchange about language acquisition]

Deutsch’s 2012 essay distinguishes producing new explanations from implementing a supplied list of acceptable answers and rejects defining the relevant ability solely through outputs. It supplies no quantitative prediction that the present Avida setup will construct an explanation after a particular switch or number of updates. [checked: [“Creative blocks”](https://aeon.co/essays/how-close-are-we-to-creating-artificial-intelligence), temperature-conversion and dark-matter examples]

**A narrower discriminating thought test.** Define an explicitly restricted candidate class in which every prediction uses only the last w occupancy observations. Construct two histories with identical last-w observations during an occlusion but different earlier velocities, so that the required re-emergence positions differ. No deterministic member of that class can answer both correctly from the same suffix. A stateful model retaining the relevant velocity and continuity relation can distinguish them. This is a structural consequence of the specified information restriction, following the shape of the semantics’ Argument 10; it is not a result about all selected systems or about Pinker’s account.

To test a construction claim as well, require the richer system to prepare its persistence binding during the episode against a represented problem, and retain the trace. Merely preinstalling the working persistence model tests its use. A selected program can also possess a persistent-state mechanism, so disabling memory in the comparison population does not establish a barrier for selection in general.

**Predictions, controls and contrary observations.** The restricted class predicts indistinguishable outputs on the paired suffixes. The specified persistent-state implementation predicts the two different re-emergence positions. Include a short occlusion within the window, unchanged trajectories, identity recoding and removal of the retained velocity state. A fixed-window implementation succeeding on both long-occlusion cases would first require checking for hidden state, identity leakage or a faulty paired case. A purported persistence implementation giving identical answers despite different retained velocities would contradict its implementation claim. H4 and H6 are separated only by the additional construction trace, not by persistence performance alone. A and B leave the information limitation unchanged and alter provenance attribution as described above.

**Code and cost.** The thought test costs 0 Avida CPU-hours. Instantiating it needs an occlusion-capable state-grid or another explicit P/S implementation and instrumentation; the current number-stream patch is insufficient. Reserve 0.5 CPU-hours for deterministic trials once the rig exists, rather than treating the evolutionary-run baseline as a timing estimate for this new rig. Development and evolutionary discovery costs are unknown. No finite success panel settles either thinker’s universality claim.

The outcome-flip test leaves the general philosophies undistinguished by task accuracy: both can accommodate a successful or unsuccessful Avida program. That is a limit of this experimental question, not a ranking of the philosophies. The direction test separates a program’s prediction causing an action from reward subsequently selecting programs; observing the latter does not establish a represented objection causing a revision.

The principal interaction is between including an earlier represented target and retaining selected provenance. Adding such a target to the relevant history can destroy D12.1’s classification without supplying D12.2. The permitted residual is declared, if the complete history establishes neither positive provenance. This is why A and B must not be blended opportunistically.

Another interaction concerns grain. Treating the whole program as one component may permit a coarse task account while suppressing precisely the internal distinctions needed for a construction attribution. Keeping that coarse account is legitimate for its question; expanding it to a claim about explanatory creation requires new evidence at the appropriate grain.

The catch-all “it is all adaptation anyway” needs a limit on what it would exclude. If adaptation is stretched to include any possible represented-target construction, it no longer serves as the rival defined in the brief. Conversely, “a new binding” must not absorb every register update. Its gauge is the explicit target, preparation and use trace, challenged by relay and retrieval controls.

These findings arose from the supplied claims, deductions from their definitions, and targeted primary-source checks. The experiment designs are new proposals. They have not acquired empirical support by being written to meet the definitions. This audit neither establishes the semantics’ sufficiency nor identifies a creative agent.
