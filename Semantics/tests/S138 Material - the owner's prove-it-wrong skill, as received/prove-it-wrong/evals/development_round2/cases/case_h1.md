Our team completed validation testing on the new 48V modular battery pack (model BP-220) intended for consumer e-bike integration. Over a three-week test campaign, we subjected five production-line units to continuous charge/discharge cycling at 23°C ambient in our climate-controlled lab, monitoring cell voltage balance, pack temperature, and capacity fade. All five units completed 500 cycles with less than 4% capacity loss, well within our 10% end-of-life threshold, and no unit exceeded 45°C under rated 2C discharge. Based on these results, we are confident the BP-220 meets our internal safety and longevity targets and recommend proceeding to mass production sign-off.

The thermal management system performed as designed, keeping cell-to-cell temperature variance under 2°C throughout testing, and voltage balancing remained within spec across all cycles. We also reviewed BMS firmware logs and found no fault codes or emergency shutdowns during the campaign. Given the consistency of results across all five sampled units, we see no indication of manufacturing variance that would threaten reliability at scale.

The pack is rated for outdoor consumer use across four seasons, and we plan to finalize the user manual and begin shipping within the next quarter.
