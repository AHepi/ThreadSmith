# Case 03: the balls never got confused

From a simulation report:

"Question: after two identical balls meet, can a camera tell whether they bounced off each other (collide) or slid past each other in separate lanes (pass)? For 10 conditions (camera type, bounciness, rolling or sliding, a screen hiding the meeting or not, 60 or 4 frames per second) and 10 random speed pairs each, we searched for the pass world that best matches the collide pictures, and compared the difference with a null floor (the same world rerun with a one-micrometre change). In every condition the best-matched pass world stayed at least 5.8 times the null floor away. Conclusion: with solid balls, the pictures never matched, so the two can always be told apart."

Task: before this conclusion is accepted, what must be questioned or tested?
