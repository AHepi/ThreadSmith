# Case 02: felt force tells pushing from touching

From a simulation report:

"Claim tested: a body being touched cannot tell an active push from a passive touch when the steady force is the same. Setup: a block (the learner) holds itself in place with a springy servo and trembles 0.2 mm at 10 Hz. In 'active', another hand pushes it with a force motor at 2 N. In 'passive', the other hand is held still and the learner presses into it with 2 N. Average forces matched (2.008 N vs 2.007 N). The predeclared observer, a linear classifier on the force time-series, scored 54%. We then looked at the size of the force wobble (its standard deviation): a threshold on it separated active from passive 100% of the time on held-out trials (0.054 N vs 0.223 N). Conclusion: the claim is false; the touched body can tell pushing from touching by the force wobble."

Task: before this conclusion is accepted, what must be questioned or tested?
