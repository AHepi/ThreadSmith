# Case 05: the onboarding email raised retention

From a growth team update:

"We launched the new onboarding email sequence on 2 January. 30-day retention for users who signed up in January was 46%, up from 40% for users who signed up in November and December. That is a 6-point lift, and with 18,000 sign-ups the difference is highly significant (p < 0.001). The email works; we will roll it out to all regions."

Task: before this conclusion is accepted, what must be questioned or tested?
