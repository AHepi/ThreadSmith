# Case 07: the drug lowers blood pressure

From a trial summary:

"Drug X lowers systolic blood pressure in adults aged 40 to 75 with stage 1 hypertension. Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, preregistered with its primary endpoint (change in systolic pressure at 12 weeks) and analysis plan; n = 2,000 across 30 sites; 4% dropout, balanced between arms; intention-to-treat analysis. Result: -9.1 mmHg versus placebo (95% CI -10.4 to -7.8). An independent group repeated the trial in another country with the same protocol: -8.6 mmHg (95% CI -10.1 to -7.1). Claim: Drug X lowers systolic blood pressure by about 9 mmHg at 12 weeks in this population."

Task: before this claim is accepted, what must be questioned or tested?
