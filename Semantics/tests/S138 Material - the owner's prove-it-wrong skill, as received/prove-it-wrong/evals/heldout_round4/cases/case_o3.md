# case_o3

People Analytics — Hiring Process Review, H1 2026

**Structured interviews improve quality of hire.**

In January 2025 we offered business units the option to move from unstructured interviews to the new structured interview kit (fixed competency questions, anchored rating scales, a panel of three). Eleven of our 26 engineering and sales teams opted in during the first quarter; the remaining 15 continued as before and serve as the comparison group.

We followed all 2025 hires through to their 12-month review. Quality of hire is measured as the hiring manager's 12-month performance rating (1–5) for each hire still employed at that review.

Results:
- Structured-interview hires: mean rating 3.94 (n = 162)
- Comparison hires: mean rating 3.51 (n = 208)
- Difference 0.43 points, p < 0.01

Twelve-month retention was also higher in the structured group (84% vs 77%). Retention rose across the company in 2025 as the tech labour market cooled, but the structured group still came out ahead.

Hiring managers in the structured teams reported in a pulse survey that the kit "made decisions easier and fairer" (89% agree).

We recommend making the structured interview kit mandatory for all teams from Q3 2026, and extending it to operations and support roles, where we expect similar gains.
