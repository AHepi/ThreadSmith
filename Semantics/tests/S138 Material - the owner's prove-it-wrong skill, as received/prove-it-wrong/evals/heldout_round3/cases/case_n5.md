SMT Process Engineering Note — Line 3 reflow profile revision

After the Q2 defect spike on Line 3, we revised the reflow profile on 12 August, extending the soak by 30 s and raising peak temperature to 245 °C. In the two weeks following the change, the AOI defect rate fell from 3.1% to 1.7% (≈18,000 boards inspected), with tombstoning down 60% and solder bridging down 45%; the line has since held at 1.6–1.8%. Weekly defect figures are compiled by this group from the Line 3 AOI station. The estimated yield gain is about €9k per week. These results indicate the revised profile is the fix for the Q2 spike, and we propose transferring the same profile to Lines 1 and 2 at the next scheduled downtime, with a decision requested ahead of the Q4 production ramp.
