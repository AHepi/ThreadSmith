# case_n6

Customer Support People Team — Meeting-Free Wednesday pilot, week-8 results

Five Customer Support teams (94 staff invited) piloted meeting-free Wednesday afternoons for eight weeks in Q3. Participation was voluntary; team leads signed their teams up in July. Seventy-one staff completed the baseline PSS-10 stress scale, and 44 completed the week-8 follow-up (62% follow-up). Mean PSS-10 fell from 19.4 to 16.1, paired t-test p < .01, and every team improved. Free-text feedback was enthusiastic — "finally time to actually think" was typical. The pilot period also coincided with the department settling in after its summer hiring wave, which staff had welcomed after a stretched Q2. We conclude that protecting one afternoon a week materially reduces stress, and we recommend extending meeting-free afternoons company-wide, delivered through team leads with a weekly check-in to keep adoption high.
