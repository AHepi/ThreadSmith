# case_n2

Evaluation brief: MathQuest retrieval-practice app, Grades 6–8 mathematics, Spring 2024

Fourteen middle schools licensed MathQuest, a 15-minute daily retrieval-practice app aligned to state standards. Adopting classrooms (n = 3,112 students) averaged 62 completed sessions over a 14-week term; comparison classrooms in the same schools (n = 2,870) kept their standard warm-up routines. On the district's spring interim algebra assessment, MathQuest students scored 0.31 SD higher (p < 0.001; two-level model, students nested in schools). Gains were similar across the three grade levels. Students completing fewer than 20 sessions showed roughly half the gain, a dose–response pattern the evaluation reads as consistent with a treatment effect. About 300 students who transferred mid-term or missed the spring assessment were excluded from the analysis.

Teachers of adopting classrooms attended a one-hour orientation on retrieval practice before the term, and several later reported adding short written quizzes to their weekly routines. The district's board votes next month on a district-wide license at $14 per student.
