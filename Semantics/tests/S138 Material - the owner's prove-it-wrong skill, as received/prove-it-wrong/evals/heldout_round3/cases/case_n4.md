# case_n4

CleanAir District Office — Low-Emission Zone pilot, month-3 update

The LEZ came into force on 1 March across the four inner wards. To evaluate it, we deployed 45 low-cost electrochemical NO2 sensors at volunteer households in December, giving three months of baseline readings and three months of post-launch data. Mean NO2 fell from 34.6 µg/m³ (Dec–Feb) to 27.1 µg/m³ (Mar–May), a 22% reduction; 41 of 45 sensors show a decline, and the improvement holds in every ward. Total sensor cost was under 3% of a conventional reference monitoring campaign, and readings updated in real time on the public dashboard. We conclude the zone has delivered a substantial, spatially consistent improvement in local air quality, and we recommend extending the scheme to the outer wards before winter.
