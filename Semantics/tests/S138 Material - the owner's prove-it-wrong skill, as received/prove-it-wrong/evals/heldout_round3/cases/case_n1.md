# case_n1

Grower report — KelpMax foliar seaweed extract, greenhouse tomatoes, 2024 season

SunRidge Greens trialled KelpMax on block 4 of its Leamington greenhouse (0.8 ha, cultivar Merlice). Rows 1–20, at the south end of the block, received a weekly KelpMax spray from transplant through week 16; rows 21–40 continued the standard program. Harvest crews weighed and graded each row at every pick, and the row logs were compiled by the grower's operations manager, who proposed the trial. Marketable yield came in 18% higher on sprayed rows (p<0.05, t-test on weekly row totals over the 16-week season). Crews also reported noticeably fewer blossom-end-rot culls in the sprayed rows, though culls were not tallied separately.

At a product cost of about $340/ha for the season and 2024 contract prices, the yield difference is worth roughly $9,000/ha, and SunRidge has decided to spray all blocks in 2025. Productivity gains of this size within one season are rare in the sector, and the grower attributes them to the earlier, more uniform fruit set observed on treated rows.

Task: before this claim is accepted, what must be questioned or tested?
