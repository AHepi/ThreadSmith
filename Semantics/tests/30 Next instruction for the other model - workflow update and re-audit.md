## Stage C - Outside cases
Fill one case card for each case below, using the updated card. Give the theory's verdict under R2, and for clauses A and C also under the proposed replacements printed here. **Proposed clause A-second.** This clause applies to a limit that was added after the broader claim failed. Such a limit is a part of the account and is tested by a swap: put a neighbouring limit in its place. When some job goes undone, the limit is held, and the account names the part that holds it. What is held may be coarser than what is stated. When every job is done as well as before, the limit is loose, and the narrowed claim has yet to earn its boundary. Looseness is a fact about the present account; how the limit came to be chosen is a separate, historical question. A limit that only says what is being asked about, or that rests on stated background, is free, and the account says so.

**Proposed clause C-second.** This clause applies wherever the account or the case contains something that reads or reports the part offered as producer. Keep hold of that same part through two changes. Under a change to the thing itself, a producer's outcome moves. Under a change only to how the thing is read or reported, a producer stays as it was and so does the outcome; a measurement moves while the outcome stays. A part with the response of a measurement supports an answer to "what can we tell", which is a different question from "what produces it". Where nothing reads or reports the part, this clause is silent and the rest of Amendment C applies.

**O8 - Petra's own ovens** (A-second, a free limit). Petra explains why the fuse trips in her bakery when both ovens and the mixer run together: their combined draw exceeds what the old wiring carries. Her account covers her bakery and its wiring, and that was its scope from the start. It says nothing about other bakeries. *Thoughtful person:* a good explanation with an honest scope. The limit says what she is asking about.

**O9 - The float that does two jobs** (C-second, the same part throughout). A float sits in a fuel tank. A rod from it turns a dial on the dashboard. The same float, when it drops low enough, pulls open a valve to the reserve tank. Milan says the falling float opens the valve. Lena says the dial reaching "low" opens it. Bend the needle to read "full": the float is where it was and the valve opens as before. Hold the float up with a wire: the dial reads "full" and the valve stays shut. *Thoughtful person:* Milan is right, Lena is wrong. The float is read by the dial and also does the work.

**O10 - Two thermostats** (Amendment D). Two thermostats of the same make hang in two rooms, one set to eighteen degrees and one to twenty-two. Rosa says they are one kind of thing in two different states. Emil says they are two kinds, since one keeps a warm room and one a cool room. *Thoughtful person:* Rosa is right about what the devices are. Emil's difference matters only when the question is why this room is warm.

**O11 - The apprentice and the third lock** (Amendment E). On Monday an apprentice tries keys from a ring until one opens the lock. On Tuesday, at a new lock, she feels where a blank catches and files it until it turns. On Wednesday, at a third lock, she picks a key from the ring at once, saying it looks like Tuesday's. *Thoughtful person:* Monday is blind fitting. Tuesday is making something new. Wednesday is neither: she is reusing what Tuesday gave her.

**O12 - The rota** (Amendment G). A team argues for weeks about who is lazy. Noor asks a different question: which tasks have no owner? The arguing stops and the work gets done. Nobody had written down, or said aloud, that every task should have an owner. *Thoughtful person:* Noor found a better question, and its merit is real, even though the need it met was only recognised afterwards.

**O13 - Two plumbers** (Amendment H). Ana inspects a leak, says the washer has cracked, and explains why that lets water past. Before she can replace it, Ben tightens a nearby joint at random. The leak stops, because tightening shifted the cracked washer back into place. *Thoughtful person:* Ben's act stopped the leak. Ana's explanation is correct and produced nothing. Nobody here made a repair that came from an explanation.

**O14 - The phrasebook** (Amendment I). Kofi orders dinner abroad by reading phrases from a book. After a month he can order the same dishes with the book shut. Ask him for a sentence that was never in the book and he has nothing to say. *Thoughtful person:* Kofi has kept a narrow skill by his own effort. He has yet to understand the language.
