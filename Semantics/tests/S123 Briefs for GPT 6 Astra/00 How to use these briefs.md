# How to use these briefs

*For the owner. Written by Claude on 2 October 2026 (log S123, decisions S77, S78, S79).*

## What they are for

To build a hypothesis of what knowledge could mean in Avida, four different questions, each with tests. Before writing them I checked whether our path so far is enough. It is not: we have measured one meaning of knowledge well (your three properties, in Avida's programs), but nothing so far has tested an effect on Avida itself, told adapted from explained, asked whether the selector holds knowledge, or fed Avida any data from outside its own code. The full reasoning, with six rival hypotheses, is in `results/S123 What knowledge could mean in Avida - how best to find out, and whether the trajectory is sufficient.md`.
