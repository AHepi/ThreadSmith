## The two attribution tests for H64 (H, row 90)

**O25 - One second apart.** Ana closes valve A at ten o'clock exactly. Ben closes valve B one second later. The flow meter on the leaking pipe reads zero from the moment valve A closed; when Ben's valve closed there was nothing left to stop. Either valve alone would have stopped the leak. *Thoughtful person:* Ana stopped it. Ben's act would have stopped it and did not, because there was nothing left to stop when it came.

**O26 - Two feeds, one moment.** The leaking pipe is fed from two sides. Ana closes feed A and Ben closes feed B in the same moment. The leak stops only when both are closed; either alone would have halved it, and the meters on each feed show which half each stopped. *Thoughtful person:* Both did the work, and the record divides it: each stopped the half that ran through their feed.

## The assisted diagnosis for I64 (I, row 96)

**O27 - The test that fitted neither.** A remote expert sends the robot two candidate diagnoses and the one test that would separate them. The robot runs the test on its own evidence. The result fits neither candidate. The robot notices that the test assumed a pressure sensor was reading true, swaps to the second sensor, runs the test again, and finds a third fault that neither candidate named. *Thoughtful person:* The expert framed the choice. The robot found the answer. The achievement is mostly the robot's, because the decisive step was its own reinterpretation of the test.

## The next ten rows

**O28 - The card nobody can read** (D, row 38: "no faithful match-up with the required provenance remains"). A forecast card was printed in ink and also embossed in raised marks. The ink has faded past reading. The raised marks are intact and were read by touch, in a method one reader knew; that reader has died and taught nobody. A copy made while the ink was fresh gives the card's content. *Thoughtful person:* The marks are still there and still say what they said. What is lost is a reader, and a reader can be trained again from the copy. The provenance is intact; the access is not.

**O29 - The margin note** (E, row 56: "a grounded source"; "cannot be supported solely by a circle of mutually copied occurrences"). Diagram A was copied from diagram B, and B from an earlier version of A. That earlier version carried a margin note recording one fitting trial at a real lock. The earlier version burned; the margin note survives only as a line quoted inside B. *Thoughtful person:* There was a grounded source once. What remains of it is a quotation inside the circle. The chain rests on testimony to a fitting, and on no fitting anyone can now check.

**O30 - The routine uploaded that morning** (I, row 92: "Ownership of a history"; "its processes belong to that attributed continuing system's admitted process organization and resources"). The robot compares the pressures itself and chooses the valve, using a routine a remote person uploaded to it that morning. *Thoughtful person:* The comparison ran inside the robot and was the robot's work today. Who wrote the routine is a separate fact, and a true one. Ownership of today's history is the robot's; the origin of the routine is the person's.

**O31 - The wrong valve** (I, row 99: "the recipient's relevant originative contribution"; "receiving and relaying another's finished contribution"). The learner receives a finished bypass diagnosis. Applying it, she finds it names a valve this plant does not have, works out which valve does the job here, and corrects that one line. *Thoughtful person:* The diagnosis is received. The correction is hers, small and hers. Both facts stand: the received content stays received, and one relation was supplied by her.

**O32 - Found aloud, drawn later** (I, row 101: "a substantive division of contributions"). In a meeting, one engineer says aloud that the pressure depends on the second pump; the minutes record it. A week later another engineer draws the bypass on the diagram. The written report credits the drawer with both. *Thoughtful person:* The report is wrong about the division. The minutes are enough of a record: the dependence was found by the one who said it, and the bypass by the one who drew it.

**O33 - The table with empty columns** (G, row 67: "a faithful semantic presentation of the question data"). Lea's question is what happens when the two freezer knobs are turned together. A colleague rewrites it as a table with one column for each knob. Every row moves both knobs together; no row moves one alone. *Thoughtful person:* The table is faithful. Its columns could have carried single-knob rows, and none was added. A form that could ask more has not asked more.

**O34 - Where the second compartment used to be** (G, row 71: "the query is defined throughout the claimed changes"; "a coherent question"). One claimed change removes the freezer's second compartment. Lea's question asked for the second compartment's temperature. The colleague answers it, after the change, as the temperature of the space where the compartment used to be. *Thoughtful person:* A meaning has been supplied, and the question now asks something. It is not what Lea asked.

**O35 - Four seconds** (H, row 82: "protected obligations must remain satisfied"). During the washer repair the protected tap stops for four seconds while the mains is switched over, then runs as before. Nobody drew from it in those seconds. *Thoughtful person:* The protected condition held in every way that mattered: nobody was without water. The interruption is on the record and is not a loss.

**O36 - A premise both routes use** (J, row 104: "critical in some successful support"; "not necessarily in the full candidate"). Two proof routes reach the fixed answer. Both use premise P. Route two could be rewritten to use a different premise in P's place, but nobody has written that version. *Thoughtful person:* P is critical in each route as written. That another route could be written without it is a fact about a candidate nobody has written.

**O37 - The uncited textbook** (J, row 109: "a well-founded dependence order"; "dependencies have been settled"). The account traces every representation to its starting basis except one, which it justifies by a later theorem; the theorem's proof in the account uses that same representation. A separate proof of the theorem exists in a textbook the account does not cite. *Thoughtful person:* As written, the order has a circle in it. The textbook proof would break the circle, and the account has not used it. The order is not settled until it does.
