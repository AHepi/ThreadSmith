## 8. Building something new: construction, understanding and finding questions

### Example: the apprentice and the third lock

On Monday an apprentice tries keys from a ring until one opens a lock. On Tuesday, at another lock, she feels where a blank key catches and files it until it turns. On Wednesday, at a third lock, she picks a key from the ring at once, saying it looks like Tuesday's.

The **fixed verdict**: Monday is "blind fitting"; Tuesday is making something new; Wednesday is neither, but reuse of what Tuesday gave her. The **ruling** on file 11: the theory **agrees**. Monday is **selection**: a **pool** (the ring), **trials** (the keys tried) and a key that **survives**, with nothing in its history **picturing** the lock. Tuesday is **construction**: she makes a **join**, filing the blank to a picture of what the lock needs, and the key is **new** to her. Wednesday is no **origination**: what she uses is **equivalent** to something already in her **stock**, and she builds nothing that day.

### The idea in plain words

Making something new means building it yourself, to a picture of what it is about, and using it. Passing on is not building; reusing is not new. Questions too can be built; whether one was worth finding, the theory does not say.

### What the theory says

#### Understanding

Kofi orders dinner abroad from a phrasebook. After a month he can order the same dishes with the book shut; ask for a sentence not in the book and he has nothing.

A system **understands** a **content** (a set-up with what it claims on its range) when it holds a picture of it (a matching link to it, selected or constructed; section 7), uses it in its work on **challenges** (anything it is trying to solve or advance), and keeps the ability to use it for a stated **use task**, the task by which the claim is judged. Its **stock**, at a moment, is the contents it understands for some real use. A system can understand a false theory.

A narrow **lasting** use is what it is: Kofi's shows neither the wider understanding he lacks nor that he can never get there. The ruling on file 11 agrees: a narrow skill kept, the language not yet understood.

#### Construction

Tuesday shows it. **Construction** is a **stretch** of what actually happened, ending at a stated point and the system's **own** (inside its stated boundary; section 10), which:

- prepares a pictured set-up for explaining with the content;
- makes at least one join, not a trivial one, that matters for that use;
- is not just a chain of passings-on that leave the content as it was.

**Construction evidence** is its record: the processes at work, the **carriers** (physical things or events bearing content) that came in, the joins made, and the picture that came out.

A learner who works received content out afresh (**reconstruction**) constructs; a carrier that only passes it on (**relay**) does not. A first picture can be constructed from a challenge at hand, without ever observing what it pictures.

A learner receives a full key diagram from Sam. At another lock she finds a catch the diagram never covered, files one tooth for it, and keeps every other tooth as given. A small join she makes inside received content is construction of that join; the rest keeps its **inherited history**, the one it came with. The ruling on file 11 agrees: the tooth is hers, the rest is relay.

#### Unspoken pictures

A pianist can imagine a passage without playing it. A geometer can handle a relation in space without naming every piece. An investigator can notice a clash before she can say what it rests on. These are **unspoken pictures**: present and used though never said, and so not absent.

The definitions of picture, understanding and construction ask for no particular written form. They ask whether the needed distinctions, and the moves between them, are really there, with the matching and history a picture needs, and used wherever the definition asks for use.

A picture can be partial, spread out or stretched over time, and need not be spelled out all at once; its joins can live in memory, imagery, a rehearsed action or a notebook. Keeping a **criticized item** (whatever a criticism is aimed at; section 9) in mind can mean being able to bring back the distinction that matters, not storing a transcript.

So construction evidence may pick out a join by its use, not a statement: by responses that keep what the join ties together, treat the same content in another code alike, answer a change in content with the change the join calls for, and lie on a **route that ran** (events the difference passed through), as **using an objection** requires (section 9).

But use alone does not construct. Received content used as received keeps its inherited history; a carrier passing content on without such use has relayed it.

#### Construction is not selection

Monday and Tuesday again. A selected link has no pictured target in its history; a constructed one has. One physical system may show both, and the evidence differs.

#### New

Wednesday's key "looks like" Tuesday's; looks do not count. Two contents are **equivalent** at a level of detail when links run both ways between them, each matching on the range of the content in question: sameness of structure at that level of detail, not of wording or likeness. A content is **new** to a system when nothing already in its stock was equivalent to it.

On draft 4's view, untried in a round, Wednesday's verdict holds on either ground: if her key is equivalent to Tuesday's it is not new to her, and whatever it resembles, she built nothing that day.

#### Origination

A system **originates** a content for a question when it **used** it **on the question**, it was new to the system, and the system constructed it. The content may be a set-up, a link or a range. Originating a range is **finding a question**.

An investigator's notes mention in passing that "the seal was tight". Later she measures how tight it is, as something that varies, and finds it matters. A matter mentioned in passing is not thereby a **slot** of her account; it becomes one when the account allows changes to it, and adding it is construction. The ruling on file 11 agrees.

#### Finding a question

A team argues for weeks about who is lazy. Noor asks which tasks have no owner. The arguing stops and the work gets done. Nobody had said every task should have an owner.

A question (target, range, what is asked) can be found as well as answered, and finding questions, the theory says, is at least half of creativity. Ranges have histories: **declared** (written in), selected (the survivor of a pool), or constructed (made in an **episode**, a stretch of inquiry, with construction evidence). Any of the three may be used in judging, but a claim that an episode found a question needs a constructed range, with the evidence.

The theory proves that a range, with what is asked, can be treated as a set-up: its slots are the changes and their circumstances; its pieces, whatever settles which changes belong; its allowed changes, adding or removing a change, or altering what is asked. So understanding, construction and newness apply to it, and where the rest holds, finding it can be **created knowledge** (section 9). Finding the right question is creative on the same footing as answering one. A theory taking questions as given cannot say this; this one can, since ranges have histories.

That a question was found says nothing about its **worth**. The rota's fixed verdict: "Noor found a better question, and its merit is real, even though the need it met was only recognised afterwards." The ruling on file 11: **silent**, on merit only. The theory gets as far as "found a question": if Noor constructed the range, it was new to her and she used it on the team's challenge, she originated it. Whether it was better, its merit real, is a claim of worth, which needs a **standard of worth** from outside the theory. The case supplies none, so that half is **unsettled**.

This is the fifth **main claim** (section 11). It would be **defeated** by a showing that treating a range as something that can be constructed, be new and be originated either cheapens creativity or misses some real case of finding the right question.

### What changed in this revision

- Unspoken pictures are added, with a join picked out by its use and the guard that use alone does not construct.
- Equivalent is defined; file 11 used it without saying what it meant.

**Departure** from the source books: David Deutsch holds that grasping any idea takes a **guess**. Here taking in an idea need not be construction: reconstruction is, relay is not. Likenesses to him on relay and unspoken knowledge are noted, not claimed as borrowed.

### What is open

- Counting found questions as creative may cheapen creativity: any question built, new and used counts, even "which tasks were done on a Tuesday?"
- An outside reader called the proof about ranges "a sketch, not a proof", finding nothing false: it names a range's slots, pieces and changes, not how the pieces behave. The project agreed a step is missing and counted no defeat: the list of defeats names no numbered proof past the third.
- The text never says in words when a link into a content matches on that content's range, which the definition of equivalent needs.
