### An example

Section 5 says two rivals that both fit make a problem. The theory says so in Part VI, lines 315–317, which the map sends to section 5.

### The idea in plain words

Every paragraph of draft 4's theory text went to a section, ordered for a newcomer, not by Parts (the theory's numbered divisions). Each Derivation, one of its ten numbered proofs (short arguments that something follows from what it says), sits beside the idea it proves.
