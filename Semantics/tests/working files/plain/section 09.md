## 9. Criticism, repair and created knowledge

### Example: two plumbers

Ana inspects a leak, says the washer has cracked, and explains why that lets water past. Before she can replace it, Ben tightens a nearby joint at random. The leak stops: the tightening shifted the cracked washer back into place.

The **fixed verdict**: Ben's act stopped the leak; Ana's explanation is correct and produced nothing; nobody made a repair that came from an explanation. The **ruling** on file 11: the theory **agrees**. Ana holds a correct **account** (an explanation meeting the four requirements) that produced nothing; Ben made a **repair** without one; so there is no **created knowledge**.

### The idea in plain words

Being right is not fixing, and fixing is not knowing. Created knowledge needs an account you built, a fix through it, and the account understood afterwards.

### What the theory says

Ana offered her diagnosis unchecked, as the theory allows: an idea may be held before anything justifies it. A criticism is aimed at something, and is itself a **guess**. A thinker may act on an opinion without proving it first.

#### Routes that ran

A route ran from Ben's hand through the washer to the stop; none ran from Ana's explanation.

The **course of events** is everything that happened, in an order that never loops back, with the physics saying which events, **slots** (places holding values) and connections there were. A **route that ran** is a connected **cluster** of actual events joining a **pictured** input to an outcome, each **piece** along it keeping to its behaviour, through which the pictured difference actually passed: the outcome depended on it, against stated alternatives. A route that started and did no work, or had stopped before the outcome, did not run for it: this is found in the course of events, not inferred from the outcome.

#### Criticism

Asked "does this bath leak?", a builder says "no". A neighbour points to a damp patch on the ceiling below: water gets there only past the bath's seal.

A **criticism** has a **criticized item** (the builder's answer; or a question, range, link or method), an **alleged fault** (it is false), **grounds** (the patch) and a **connection** from grounds to fault (only past the seal). It **bears on** its item when the connection, taken as a **candidate** for the **fault question** (does the item have that fault, with respect to the original question?), is an account of it. If the water came from a pipe in the wall, the criticism is made but does not bear.

Until something pictures the patch as grounds for an alleged fault, it is only a **warning sign**: it tells against the answer, but is not yet a criticism.

#### Using an objection

The builder lifts the bath panel because of her **objection** (a guess that something is wrong). A response **uses an objection** when the objection shapes it rightly and lies on a route that ran. Rightly means: each role in the objection keeps its role in the response; the objection in another code, saying the same, gets the same response; a change in what it says brings the change the responder's way of deciding calls for (a patch under the basin, and he opens the basin). Using a wrong objection does not make it right.

#### Episodes

Turning off the mains would stop the leak, and the tap that must keep running.

An **episode** is a stretch of inquiry, from a difficulty to a response. An **episode of criticism** holds a **noticed difficulty**, a criticized item there before its criticism, an objection, and a response that depends on what the objection says. A noticed difficulty is, when the system pictures it, a failed **demand** (the leak goes on) or a conflict in which what the system holds meets a demand only by breaking a **safeguard** (as turning off the mains would). A **creative episode** is an episode of criticism holding an **origination** connected to its inquiry. Closing an episode is a decision, not a proof.

#### Repair

Ben's tightening is a **contribution**: a stretch of events, with the changes of content it makes.

Demands (what is to be repaired) and safeguards (what must keep holding) are **stated inputs**, recorded by the theory, not supplied. They are fixed for comparing before with after, each with the **occasions** (times or cases) it covers; a safeguard is lost exactly when it fails on any of them. A contribution makes a repair when some demand failed before and holds after; every safeguard that held before held on every covered occasion in between, not only at the end; and the repair was **produced by** the contribution.

Stating a demand claims nothing about its worth; the theory ranks no repair above another; losses outside the safeguards must go on the record.

#### Credit

*Two valves in one second.* Ana and Ben each close a different valve within the same second; the leak stops. The records are complete: either valve alone would have stopped it. Each claims the repair. Fixed verdict: both did something that would have stopped it; neither can claim it alone; nothing says whose water stopped. The ruling on file 11: agrees.

A repair is produced by a contribution when a route that ran joins the two. **Credit** (which contribution an achievement is attributed to) goes to each contribution the course of events shows: both routes ran, so both are credited. The course of events holds no **sharing of credit**, so the theory gives none; a **weighting of credit** beyond it is a stated input.

Three attributions stay apart: a correct account that produced nothing (Ana, among the plumbers); an act that repaired without one (Ben); a repair produced through the use of an account (nobody).

#### Created knowledge

Change the story: Ana works out for herself, for the first time, why a cracked washer leaks, puts in a spare because of it, and the leak stops through that.

A **demand for knowledge** asks that a correct account, or the correction of a use through one, be understood. Created knowledge is a creative episode making a repair in which such a demand failed before and holds after, through a content that:

- the system **originated** (built, **new** to it, used on its question; section 8) by the episode's end;
- is an account of that question;
- is among **what the contribution prepared**, the contents made ready by its end;
- the system **understands** afterwards;
- has its **join** (a connection its builder made) on the route that did the repair: the repair was **produced through the account**.

The account is held to the range fixed at its origination: a later narrowing to rescue it makes a **different question**, and does not count. The changed story would count if her inquiry was an episode of criticism and a demand for knowledge was stated.

#### Worth and art

A plumber mends a leak in a shed nobody uses: still a repair. It shows a demand was met, not that it, or the question behind it, was worth having. Where a claim invokes **worth**, the theory takes the **standard of worth**, one of its two **foundations**, from outside, and marks the place.

The eighth complaint asks "where is beauty?". Here: the artistic case keeps apart a work's **effect** (what it does, in a context), its **purpose** (what it aims at), **achieved purpose** (some effect, and every effect inside the aim) and **artistic value**, taken from the standard of worth, with its **reasons of taste**, whenever it is claimed. None is defined as another. The theory does not pretend to know which reasons of taste are true; achieving a stated effect implies no view of beauty.

### What changed in this revision

- The fault question and connection are spelled out; a warning sign becomes a criticism when pictured as grounds for an alleged fault, not when its bearing is pictured.
- Now defined: noticed difficulty, contribution, what the contribution prepared, produced through the account.
- A safeguard must hold on every covered occasion, not only at the end.
- The standard of worth is a foundation, no longer a stated input.

**Departure**: David Deutsch holds there are objective truths about beauty; the theory abstains.

### What is open

- **Four seconds.** During a repair, the tap that must keep running stops for four seconds; nobody draws from it. Fixed verdict: the safeguard held in every way that mattered; the pause is recorded, not a loss. The ruling on file 11: **silent**; the occasions are not stated (every moment, or only when someone draws water).
- **"Mostly".** The fixed verdicts of the expert's question, the test that fitted neither, and the same afternoon, three achievements call an achievement "mostly" one party's. The rulings on file 11: silent, lacking a stated weighting of credit.
- The **building order** (how definitions rest on one another) places neither what a route that ran depends on nor the two terms defined for created knowledge.
