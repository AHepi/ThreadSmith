## 7. Where links come from: selection, pictures and surprise

### Example: the discarded lamp controller

Each lamp controller has switches A and B. A sorting machine keeps controllers whose lamp, with B off, is dark with A off and lit with A on. Only two designs exist, both buildable; they differ with both switches on, which the machine never tries. One design stayed dark with A on and was thrown out. Ivo says it shows that passing leaves open what a controller does with both on.

The **fixed verdict**, written in advance, with high confidence: no. That design failed at a setting the machine tries, so it says nothing about controllers that pass; Ivo would need two designs that both pass and still differ. A **proposed case**, used in no **round**: below is a view of draft 4, not a **ruling**.

### The idea in plain words

A tie between mind and world can be shaped blindly by weeding out, built to a picture, or written in; creativity lies only in building. Where blind shaping never tried, it leaves open only what alternatives that would also have passed leave open.

### What the theory says

#### Carriers, contents and two layers

A printed page and a spoken sentence with the same words are two **carriers**, physical things or events bearing content. A **content** is a **set-up** with what it claims on its **range**, the **settings** (allowed changes in given circumstances) it covers. Same words do not make carriers the same, nor same outputs contents.

You see a bus, the same bus as before, go behind a lorry. A thinker has at least two set-ups. The **thing layer** holds lasting things with edges and identity, not a blur of sensation: its slots are the things, its pieces the **threads** keeping each the same, and its allowed changes include **moving**, **hiding** and **telling which is which again**. The **expectation layer** above holds dependences among things and is asked for predictions: "the bus will come out there". Neither layer is assumed to exist in any real system; the theory says what their existing and being linked would be.

#### Three histories

A **link** ties one set-up to another. It has exactly one of three **histories**, settled by what physically happened:

- **Selected.** From a **pool** of links, fed by **variation**, the world weeds out those failing to **match** (behave alike) on the **trials**, the finitely many settings met during shaping. A link **survives** when, and only when, it is in the pool and matches on every trial. Nothing in the trials **pictures** the link, the trials or what counts as surviving.
- **Constructed.** An **episode** of inquiry prepares it, with **construction evidence** (the record of the building), and the link, or the set-up it leads to, is **pictured**: held in a picture by the system.
- **Declared.** Written in by the **modeller**: a convenience, on which no claim of creativity may rest.

So a picture's tie to what it pictures is a link with a history, not a given. Creativity lives in construction; selection supplies its raw material. A selected link has no pictured target and no criticism in its history, a constructed one both; neither reduces to the other. So, to the **complaint** "You have replaced explanation with evolution": selection appears once, at the bottom, producing the thing layer explanation works on, and every claim of creativity needs construction. Selected links are expected at the thing layer, constructed ones above, though not necessarily; construction may work on selected material, selection beneath it.

A selection is a physical history, fallible and checkable; a label like "is a cause" claims nothing. The pool belongs to the claim: it is the links **the physics** and the **stated design** (how members are built) allow, and a link needing something every member is built without is not in it.

#### Pictures

A sea chart claims a coastline. It **pictures** that content, at a **level of detail**, when the set-up it **embodies** there has a selected or constructed link to the content that matches on the content's range. A chart can picture a wrong coastline, the content's link to the world failing; so a system can picture a false theory. A declared link pictures nothing; it only lets a modeller say so. A lost chart keeps its history; a record later worked out from it is no separate evidence of that history. Which set-up a carrier embodies comes from outside: the physics.

#### Expectation, miss and surprise

The bus fails to come out. For a link to the expectation layer, at a setting that actually happens, the **expectation** is what that layer gives; a **miss** is the link failing to match there; a **surprise** is a miss by a selected link at an **untried setting**, one not among its trials. So a constructed link that fails has a miss, never a surprise. A miss the system pictures can be a **noticed difficulty** (section 9).

The theory proves that only a system holding a selected link whose trials fall short of what the world allows can be surprised. Surprise is no added feeling, just such a link meeting a change it was never shaped against. Two responses: **re-tuning** adds the setting to the trials and lets variation adjust the link within its pool; a **response by construction** builds a fresh set-up or link, with construction evidence. These are learning and creating, told apart by evidence, not outcome; only the second can **originate** anything (section 8).

#### What survival leaves open

At an untried setting, the theory proves, the trials leave a selected link's value **left open** only where the pool holds another survivor that differs there. An untried setting alone does not show such a survivor exists; where none does, the pool fixes the value. So a selected link matches where it was tried and, wherever its pool allows an alternative, is unconstrained elsewhere: that is why the thing layer can be wrong, and why surprise can happen. Given up is the older claim that every untried value is open.

Hence the lamp's fixed verdict. Its pool is the two designs, its trials the settings tried; the other design failed one, so did not survive, and "both on" is not left open: passing tells you which design you have, and so what it does there. Draft 4 adds a second reason (section 6): any design giving the failed answer at a tried setting fails there, whoever offers it.

So, against a complaint, survival is not truth: it is how a link got there; whether it matches at changes never met is a separate fact.

#### The hidden thing: an episode worked out in full

A made-up world: a row of cells; two things, each with a position and a speed; allowed changes: move a thing, set its speed, hide a cell, swap the two. The senses show which cells are filled, not by which thing.

An expectation layer, selected on trials of moves and speed changes but no hiding, predicts filled cells from recent ones. A thing goes behind a screen and keeps moving; the layer expects nothing there, and has a miss when the thing comes out where its speed puts it: at an untried setting, a surprise. Re-tuning fails if every member of the pool predicts from filled cells alone: none survives; the fault is built in.

Construction builds a fresh layer with a **tracker** for each thing, a piece carrying its position and speed through hiding, linked to that thing's thread. This link matches on the wider range, and the trackers have the **response pattern** of things, moving and changing speed as things do, unaffected by hiding. Nobody declared them "objects"; they respond as objects do. If the system built the layer in a stretch of its **own** (section 10), with a real **join** (tracker to thread), and the layer is **new** to it, the system originated it (section 8). If this meets the **demand** "hold, and understand, an account of reappearing" while keeping the **safeguard** "predict moves correctly", it is a **repair** and **created knowledge** (section 9).

The swap changes nothing seen. On a range allowing each change for both things alike, exchanging the two things turns the link into one tying each tracker to the other's thread, with the same matching and answers, as things and trackers are built alike: as far as matching and answering go, the two are one account. "Tracker one is really thing one" needs an allowed change telling them apart, and there is none: at this level of detail, which thing is which is only its path.

The episode is a **proof by example that the theory hangs together**, not a claim about any infant, animal or program.

#### Nadia and the cards she has never seen

Nadia, the everyday twin, learns a card game only by watching cards laid down, predicting from cards just played, and for weeks "is often surprised". Then she keeps a tally of cards yet to appear, "waiting somewhere", and her predictions improve sharply.

The fixed verdict: "Nadia built something new: a part that stands for cards she has never observed. That is construction, and it is her own." The ruling on file 11 agrees: the tally is a response by construction, like the trackers; a first picture can be constructed from a **challenge** at hand without observing what it pictures. On draft 4's view, whether her early misses were surprises turns on an unstated fact, whether her old way of predicting was selected; the verdict does not. "Her own" needs a stated boundary (section 10).

#### Recoding and rewriting

A coded message keeps its content if a reader using the stated key recovers every pairing: that is **recoding**. The theory proves that **rewriting** everything (carriers, behaviours, links, histories, ranges) by a one-to-one mapping keeping every connection keeps accounts, originations, repairs and created knowledge; **blurring**, a changed **boundary** and merged events are not covered. A gap in a recording filled from another keeps that other's history, whatever it agrees with.

#### What would defeat this

The two histories, and what trials leave open, make the fourth **main claim** (section 11). Three findings would **defeat** it: a selected link fixed by its trials at an untried setting though its pool holds a differing survivor; every construction retold as selection with nothing lost; explanation shown not to work on the thing layer.

### What changed in this revision

- Surviving the trials is defined.
- Expectation and miss cover every link to the expectation layer, surprise only selected ones; file 11 gave a failed constructed theory no miss.
- The pool is what the physics and the stated design allow, not links already made; the survivor requirement is restated to match.
- The swap now runs through an exchange of the two things, replacing file 11's false "the trackers are one **kind**"; a wrong remark on the episode goes.
- The opening says selection's history holds no pictured target: a clarification.

**Departures** from the source books: David Deutsch calls criticism and experiment selection; here selection is blind. His "problem", which can arise without observation, is wider than surprise; here it can be a noticed difficulty.

### What is open

- If a programmer designed the pool, success at untried settings is the programmer's knowledge; the theory cannot yet say so.
- The survivor requirement makes the claim on untried settings close to true by its wording alone, so hard to defeat.
- Nobody has tried retelling a construction as selection; no case looks at the thing layer.
