# S134 About this material: what the reading record is

*Kept as received on 3 October 2026 (decision S89). The owner uploaded "Thinking_Machine_Reading_Record.zip" (md5 2105c0148b5f38ffddb1f36e630c2f7f, 1,189,997 bytes) with the words "Ok. So this is what it was doing." The zip was extracted here unchanged (373 entries, 2,602,166 bytes uncompressed) and the zip itself dropped, as the convention says. Nothing in it was run.*

**What it is.** The research record behind the owner's outside experiment (S133 material: the read-me and report of a four-stage non-neural visual experiment, whose read-me says this archive was extracted beside it for reading and provenance). It was made on the owner's own computer by an outside coding agent (the "main assistant" of its read-me) with MiMo 2.6 Pro as the designated reader of Steven Pinker's *How the Mind Works* and *Learnability and Cognition*.

**What is in it.**
- `Thinking_Machine_Theory.txt` and `.pdf`: "A theory for creating a thinking machine", the outside agent's own proposal (4,440 words, 12 sections, dated 3 October 2026). Its first line says no thinking machine was implemented or tested in that research.
- `research/readings/`: 117 short readings of the two books by MiMo (234 files: a json with the model's reply and usage, and its text).
- `research/second_reading/`: three role readers (implementable mechanisms; learning, generalization and temporal memory; independent criticism), each rereading both books in sections of about thirty percent (24 files each).
- `research/role_syntheses/` and `research/synthesis_attempts/`: the consolidated memoranda per role (the attempts are earlier drafts, two of which exceeded their output limit).
- `research/verification/`: ten focused checks of claims against original passages.
- `research/audits/`: two short original proposals by the outside agent, "bounded program construction candidate" and "independent design critique", and a note that an optional final review by MiMo was blocked by an automatic approval review.
- Six research scripts (reading, rereading, consolidating, verifying, rendering); manifests and a receipt of requests and tokens; the verified endpoint file (a public endpoint address, no credential).

**What is not in it.** The two books and their extracted full texts (its own read-me and manifest say so; the manifest records their hashes and page counts). No credential (checked by the standing key search). No code of the four-stage experiment itself.

**Caution.** The readings and memoranda are an outside model's notes on two copyrighted books, with page references, and may carry quoted passages; its read-me says the working notes are not authoritative transcripts and may contain mistaken page references. Nothing from them is quoted at length anywhere in this project (the 25-word rule stands), and the books stay out of the repository.
