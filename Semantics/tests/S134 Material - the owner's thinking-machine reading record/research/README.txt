Reading record for A theory for creating a thinking machine

The theory is the main assistant's original proposal, informed by MiMo 2.6 Pro readings of the two supplied Steven Pinker books. Read Thinking_Machine_Theory.pdf or Thinking_Machine_Theory.txt first.

MiMo read both books in 117 short sections, then three separate role readers each reread both books in approximately thirty-percent sections, with a final ten-percent remainder. Every request supplied the thinking-machine purpose. The roles were implementable mechanisms, learning and temporal memory, and independent criticism. Each role has eight completed original-text readings. Ten focused verifications return to original passages. The final accepted role syntheses are complete.

Working notes are not authoritative book transcripts. Some contain mistaken page references, overstatements, or proposals rejected in the final theory. The focused verifications and final theory take precedence. First synthesis attempts are preserved separately: two exceeded their output limit, and none is used as the accepted final consolidation. Their token use is retained in the receipt. No machine was built or behaviourally tested.

An optional final MiMo review of the authored theory was blocked by automatic approval review. The draft was not sent for that review. See audits/final_review_status.txt. This does not invalidate the completed book readings or source verifications.

The source books themselves and their extracted full texts are not duplicated in this archive. Their hashes and page counts are recorded in manifest.json. The source page numbering throughout is ordinal file-page numbering. Source images and complex diagrams were not independently interpreted. No access credential is included.

The scripts reproduce the reading preparation and requests when supplied with the original books and a separately configured valid MiMo credential. They are research tools, not an implementation of the proposed thinking machine. The verified endpoint is public configuration. read_books.py expects a credential in the private temporary file named in its KEY_FILE setting; never place a credential in this archive. extract_books.py recreates source text and small sections from the original document directory. reread_books.py constructs and reads the larger sections. consolidate_roles.py builds final role syntheses. verify_claims.py performs the focused passage checks. These tools make externally billed or quota-consuming calls only when explicitly run with valid access.
