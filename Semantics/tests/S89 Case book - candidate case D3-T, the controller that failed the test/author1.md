## Fixes needed

1. The switch setup is unclear. "Stay dark with one switch off and light with it on" mentions one switch, and then "an untried joint setting of two switches" appears. Say that each controller has two switches, A and B, that the tester only flips A (with B held in one fixed position), and that the untried setting is a combination the tester never uses (for example A on with B in its other position).
2. "Failed the earlier switch-on test" makes it sound as if there was a separate, earlier test. Say "failed the tester's switch-on check (it stayed dark with the switch on)", so it is clearly the same test.
3. "Undecided" can be read two ways: "not fixed by the test" or "not yet observed". Change the question to "Does the discarded design show that passing the test leaves open how a controller behaves at the untried setting?" That keeps it about what the test fixes, not about what someone has watched.
4. "What passes the testing" reads awkwardly. "the controllers that pass" or "a controller that passes" is clearer.
5. "Ivo has only two controller designs to choose from" suggests that Ivo does the choosing, not the tester. "Only two controller designs exist" is neutral.
6. The case is not leading in a way that pushes toward a wrong answer. "Only the other passed" and "there are no others" point clearly to the verdict, and that seems intended.
