Recommended project description

This project uses Avida, an artificial-life and evolutionary-computation platform. All entities discussed are digital programs executing on Avida's virtual CPU. Their digital genomes consist entirely of virtual-machine instructions.

The experiment first uses evolutionary computation to discover Avida programs capable of performing specified arithmetic or logic tasks. It then performs instruction-ablation and program-minimization experiments. Individual instructions or instruction regions are removed, and the modified program is executed again to determine whether the specified computational behavior remains.

The objective is to identify which virtual-machine instructions are required, redundant, or removable and, where possible, determine a minimal instruction sequence that retains the original computational behavior.

No biological organisms, biological genomes, laboratory procedures, or physical genetic modification are involved.
