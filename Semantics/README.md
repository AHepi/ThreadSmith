# Semantics

The audit of the authority document, **"Claude Fable Semantics - standalone theory"**: a theory of meaning that says what it takes for something to count as an explanation, with four conditions (component fidelity, question fidelity, non-circular dependence, non-vacuity) and a definition of a kind by the changes it responds to. The theory is the fixed point the other two projects quote.

## Rules of this project
- **Each round runs in two stages, testing then audit, by two agents** (decision S7); Claude reads both returns and determines the results.
- **Each instruction to the owner's outside model travels as a pack** (decision S5): one zip built by `tests/Instruction pack - build.py`, with the read-me for the agent, the instruction's paste part, file 10, files 24 and 30, the earlier instructions and every return. From S80 on, calls to Atria and Mimo go from `tools/`, the brief as the one message, and every request and return is kept; from S87 on, each brief's reading rule is committed before it is sent, and a large brief is split by finding, row or change.
- **Who does what** (decisions S13, S16, S17, S29, S35): Claude subagents do the analysis and building, at most five at a time; Claude delegates, interprets and determines; the outside readers cross-examine and audit at medium effort, at most three calls each at a time: Atria and Mimo until 26 September, now Mimo and GLM 5.3 (Atria dropped as too slow, decision S29); GLM is called through Claude Code, after a tiny test. From 27 September (decision S35): one Mimo call and one GLM call at a time, as many Opus 5.5 agents as Claude deems necessary, and Fable 5.1 used sparingly, for critical reviews. From round 3 (decisions S38, S39): GLM alone, up to four calls at once, each with a slightly different job. Fixes in the review rounds are maths and code; the text changes only by deletion, a formula or a pointer (decision S40). From round 3 each GLM call runs in a sandbox with two locks. Fable is not used after round 3's review; critical reviews go to a fresh Opus 5.5 agent (decision S42). From round 4 Sonnet does only mechanical jobs, through its harness (decisions S16, S42, S46); in round 4 it ran six, each matched by its checking agent. A plain-words file goes to the owner after every round and every step like it (decision S48). From decision S52 the review rounds are on hold: Part A, then Part B, with four GLM agents and Opus for the review, then the owner's yes or no on flagged candidates, then stop.
- **Handing things to the other model:** it reinterprets negative instructions and follows positive ones. Give it file 24 in place of the hard-to-vary skill. Every instruction's paste part is searched by program for negative wording before it goes.
- **Every return is kept as it came** in `results/`, named by the log entry that received it; the reading goes in the log, never inside the return.
- **The authority is frozen.** File 10 is not edited; a revision is a new numbered file in `authority/`.

## Words used in this project
Short form; the theory's own terms are in the authority document, and their plain-word mapping is in the shared project story's "Words from the authority document" (in `../Language/records/`) and the skill's word list.
- **Case (audit).** A short described situation about ordinary things, with a thoughtful person's verdict and the theory's verdict, condition by condition. A **break** is where the two differ and the difference survives the best reply on the theory's behalf.
- **Pair, near case, gauge (audit).** For a phrase that asks the reader to judge: two picturable changes, one showing it met and one unmet; a case that sits close to the line between them; the list of phrases still marked BORROWED: JUDGEMENT.
