## 4. Your two points of today, in the text

Take the water wheel from file 96 again: the wheel lifts its own water back up and turns forever. A student takes "perpetual motion is impossible" as given, without a thought, and rules the wheel out.

**"That "ruling out" is a choice that was made."** The repaired copy had said that ruling out one rival by such a claim "is not a response to the conflict". You answered that it is. The new copy says: when a claim taken as given is used to rule out a rival, that ruling out is a choice the person made, not something the claim does by itself, and it is already something done about the conflict. Your earlier words stay beside it: the bare claim, on its own, "is not enough for a creative agent to do anything about it". The claim alone does nothing; the student, in going on with it, has done something, by choice.

**"A theory is never settled."** The repaired copy had said that whether a candidate meets the four requirements "is fixed by" the candidate, the question and the thing explained, and it marked that sentence as your open question. The new copy says it depends on the thing explained and not on anyone's view of it, and that nothing about it is ever settled: whatever anyone accepts about it, that the wheel's explanation meets the requirements or that it does not, is accepted tentatively. The same is said once near the start, where the theory defines its two words, with your sentence quoted.
