# W2 Results - five Opus 5 judges on the draft research plan; what they forced into W3

Written 22 September 2026. Five Opus 5 agents at effort xhigh, each armed with the hard-to-vary skill (file 33 and five of its modules) and made to falsify its own findings before returning, read draft W1 through the session's Workflow tool: one against file 10 sentence by sentence; one running the skill's full procedure on the plan as an explanation; one checking every claim about the record and every rule; one building the best rival programme and looking for what tells the two apart; one taking the owner's note H6 sentence by sentence and checking whether phase 3 could be run with the rig that exists. Raw returns, as they came: `rigs/W2 judges/` (five JSON files, one per judge, and `tool_audit.txt`). About 1.29 million subagent tokens, 66 minutes.

No prediction file was frozen before this round (Lesson W2). It is read as a reading of the draft, not as a test of anything; what it forced is below, and the frozen plan W3 is the draft with those edits.

## The short answer
- **The draft did not survive as written, and W3 is not a patch of it.** Every judge found the same three faults from a different side: M8 fused two losses (a component replaced when a harness sets a port; what only the parts held together lost when a task is partitioned) and its own falsifier P2.3 fired on its own arm (d); M3 read Derivation 9 as a licence for the inference it forbids, so M5 (i)'s claim that two arrangements "instantiate the same thing" could never have been shown by the agreement it predicted; and P3.2 and P3.3, the two predictions carrying Q1 and Q2, counted the same-explanation verdict, a field H64 struck from use as a count. W3 splits M8 into M8a and M8b with different arms; states what phase 4 can settle (arrangements separated by their projections; nothing about instantiation); and inserts an instrument phase that must certify a field before any arm difference is read off it.
- **The theory was read wider than written, or at the wrong Part, in nine places**, the fault the plan had named as its likeliest: Part IX's definition of a history cut at a comma before its third clause; a phrase of Part IV cited to Part X; Part XIV's phrase cited to Part XI; (F2) made to carry a claim about procedures that Part VI carries; Derivation 5 given as the example of a construction the theory does not contain, when its claim sentence contains it; the weights called a transport with components, when a transport has none; "fixed" mapped to "protected" where the nearer half of the same quotation is "fixed for the comparison"; Part XIV's dependence order made a reason for an order of work; "the owner's failed harness" where the owner recorded a difficulty. All corrected in W3, and the quote-checking program now reports the Part each quotation sits under (`rigs/W3 quote check/`).
- **The record was misread in four places**: "every run read" left out the 99 light-marked reports, which are a held-out set H67 still holds; "no answer-keyed test has ever run" left out Language file 37; the reserve was listed as clean when fourteen of its Semantics cases are already run and three of plan 42's cases cannot confirm the lines they would test; the CANNOT count was carried over 147 runs when it was marked on 48. Each is corrected in W3's section 2 and P4.7.
- **Three constructions were unmarked**: the stateful/stateless definition on CT2; "a skill is a carrier of the theory's operations"; and the frame M1, which had declined to declare the physical input (R) needs and was marked *fixed* by its own author, a mark the skill reserves for the owner. W3 marks each, declares \(\operatorname{Org}_\ell\) at the grain of text as the model's declared input, and declares the two indices (boundary, continuity) that every ownership predicate carries and the draft had never named.
- **The skill map left out nine operations of the list it quoted** ((O), (R), Deploy, (N), (G), (EK), (U1) to (U3)) and gave two of its "no home" rows to things a file already holds (bearing, receipts). W3's table has a row for every operation of Part XIV's order, defines "no home" as "no skill holds it", and gives the contract two routes.
- **Two attack points of the theory were rested on as settled**: M2 on Derivation 3, which is attack point (D) and a Part XV defeat condition; M8 as an eliminative explanation, which Part VII lists under attack (B). W3 names both beside the parts and says what the programme does if either attack succeeds.
- **The rival judge found the two programmes the same at the level of the end goal** (what the workflow must carry; the owner's-word gate; Derivation 9's bound; the read-only rule; an answer-keyed run at the end) and different on one count: the draft predicts at most three holders change between its sketched map and the frozen one; the rival, drawing the map by edit-signature, predicts four or more. W3 keeps the draft's count as P5.1 and records the rival's as the opposite prediction on the same observation, so that phase 5 decides it.

## Lessons
- **W1.** The program that checked the draft's quotations against file 10 was run and its output not kept, so the check could not be re-run or audited (record judge). Fixed: the program and its output are kept under `rigs/W3 quote check/`, and it now reports the Part each quotation sits under.

## What this does not show
- Anything a reader under test would do under any arm. Nothing has run.
- Whether the five judges are independent of the record they judged: they are the same kind of agent, armed with the same skill, and one said so of itself. The two that found what the writer could not (the file-10 judge on Derivation 9's direction; the rival judge on the same-explanation count) were the two reading against the draft rather than with it.
- Whether W3's corrections are themselves right at the wrong Part or wider than written. The program checks presence and the Part; it does not check width. Phase 1's judges do.

## Traps
- Reading the counts of findings as scores for or against the draft. Each is a count of quoted items, and one (M8's fusion) mattered more than twenty.
- Reading the rival's programme as rejected. Its instrument phase is in W3; its order is held with the count that would decide it.
