### 14.2 Fault 25 — `rivals_built`'s naming rule was not decidable on a report neither of us had named

### D1. `marks_per_part`: the gate counted by document (harness) or by report (independent). 4 items.

**What differs.**

- **Explanation.** `stage_b.py` reads "marks_per_part read by document" as a count of the documents all of whose reports agree, against ceil(40/48 × the documents in the set). The independent reads it as a count of reports against 40 of 48.
- **What it forbids.** Any report on which the two programs' outcomes differ.
- **Rival.** The programs differ on some reports, and the document counts only show it.
- **Check** (seen, `checks/c3_marks_per_part_unit.py`). The independent's own per-report outcomes, grouped by document, give 6 of 16 and 0 of 2, which are `stage_b.py`'s numbers. c1 also shows the same reports named on both sides. The rival is ruled out.

## 1. What the program does

1. **Pairs the marks.** Each first mark `M###` is matched to its run id through `marking1/index.json`. Each second mark `R###` is matched through `marking/secret_mapping_record96.json`. 3. **Applies the agreement rule** field by field (marking plan section 5; `criteria.json` `agreement.per_kind`):
   - enum and int fields agree when the two values are equal;
   - a set agrees when the two sets are equal, with exact strings, so `A|B` is not `B|A`;

7. **Applies W10.18** from `tool_audit.json`, including its `M085_rerun` block.
8. **Runs the rival checks** in section 6. They change no tick. They say what a tick can and cannot be read as.
