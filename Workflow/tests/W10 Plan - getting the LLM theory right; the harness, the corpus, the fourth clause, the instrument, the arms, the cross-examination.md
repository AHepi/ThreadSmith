# W10 Plan - getting the LLM theory right: the harness, the corpus, the fourth clause, the instrument, the arms, the cross-examination

Frozen 22 September 2026, before any agent of it starts, on the owner's word (decision W6, quoted in full in the Decisions file; decision W5 names the three steps: the port-setting arm, the fourth clause against a trainer's record, the re-identification arm). This plan replaces the order of W3's phases 2 to 4 for this round and says what that gives up (section 9). It is not edited after the first agent starts; changes are addenda.

## 1. The question
One claim under test: model W8 (theory W9), and in particular its three risks as the owner names them: the fourth clause of a selected provenance (W8 part A6), training as selection (A5), the placement rule's first rider (C11); and its two runnable predictions the owner names first: the port-setting loss (B10, P4.4) and the re-identification counterpart of the primitive layer (A10, PA.3). The aim: every part of W8 that a run or an open record can reach is reached, the marks move where the evidence moves them, and the theory is reissued as a new numbered file with what it gives up. What would count as the claim failing is written per part in W8 and per prediction below.

## 3. The stages, in order
**Stage A, building and collecting (Opus 5, xhigh, four agents in parallel, then one reviewer).**
- A1, the harness coder: a Python rig under `rigs/W10 harness/` that runs every arm of section 4 on DeepSeek (chat completions; the beta prefix completion for the port-setting arm; five threads; resumable; the skill copy diffed against file 33 before every call, Lesson 44; the request as sent and the reply as returned both saved, PA.1) and that writes the prompt files and step prompts for the Sonnet subagent transport (one subagent per call, the workflow script carrying outputs between steps); the fixed summariser for arm (c) as a program; the partition assembler; the name-exchange edit for the re-identification arm as a program over a marked document; the marker programs: a first-marker template over the frozen criteria of A4, the blind second marker (shuffle and closed mapping as `second_marker.py` does), and the agreement-by-field program that computes run-to-run agreement per field and document, which no program in the record does.
- A2, the corpus collector: at least twelve open documents no run in the record has read, across the domains of HV file 50 (science, computability, economics, philosophy, fiction, design, rules, instructions), each with an address and a fetcher entry (texts not kept), each with the passage that answers "why", and at least six with two named speakers or agents whose names the re-identification arm can exchange; a coverage map in the skill's own words; the split frozen: eight documents for the arms, four in reserve for a repeat under a new sorting (W3 section 8).
- A3, the fourth-clause collector: what open trainers' records say. For every fully open training corpus with published composition (at least: the Pile, Dolma with OLMo, RedPajama, FineWeb, C4) and for DeepSeek's own technical reports: does the record state that the corpus contains texts stating a training loss, an optimizer, a preference rule or a list of principles used as a criterion; quote and address every claim; then the two readings of the clause (formal pairs; the occurrences that realised them) with what each makes of the evidence; then the sharpest test an open corpus allows: a named document in a published corpus that states the loss function of the model trained on it.
- A4, the instrument: the marking plan for this round, frozen before any report is read: the three fields H64 struck reworded (shape's PART boundary; "turned own test"; the same-explanation verdict), criteria for the three new fields (the eight marks per part; pairs that pull, named; rivals built), the question-identity field, the within-step and cross-step sorting as W8 part B4 fixes it, the modules-opened and request-as-sent fields, and the port-setting arm's fields; each criterion in words a stranger could apply; the rule for agreement by field.
- A5, the reviewer (after A1 to A4): reads the four returns against this plan and W8 under the skill's procedure; every fault is returned to the maker before stage B; a dry run of the harness on one document, sending nothing.

**Stage B, the instrument certified (Sonnet 5, high, five at a time).** The 96 marked reports in hand re-marked by two blind Sonnet markers under A4's criteria; agreement by field computed; W3's P3.1 to P3.5 ticked. No field carries an arm difference in stage C unless it agreed here.

**Stage C, the arms (Sonnet 5 as reader through the subagent transport now; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash through the harness when the key arrives; never summed).** On the eight documents, three repeats per box: arms (a), (b), (c), (c'), (d) with the parts split, (e) the port-setting arm (on DeepSeek by prefix completion; on Sonnet by the nearest available device, a skeleton the reader is told to fill, which W8 part B10 says is naming a list and not setting a port, and is run as the control that shows the difference), (f), (r), and (x) the re-identification arm (the document with two speakers' names exchanged, against the unexchanged control). Optional on the owner's word: (k). Every tool call audited; every request saved as sent.

**Stage D, marking (Sonnet 5, five at a time).** First marker and blind second marker over every report under A4's criteria; agreement by field; the counts of section 5 read.

**Stage E, cross-examination (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, five agents, armed with the skill, unlimited).** Five independent DeepSeek examiners, each handed the marked results of one arm family and the model's part they bear on, told to falsify the marks and the reading, and to re-mark a random half of the reports blind; disagreement with the Claude markers reported by field. Anything the examiners leave unsettled is settled by more DeepSeek runs where a run can settle it (decision W6).

**Stage F, review and reissue (Opus 5, xhigh, up to five).** The results file for this plan; every part of W8 re-marked from the evidence; the theory reissued as a new numbered file with what it gives up; the fourth clause's standing from A3 and the examiners' reading.

## 6. What counts as each outcome
As W3 section 7 and W6 section 4, with A4's criteria for the fields. A DeepSeek examiner's disagreement is a disagreement, listed by field; it settles nothing until more runs settle it. A mark moves only on quoted evidence.

## 7. What follows either way
Each prediction's failure is read as W3 section 8 and W6 section 6 read it. The fourth clause: if W10.3 holds and the examiners' reading of the clause is the physical one, A6 moves from unknown to "held if the strict reading is wrong", and the theory says what that costs; if the strict reading wins, the clause does no work and the finding is an ask for the Semantics project.

## 8. Failure of the round itself
No key arriving: stages C (DeepSeek half) and E do not run, and the results file says which predictions were unreachable. Stage B certifying no field: stage C runs on the marker-free read-outs alone. The reviewer finding the harness cannot run an arm as W8 specifies it: the arm is redesigned in an addendum before stage C and its prediction's shape is kept.

## 9. What this plan gives up
W3's order (phase 2, the reading, before phase 3, the instrument, before phase 4, the arms): the reading of the evidence in hand under the model is folded into stage F, so the coverage map of W3's phase 2 is drawn after the runs, not before, and what it would have caught early is caught late. The single-reader design for the whole of stage C: DeepSeek is added as a second reader, never summed. The five-agent cap per round: the owner sets five Opus and Sonnet at a time and five DeepSeek beside them.

## 10. Known weaknesses
The Sonnet port-setting arm cannot set a port and is a control, not the test; the test is DeepSeek's prefix completion. Every marker and examiner is a language model armed with one skill; the DeepSeek examiners are the only reading not by a Claude model. Three per box is small. The fourth clause is reached only as far as open corpus documentation reaches; the models actually run here have no open corpus.

## 11. Not tested
Anything at the grain of weights; the skill's own faults (held for HV); the theory (held for Semantics); the generation half of Q5; the attention half of Q1.

## 12. Traps
Summing readers or arms; reading a DeepSeek disagreement as a verdict; moving a mark on a count alone; reading a Sonnet skeleton as a port-setting harness; spending the 33 held-out sources; keeping a key in a file.
