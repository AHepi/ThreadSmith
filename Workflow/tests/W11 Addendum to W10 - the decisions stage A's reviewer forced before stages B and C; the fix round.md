# W11 Addendum to W10 - the decisions stage A's reviewer forced before stages B and C; the fix round

*Frozen 22 September 2026, before any fixer started. Read with plan W10. Written under W10 section 8 ("the arm is redesigned in an addendum before stage C and its prediction's shape is kept") and stage A5 ("every fault is returned to the maker before stage B"). The reviewer's return is `rigs/W10 harness/stage-A returns/A5-reviewer.json`; the faults are numbered 0 to 23 as it numbered them.*

## 1. What the reviewer returned
Twenty-four faults: six that block stage B (0 to 5), fourteen that block stage C (6 to 19), four of wording (20 to 23, of which 23 the reviewer fixed in the file). Four of them are not code faults but decisions the plan left open, which the reviewer would not take and which are taken here: faults 9, 15, 17 and 18. The rest are returned to their makers as forced fixes.

## 2. The four decisions
**D1 (fault 9), arm (d)'s assembler is handed no document.** The assembling call of the partition arm receives the frozen question, its step and the pile of answers, and no document block. This is W3 section 5's design ("the pile of answers to write the report") and W8 B12's ground ("A final call handed a pile of answers assesses no support that any call wrote"). A1's build handed it the whole document, which would let one context assess the whole candidate and would falsify P4.3 for a reason that is the rig's. Given up: an assembler that can check a quotation against the text; its report's quotations are therefore the test calls' quotations, and a marker who finds a misquotation in an arm (d) report attributes it to the call that wrote it, not to the assembler.

**D2 (fault 15), PA.1 read by halves.** W8 A1 predicts "exactly one request per step in arms (b), (c), (c') and (d) and more than one in arm (a)". First half: read as requests minus tool-loop requests equals steps, because the router is live in every arm and the record carries both numbers; `agreement.py` prints the plain count and the adjusted count so a later reader can take the other reading. Second half, arm (a): on DeepSeek arm (a) is five turns of one conversation, five requests for five steps, so the clause is falsified by the rig's own design and not by anything about the reader; on the Sonnet subagent transport a request inside one call is not observable from outside it. The arm (a) half is therefore **not reached** on either transport and is reported as such, never as held or falsified. The Sonnet run record says what its request count counts (calls) and that the tool loop inside a call was not observed.

**D3 (fault 17), the qualification of Derivation 3 is not sent to a reader under test.** W10 section 2 says every agent carries file 33 "with the qualification of Derivation 3 stated in file 11's words before any agent starts". That sentence is narrowed here to the agents that are not under test: the builders, fixers, reviewers, markers (stages B and D), examiners (stage E) and the stage F reviewers receive the qualification in their prompts, as stage A's did. The readers under test (stage C, both transports) receive file 33 byte-identical to `HV Skill/authority/33/hard-to-vary/`, whose word list carries the unqualified form, because the thing under test is the skill as it stands and a correction to its word list would change the thing under test (arm (k) and W8 A8 turn on what edits of the skill the reader saw). Recorded in the harness read-me and in every run record as a field (`derivation3_qualification_sent: false`, with this reason). Given up: the readers' reports may carry the unqualified Derivation 3; a marker who sees it marks it as file 33's, not the reader's.

**D4 (fault 18), the cross-step fields read over what the corpus affords.** A4 adds to the instrument, before stage B, a table naming for every cross-step field which of the eight arms documents afford it (from `corpus/coverage.md`), and P4.2 and P4.3 are read over the cross-step fields that at least one arms document affords; `agreement.py` flags a field that never varies across every run of every arm ("never varies") beside its "uninformative" flag, and such a field carries no arm difference. The split stays as A2 froze it: the reserve is for the repeat under a new sorting (W3 section 8) and is not raided to give a field purchase. Known weakness, recorded: "check the patches" has no purchase in the arms, and the five documents that give it purchase are in the reserve or held out; the results file says so.

## 3. The fix round
Three Opus 5 agents at effort xhigh, armed with the skill, each in a maker's role (the makers' own contexts are gone; a fixer reads the maker's return, the reviewer's faults and the files, and fixes only what a fault or a decision above forces): the harness (faults 0, 1, 2, 6 to 17, 19, 20, with D1 to D4's harness side), the instrument (3, 4, 5, 18, with D4's instrument side), the corpus (21, 22). They run in parallel, then the reviewer runs again (one Opus 5 at xhigh): every fault checked as fixed by running the thing, not by reading the fixer's word; the dry run of every arm on both transports repeated on **a corpus document** this time (fetched by A2's fetcher to a folder outside the repository, nothing kept), sending nothing; any fault remaining or newly made is returned with its severity. A remaining fault that blocks stage B or C sends the round again to the maker it names; at most three rounds. At most five subagents at a time (three fixers, then one reviewer). Writes only under `rigs/W10 harness/`; the stage-A returns folder and this addendum are not edited by any agent; fixers do not edit the reviewer's file. No key in the environment; nothing sent to any API. The workflow is saved as `.claude/workflows/llm-theory-stage-a-fixes.js` with a copy in `rigs/W10 harness/workflows/`.

## 4. Predictions for the fix round, as counts that could fail
- **W10.8:** after at most three rounds the reviewer returns zero faults that block stage B and zero that block stage C. Falsified by any such fault remaining after round three; then stage B does not start, and the round has failed to build its instrument (W10 section 8), which the owner is told.
- **W10.9:** W10.1 re-ticked on a corpus document: every arm dry-runs on both transports on one document of the frozen split, sending nothing, with every request saved as it would be sent. Falsified by any arm that cannot.
- **W10.10:** of the twenty-four faults, at most three are declined by a fixer with a reason the reviewer accepts; more, and the fixers read the faults against the makers rather than fixing, and the round is re-run with the faults reworded.

## 5. Not tested
Nothing is run against a reader. The decisions above are the plan's, not the theory's; D2's "not reached" is a limit of two transports, not a finding about readers. Whether the instrument's fields agree between markers is stage B's question.

## 6. Traps
Treating the fixer's word that a fault is fixed as the fix; letting a fixer improve what no fault names; sending the qualification of Derivation 3 to a reader under test in any arm; raiding the reserve; a key in the environment during a round that should send nothing.
