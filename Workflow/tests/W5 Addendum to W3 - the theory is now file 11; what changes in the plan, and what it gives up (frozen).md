## 2. The one claim change, and what it does to M2

File 11's Derivation 3 now claims: "Let \(t\) be selected from a population \(\mathcal T\) on a finite history \(H\subsetneq C\). For every \((a,b)\in C\setminus H\) at which some \(t'\in\mathcal T\), also surviving on \(H\), has a different value from \(t\), the value of \(t\) at \((a,b)\) is underdetermined by \(H\): survival on \(H\) does not distinguish \(t\) from \(t'\) there. The presence of an unseen pair alone does not establish that such a \(t'\) exists; it must be admitted, realizable, a member of \(\mathcal T\), and a survivor of \(H\)." Its consequence: "A correspondence produced by selection is faithful where it was tested and, wherever its population admits an alternative, unconstrained where it was not." And what the theory itself gives up: "What the qualification gives up is the guarantee of a differing survivor at every unseen pair, and with it the blanket claim that every untested value is unconstrained; a population restriction, a physical relation, or another stated constraint may already fix a value that the history never tested." Part IV adds to the definition of a selected transport: "The population \(\mathcal T\) is part of the claim: what \(H\) leaves open about \(t\) is what \(\mathcal T\) leaves open (Derivation 3)." And Part XII: "The population is the set of transports the physics and the stated construction admit; a transport that would need a part every member of the population is built without is not in it."

This is the qualification the W2 file-10 judge said M2 needed ("a parametric family with tied weights is exactly the shape file 10 names as what would refute Derivation 3"), and the Semantics record reached it from its own cases (O48, logs S70 to S75) and noted the convergence with that judge's finding (log S73). **M2 is restated:**

**M2 (under file 11). The organization the weights instantiate admits a selected transport, and its population is the set of transports the physics and the architecture admit.** Part IV's definition, now with the population as part of the claim. Training fits the first three clauses as W3 says (the parameter settings reached; the update; the loss condition); the fourth clause stays *unknown* with its check named. \(H\) and \(C\) are as W3 says, and file 11's Part 0 now calls the history one "of encountered changes" in place of file 10's "of predictions", which is the reading W3's M2 argued for; so M2 no longer argues against Part 0 on the shape of \(H\), and its paragraph on blindness is cut, since the word is gone from file 11 ("produced by variation and survival on a history of encountered changes"). The population \(\mathcal T\) is the set of transports the architecture can instantiate under the physics; a behaviour no member of that set carries is not in it. *What follows* is the qualified Derivation 3: at an unseen pair, the reader's value is unconstrained by its history where the population admits a differing survivor there, and fixed by the population where it does not ("Where \(\mathcal T\) contains no such transport, \(H\) is silent on the value at \((a,b)\) and the population fixes it."). So the model no longer says a reader is unconstrained off its history; it says the history settles nothing there, and the population may. What this adds to W3's reading of phase 4 is one thing, and only on the agreement side, which M3 already states: agreement across repeats of one arm no longer shows that the history settled the value, because the population may have fixed it. A difference between arms is read as M3 reads it (different organizations at this grain) and by nothing in Derivation 3, which is about one transport and its population and says nothing about two arrangements. Attack point (D) is now: "a selected transport whose value at an unseen change is determined by its history although its population admits a differing survivor there (against Derivation 3; a population with no such survivor is the theorem's own qualification, not a refutation)". *Mark:* held if (the fourth clause; the independence premise, which file 11 keeps as the proof's first sentence), provenance built. *Would count against it:* a member of a training history shown to represent the survival condition; a showing that the component relations of a reader's transport family are not supplied independently for each pair, so that survival on \(H\) does constrain a value off \(H\) by way of the history and not of the population, which is the independence limb W2 forced and W4 had dropped; or a showing that the population fixes a value at a pair phase 4 reads as the history's.

What this gives up: W3's ground for saying nothing about a reader off its history was the unqualified consequence; that sentence is gone from the theory and from the model. Q2's and Q4's answers rest on retention (M5) and on the carrier (M7), not on Derivation 3; W3's section 9 said otherwise in its parenthesis and is changed as section 1 records.

**M6's clause changes with it.** W3's M6 says a target that comes from the weights "is fitted: held only on the changes it has met", which is the blanket claim file 11 gives up. It reads now: "is fitted: settled by its history only where it was met, and by the population where the population fixes it". P4.5's third count (a target with no carrier at all) falsifies M6 as restated; its count is unchanged.
